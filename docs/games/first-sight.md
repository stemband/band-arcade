# First Sight (`first-sight/`)

- WHAT IT TEACHES: SIGHT-READING for the GMEA District Honor Band audition (Middle School, first round). Each etude is ORIGINAL music the game writes itself (never the GMEA's own etudes, which are copyrighted), as many as the student wants, in the student's own clef and written key: study it, play it with a count-in and the click, then hear how it goes.
- Zones `note-reading` and `audition-prep`; NOT `noInstrument` (the student plays their own instrument; nothing listens), `unpitched: true` (the Snare Drum reads the same etudes' RHYTHMS on a one-line staff), no `fit`. `maxStars` 0: a practice tool (no stars, tokens or leaderboard). The floor's line instead of a hi-score: `summary` = "Etudes read: N" (`gameData('first-sight').read`).
- NO MICROPHONE AT ALL (the pitched-audio exception, [CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md) hard rules and [sound.md](../engine/sound.md)): the page doesn't load `pitch.js` or `mic-gate.js`; no `requireMic`. ▶ HEAR IT's notes come only from `shared/winds.js` (`Winds.phrase`) through `Sfx.output()` (SOUND OFF and the EFFECTS slider apply) and refuse while `Pitch.listening()`; the click and the snare are `music-highway/backing.js`'s kit (unpitched).
- CALIBRATED TO THE 2026 DISTRICT SIGHT-READING (Mr. Graham's copy, not in the repository): the wind etude was 6 measures of 4/4, Moderato ♩ = 92, in concert A♭, with quarters, eighths, a dotted quarter, a dotted eighth–sixteenth, eighth–two sixteenths, an eighth-note triplet, an eighth rest then an off-beat eighth, chromatic accidentals, slurs, staccato, tenuto, p / mf / f and hairpins; every wind part the same melody transposed (low parts with octave changes). LEVEL 5 is that level. Mallets (8 bars, ♩ = 110, rolls) and snare (10 bars, ♩ = 120, flams, drags, rolls) were harder: not modeled yet.

## The level select (`#hub`)

- THE LEVEL SELECT PATTERN with a picker: `LevelSelect.show({…, picker: #keyPick, pickHead: 'Select your key', setLabel, need: 'Select your key and level'})` (the options added to shared/level-select.js for this game; [press-start-and-level-select.md](../engine/press-start-and-level-select.md)). KEY: Concert F · B♭ · E♭ · A♭ (each with "your G" etc. for the member, from `Scales.build`) or MIX (a random key for each new etude: the audition's key is a surprise). The snare has no key row ("Select your length"; its etudes use concert B♭'s seed). LENGTH: 8 bars (practice) or 6 bars (the audition's length). Both remembered (`gameData.key`, `.bars`). The mode picker's look (`.mp-notes` / `.mp-ord`), not `ModePicker` (no note sets here).
- THE SIX LEVEL CARDS (`.fs-card`: name, what it adds, its meters, "N read"). No locks: every level is open.
- "HAVE AN ETUDE CODE?": type a code (`5E8-K7QX`, any case, the dash optional) → that exact etude. A bad code: "That code doesn't look right…". `?code=5E8-K7QX` in the address opens it directly (COPY A LINK makes one: Mr. Graham can post the same etude for a whole class in Brightspace, or project it).

## One etude (`#play`)

- THE HEAD: "Level 5 · District Ready · 8 bars", "Concert E♭ · your F Major" (snare: "Snare drum · 4/4"), the CODE (a tap copies it).
- THE MUSIC: `shared/notation.js` (`Arcade.Notation.engrave`, moved from Music Highway) on the light music screen (`.stage`): the member's clef and written key signature on every row, the time signature on the first, a final bar line, the tempo marking above ("Moderato ♩ = 92", the note drawn as a small SVG: fonts draw ♩ too small or its dot apart). ROWS: 4 bars a row (6 bars: 3) while that keeps the music at least `RULES.minScale` (.8) of its natural size, else 2 (busy level 5 bars on an iPad, every phone); every row engraved to the same width, the widest row's or the staff area's when wider (the spacing stretches, the notes never grow past natural size); redrawn when the width changes (not during a run). A tie across a row break = a half tie at each edge (`tieOut` / `tieIn`); a tie inside a row = one longer event (the engine writes the tie). Beams by the beat (`groupBeats: 1`, so sixteenths read clearly in 4/4). COUNTING On = the shared counting's starting syllable under every note and rest (rests in parentheses), from `Arcade.Counting` reading the etude's own rhythm text.
- STUDY: a countdown (`Study time` Off · 30 s · 60 s, default 30; remembered) with a shrinking bar, "seconds to study it. Find the key, the time signature, the tricky rhythms and the dynamics." and I'M READY. It stops while the pause menu is open (it reads `pause.paused` itself, never a copy: "Back to levels" closes the menu without an onResume, and a copied flag once froze every later countdown) and while the page is hidden. THE GMEA'S REAL STUDY TIME WAS NOT CHECKED: Mr. Graham sets it to match.
- ▶ PLAY IT: a COUNT-IN (1 bar; 2 bars of 2/4 and 6/8: beats under 3 a bar), then the CLICK on every beat (Click while you play On/Off; the count-in always clicks; the first beat of a bar louder) and a soft MARKER moving through the music (the row's time → x map), "Bar 3 of 8". Everything is scheduled at once on the audio clock (`AudioClock`); the marker follows the AUDIBLE time. Sound off: the marker still runs on `performance.now()`. The end of a play-through: "Nice work! Now tap ▶ Hear it…", `star-earned`, `read` + `byLevel[level]` + `recent` (the last 10 codes) once per etude, and `store.noteFinished` (Today's Practice). ■ STOP ends it ("Stopped…").
- ▶ HEAR IT: LOCKED until the student has played the etude through once (a tap says "Play it yourself first, then hear it to check."): read it first, then check. Then the count-in (and the click when it's on), then every note in the member's own voice (`Winds.voiceFor`: flute; clarinet / bass clarinet; sax (also oboe and bassoon); brass (trumpet, trombone); horn; lowbrass; bells = `mallet`), at its written length × `RULES.length` (staccato .45, tenuto 1, slurred 1, else .9), tied notes as one, the DYNAMICS as loudness (`RULES.gain`: p .42, mp .58, mf .75, f 1; a hairpin glides between its two dynamics; an accent × 1.3), each note lighting (`.halo`) as it sounds. The snare: the kit's snare (accents louder).
- TEMPO − / +: `RULES.tempoStep` 4 BPM, from 40 to the marking + 16; remembered PER LEVEL (`gameData.tempo[level]`; the marking itself = no entry). NEW ETUDE: a new code at the same level, key setting (MIX picks again) and length. PRINT IT: `window.print()` with a print stylesheet = only the music, black on white, with "First Sight · Trumpet · Level 5 · Code 5E8-K7QX" above it. COPY A LINK: the page's address with `?code=`.
- KEYS: Space = ▶ PLAY IT / ■ STOP, H = ▶ HEAR IT, N = NEW ETUDE, Enter = I'M READY. The pause menu (the kit's): stops a run, pauses the study countdown; BACK TO LEVELS never asks.
- MENU MUSIC `first-sight-menu` on the level select only (off on an etude's page). Menu background: the coded `records` scene (Mr. Graham's picture in `shared/backgrounds/first-sight.*` replaces it). Cabinet: the classic cabinet in yellow / cyan with the default sparkle marquee (no art of its own yet).

## The generator (`etudes.js`, `Arcade.FSEtudes`)

- A CODE IS AN ETUDE: `<level><key letter F|B|E|A><bars 6|8>-<4 letters>` (`SEED_ABC` has no 0, O, 1 or I). Everything comes from a seeded random (`rng`), never `Math.random`, so the same code is the same music on every device. IT IS WRITTEN IN CONCERT PITCH AS SCALE DEGREES, so EVERY INSTRUMENT GETS THE SAME MUSIC (the same concert pitch classes; only octaves can differ): a class reading one code together is in unison. Saved codes depend on the generator: CHANGING THE GENERATOR'S RULES OR ORDER OF RANDOM DRAWS CHANGES WHAT AN OLD CODE PLAYS (harmless for practice; `recent` only lists codes).
- THE LEVELS (`LEVELS`, one commented table Mr. Graham tunes: meters, tempo, span, steps, cells, ties, slur, stac, ten, acc, chrom, trips, dyn):

| Level | Name | Adds | Meters | ♩ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Building Blocks | whole, half, quarter, quarter rest; mostly steps; one dynamic (mf / f) | 4/4 | 72 |
| 2 | Eighth Notes | eighth pairs, dotted half, half rest; skips; p and f; bar 1's rhythm answers in bar 5 | 4/4, 3/4 | 76 |
| 3 | Dots & Ties | dotted quarter–eighth, eighth rest + eighth, ties over bar lines; slurs, staccato, mf, hairpins | 4/4, 3/4, 2/4 | 80 |
| 4 | Off the Beat | eighth–quarter–eighth syncopation; chromatic notes (2); accents, tenuto; leaps to a 5th | 4/4, 3/4, 2/4 | 84 |
| 5 | District Ready | four sixteenths, eighth–two sixteenths, two sixteenths–eighth, dotted eighth–sixteenth, eighth-note triplets (2); mp; chromatic (3) | 4/4 (most), 3/4, 2/4 | 92 |
| 6 | Six-Eight | dotted quarter, quarter–eighth, three eighths, eighth–quarter, dotted half, dotted-quarter rest, eighth rest–two eighths, dotted eighth–sixteenth–eighth, two sixteenths–two eighths | 6/8 | ♩. 60 |

- THE FORM: a PERIOD. Bar `bars/2` (4 of 8, 3 of 6) is the HALF CADENCE (`CADENCE.half`: a long last note on the 5th, or the 2nd), bar `bars/2 + 1` repeats bar 1's rhythm (level 2 up), bar 3 of 8 sometimes does too, the last bar (`CADENCE.end`) ends on a long TONIC (the octave sometimes). Never a tie into or out of a cadence bar. `LIMITS`: at most 2 sixteenth cells and 1 rest cell a bar, 2 ties an etude, 3 repeated notes in a row; never a rest first.
- THE MELODY: steps and the level's leaps (`steps`), a leap of a 4th or more followed by a step back, no tritone between the 4th and 7th degrees, inside the level's `span` of degrees, pulled toward each phrase's goal as it nears its end (the note before the goal a step from it). CHROMATIC NOTES (`chrom`, level 4 up): a RAISED degree as a passing tone up (B♭ B♮ C) or a lower neighbor (C B♮ C), only below a WHOLE step (degrees 1, 2, 4, 5, 6 of the scale raised), so no written key from 2 sharps to 4 flats ever needs a double sharp or flat.
- THE MARKINGS: dynamics at the start, the second phrase (level 2 up) and one more bar (level 3 up), each a change; a hairpin in the bar before a change (level 3 up), sometimes one into the last note; slurs over a cell's notes (sometimes two cells), staccato over a cell's short notes, accents on long downbeat notes, tenuto on long notes. The snare keeps only accents and dynamics.
- FOR AN INSTRUMENT (`realize(etude, member)`): each degree in the member's WRITTEN key from `Scales.build(member, key)` (START's tonic, spelled in the written key); the whole etude moves an octave (`shift` −1, 0, +1) to put the fewest notes outside the member's GMEA range (`MEMBERS` chromatic), then to sit nearest the middle of the member's GMEA range (its median note; the scale sheet's octave wins near ties): the tuba's F etude reads around the staff, not under it, and the flute stays up where it sings. A bar still outside the range moves an octave AS A WHOLE when that fits (a leap at a bar line, like a real low part), else only its outside notes do. A tie's second note always keeps the first note's pitch.
