# THE RUDIMENT TRAINER (`rudiment-trainer/`)

A PRACTICE TOOL for snare drum and bells players: the 40 PAS International Drum Rudiments, each written out with its sticking, heard at five tempos (Bronze → Diamond) and OPEN–CLOSE–OPEN, looping with an optional click, to play along with on a drum or practice pad and check off each tempo the student can play. **No stars, no microphone, no judging.** Its playing time counts toward TODAY'S PRACTICE. Files: `index.html`, `style.css`, `game.js` (the screens), `rudiments.js` (the data, Mr. Graham's to edit). It plays through the shared rudiment player ([rudiment-player.md](../engine/rudiment-player.md)) and draws with the shared rhythm staff (RUDIMENT NOTATION in [rhythm.md](../engine/rhythm.md)).

## games.js

Snare and bells play it the same way: rudiments are hand patterns.
- `summary`: the floor's line instead of a hi-score, "Diamond rudiments: 3 of 40".
- The look: `cabinet: {shape: 'drumline', trim: 'yellow', trim2: 'white', marquee: 'drumline', screen: 'drumline'}` (shared/cabinets.js: a marching-snare top with gold hoops and silver rods, crossed sticks, a practice pad on the kick plate; the screen = a practice pad, two sticks taking turns and R L R R L R L L lighting as each stroke lands, ~4 a second, a soft glow each), `marquee: {scene: 'drumline'}` (shared/marquees.js: a marching snare at each end, sticks taking turns, ~1.4 strokes a second per drum, a soft gold glow, never a flash; the title only, white in a gold frame), `bg: {scene: 'drumline'}` (shared/bg-scenes.js: a practice room at night, acoustic panels, a marching snare on a stand at the left edge, a practice pad with crossed sticks at the right, a slow 3 s glint; the middle dark), `cabinet3d: {profile: 'drumline'}` (arcade3d.js: a classic body with the `snare` topper: an upright white shell, gold hoops, silver rods, two crossed sticks). Colors: `--rt-*` and `--tier-*` in theme.css (the arcade's one dark theme).
- SOUNDS (screen `rudiments`, the Sound Board's "Rudiment player (the Rudiment Trainer)" section, with the player's five recordings: [rudiment-player.md](../engine/rudiment-player.md)):
  - THE MENU MUSIC `rudiment-trainer-menu` (games.js `menuMusic`; a 30–90 s seamless loop, `vol` .45, `mic: false`; until Mr. Graham uploads it: the arcade's `select-music`; listed in the Sound Board's Music section and shared/sounds/README.md's "Game menu music" table). `Sfx.gameMenuMusic(GAME_ID)` wants it on the picker and on a rudiment's page while nothing plays. ▶ PLAY fades it out (0.5 s) and, when it was really playing, WAITS for the fade (`RULES.musicFadeMs` 500) before the player starts, so the count-off's first click and the snare never sound over it. It is wanted again whenever a run ends: ■ STOP, an Open–Close–Open run ending by itself, the kit's PAUSE (Esc/P) and a hidden tab (the music manager keeps it paused while hidden). - The leaderboard: nothing new. Like every game, `LevelSelect.played(i)` (opening a rudiment) sends the once-a-day `play` event; never stars or Endless. The PRIVACY RULE is unchanged.

## The data (`rudiments.js`, `Arcade.Rudiments`)

- `FAMILIES` (the tabs, in this order): `roll` Roll Rudiments, `diddle` Diddle Rudiments, `flam` Flam Rudiments, `drag` Drag Rudiments.
- `TIERS` = the five tempo levels `bronze`, `silver`, `gold`, `platinum`, `diamond`, each with its color token. **Their ids, names and order are SAVED** (check-offs): never rename or reorder; add at the end.
- `TEMPO_SETS` (quarter notes a minute, Bronze → Diamond; 6/8 counts DOTTED QUARTERS, the player's default beat): `A` 60/80/100/120/140 (singles, buzz, paradiddles), `B` 50/65/80/95/110 (diddles = 32nds), `C` 50/70/90/110/130 (flams and drags). A rudiment's own `bpm: [5 numbers]` wins over its set. Mr. Graham tunes these by ear.
- `OCO` = OPEN–CLOSE–OPEN, each rudiment from its own Bronze up to its Diamond and back: `upS` 40, `holdS` 10, `downS` 40. Its check-off id is `oco` (saved).
- `PRACTICE_S` 120 = the seconds of playing along today that count as practice (the same number as practice.js `PRACTICE.rudimentS`: a test checks they agree).
- `LIST` = THE 40, in PAS order: `{n, id, name, fam, time, tiers, text, bpm?}`. **The `id`s are SAVED** (check-offs, `?r=` links): never rename them. The `text` (the rhythm text format with hands, accents, graces, diddles, buzzes) and the `time` may be corrected at any time; the tests check that all 40 still read with no errors. The `MAT: CONFIRM` comments mark the ones Mr. Graham is still checking (#2's accent, #4's buzz value, #6's diddle convention, #12 and #14's release, #22's accents, #26's 3/4, #30's "drag", #35, #36 and #37): keep them until he has.
- `CREDIT`: "The 40 PAS International Drum Rudiments · Percussive Arts Society" (the picker's small credit line). Never a company's name, recordings or notation images.

## The screens

- PRESS START (shared), then THE PICKER (`#hub`): the title, the how-to line, the credit, the FAMILY TABS (All · Roll · Diddle · Flam · Drag, `role="tablist"`, ←/→/Home/End; each "3 / 15 Diamond") and the 40 rudiments as cards in PAS order: the number, the name, a small staff with its sticking (`RhythmStaff.engrave`, sticking only, the current roll view), five tier pips (lit in the tier's color when checked) + the Open–Close–Open mark, a ◆ DIAMOND badge when all five tempos are checked. THE LEVEL-SELECT PATTERN fits: `LevelSelect.show` with all 40 cards (a tab only hides cards; the pattern's keyboard skips hidden ones), the new `heading: 'Select your rudiment'` + `need` line and `voice: false` (the voice line says "level"); select a card, then START opens it (no CLAUDE.md exception needed). REMEMBERED (`gameData('rudiment-trainer')`): `tab`, `last` (and the level-select memory selects the last one opened). The lobby's practice flag (`bandarcade.practice-pick`) is removed so it never pre-selects card 1 (there are no stars to look at). `?r=<id>` opens that rudiment's page directly (TODAY'S PRACTICE; an unknown id = the picker); links use `Arcade.link`, so `?demo` carries through.
- A RUDIMENT (`#page`): ← RUDIMENTS, the number, name and family; the staff LARGE with its sticking (`counting` off by default; COUNTING on shows it too), one row, or a row per measure when one row would be more than 1.25 × the stage's width (phones), each scaled to fit; ROLLS: SLASHES ⇄ WRITTEN OUT (shown only for a rudiment with a `/`; default slashes; remembered for the device, `view`; the thumbnails follow it); CLICK off/on (remembered, `click`); THE TIER ROW: five big buttons "Gold · 90 BPM" (the tier's color when selected, a ✓ when checked) + OPEN–CLOSE–OPEN "50 → 130 → 50" (the last tier per rudiment remembered, `tierOf`); ▶ PLAY / ■ STOP (`RudimentPlayer.create`: a count-off, then the rudiment loops until STOP; Open–Close–Open ends by itself); the strokes light as they sound (`RudimentPlayer.light`, the `--rp-now` token); the tempo readout ("90 BPM", Open–Close–Open live). A new tier while playing lands at the NEXT repetition (the player's `setBpm`; to or from Open–Close–Open restarts the run). The kit's PAUSE (Esc/P, a hidden tab) stops the player and the page shows ▶ again; BACK TO RUDIMENTS = the picker.
- THE CHECK-OFF: "✓ I can play this at Gold" appears for the SELECTED tier only after `RULES.checkReps` (4) FULL repetitions at it in this visit (a repetition counts when the next one's first beat sounds, at the tempo it was planned with, so a tempo change never counts a mixed one); Open–Close–Open needs one whole run (it ended by itself, not stopped). Until then: "Play along 4 times at Gold to check it off (2 of 4)." Tapping it saves `gameData('rudiment-trainer').done[<rudiment id>][<tier id>] = 'YYYY-MM-DD'` and plays the quiet `star-earned` event (never while `Pitch.listening()`), and counts for TODAY'S PRACTICE (`store.noteFinished`). A checked tier shows "✓ You can play this at Gold! Checked 2026-10-04" + UNCHECK (undo a mistake). Check-offs are NOT stars: never `games` progress, `maxStars`, tokens or the leaderboard; they ride along in the Arcade Backup Code like all gameData.
- `RULES` at the top of game.js (Mr. Graham edits): `checkReps` 4, `saveEveryS` 5, `keepDays` 30.

## TODAY'S PRACTICE

- THE PRACTICE-TIME LOG: `gameData('rudiment-trainer').playS['YYYY-MM-DD']` = the seconds the player REALLY played that day: counted on the player's own audio clock only while it runs with the audio running (`state().audio`) and the page visible (a jump over 1 s never counts), saved every `saveEveryS` and at every stop, the last 30 days kept (`store.dayKey()`, so `?demo&today=` works). The same pattern as the metronome's `metroS`.
- `store.noteFinished('rudiment-trainer')` once a day, the first time `playS[day]` reaches `PRACTICE_S` or a tier is checked off (it also counts like any finished round for the lobby's CONTINUE).
- shared/practice.js ([page-flow.md](../engine/page-flow.md)): the SNARE's warm-up is THE RUDIMENT OF THE DAY (`PRACTICE.warmUps`, PAS numbers taking turns day by day from `rotationStart`; opened with `?r=<id>`; done at `rudimentS` seconds of playing along today OR the old metronome check); `'rudiment-trainer'` is FIRST in `skills.technique.games` (only snare and bells suit it: winds and brass are unchanged; a snare's step 2 moves on, since its warm-up already is the Rudiment Trainer).
