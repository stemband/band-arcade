# Music Highway (`music-highway/`)

- Play-along rhythm game (Technique Lab): neon LIGHT PADS race down a perspective synthwave highway (one canvas: `proj(dt)`: depth d = (1 + `roadDepth`)^(dt ÷ lead), so a pad's size and its distance to the next shrink together) to their lane's GATE on the strike line in time with a backing groove; a scrolling staff underneath on its own dark panel (written pitch, the student's clef + key signature pinned on the left, letter names, a playhead; hit notes gold, missed coral). LISTENS (pitch.js + mic-gate.js). `unpitched: true` (the snare plays each song's rhythm in two STICKING lanes, L left / R right; judging stays timing-only: any clean attack), `byMember: true`: `setLevel('music-highway', member id, song number, {stars, best: score})`; `maxStars` 141 (47 songs × 3). SPEED: SLOW 75 % (no stars) | NORMAL | TURBO 125 % (`turboRate`; stars like Normal, and a star on Turbo earns that song's ⚡ TURBO badge on the song list: `gameData.turbo[member][song id]`); judging windows stay the same in ms at every speed. The best stars and score are kept (`setLevel` gets the max). PRACTICE THIS PART (the trouble-spot loop) gives no stars. MODE: PLAY | PRACTICE (`gameData.mode`): PRACTICE never calls `requireMic` (no prompt, no pitch detection, nothing judged, scored or saved); the full pitched band plays out loud (the headphones voices at `practiceMelodyVol`/`practiceBassVol`/`practicePadVol`; MELODY On/Off + a MELODY VOLUME slider, `gameData.melody`/`melVol`; the snare hears the song's concert melody + chords and the full drums), each note's gate lights as it sounds (`res: 'guide'`), the HUD says "PRACTICE: not scored", and the results say "Practice complete!" with PLAY THIS SONG / PRACTICE AGAIN; pause, restart, song menu and every speed work. Unlocks: tier 2 = stars on any 3 tier-1 songs, tier 3 = stars on any 3 tier-2 songs, tier 4 (THE FINAL BOSS) = stars on any 3 tier-3 songs (per member; `?demo` all).

- `songs.js` = THE SONG LIST (Mat edits; songs are levels: NEVER reorder or remove, add at the end; 47 songs, 27–46 = Mr. Graham's transcriptions from trumpet parts, in method-book order, 47 = THE FINAL BOSS): `{id, title, source, tier, tempo, timeSig, key: 'Bb' (concert major), mode?: 'minor', style: rock|march|swing|waltz, chords?, notes: [{deg, oct, beats, acc?} | {rest}]}`. WRITTEN (trumpet) KEY → CONCERT: C major = 'Bb', F major = 'Eb', G major = 'F', B♭ major = 'Ab', D minor = 'Eb' + minor, A minor = 'Bb' + minor, E minor = 'F' + minor; repeats, endings and D.C. al Fine are written out. METERS (`timeSig`): [4, 4], [3, 4], [2, 4], [2, 2], [6, 8], [3, 8]; beats are always QUARTER notes (`tempo` = quarters a minute; 6/8 = 3 beats a measure, 3/8 = 1.5) and everything follows the PRIMARY beat = `SongMap.meter(song)` `{per, pulse (1; 2/2: 2; 6/8 and 3/8: 1.5), pulses, countBars, countClicks, countBeats, label}`: the COUNT-IN (a quarter-beat meter: one measure; 2/2 two bars of half-note clicks, 6/8 two bars of dotted-quarter clicks, 3/8 four bars of one: always 4 clicks, each bar's downbeat the strong click; RESUME counts in the same way), the GROOVES (backing.js `groove(style, timeSig)`: 2/2 = a march in 2: kick 1, snare 3, hats on the quarters; 6/8 = kick 1, snare 4 (beat 1.5), hats on every eighth, 1 and 4 stronger; 3/8 = kick 1, soft hats on the three eighths, no snare; 3/4 always the waltz), the highway's cross lines (one per primary beat, downbeats magenta: `drawRoad({pulse})`), the chords' weights and the snare's DOWNBEATS RIGHT (R on a primary beat); the song select and the Song Board print the meter ('¢ 2/2'). The notes are typed as NOTE TEXT through `N()` (bar lines checked; A `|` AFTER EVERY MEASURE (each string also ends one): `check` reports any stretch longer than one measure with no bar line ("<id>: measure N has no bar line after it (add | so each measure is checked)"), because a wrong rhythm can hide inside it (The Entertainer's missing sixteenth rest on beat 2 of measures 3/7/11 did, fixed: 13 measures, tests/music-highway-entertainer.spec.js, which also checks every other song's events against tests/fixtures/mh-song-events.json); `5:2~` = TIED into the next, same note: ONE held note in the game, one pad and trail, each side counted in its own measure, drawn as a tie; `(3 4 5)` = a SLUR (may cross bar lines, may hold a tie; an unclosed `(`, a stray `)`, a slur in a slur, a rest inside or a one-note slur are reported by `check`); `key: 'Eb'` = written F major for trumpet). Format + future MIDI importer: `music-highway/README.md`. `song-map.js` (`Arcade.SongMap`): degrees → concert (B♭3 = degree 1) → each member's WRITTEN notes (`sounds`), spelled in its written key, octave: tier 1 = degree 1 on the group's first-five tonic, else the best fit in the GMEA range, and THE RANGE RULE: a tier 2–3 note that octave still leaves outside the member's GMEA range moves by an octave into it (`folded`), so every instrument plays every note (the oboe's D6 in The Merry Minstrels / March of the Toreadors, the tenor sax's B3, the F horn's E3 and G5); a group whose first five start elsewhere (the C–G horn) gets the song moved to its first five (concert F); `check(song)` reports measures that don't add up (console + Song Board); chords per measure (given, else I/IV/V from the melody).
- LANES = PITCHES (`SongMap.lanes(song, map, group)`): one lane per written pitch, lowest on the left, so the melody's shape shows on the road; tier 1 = exactly the group's first five (all five even if a song skips one), tier 2 ≤ 8, tier 3 ≤ 12 (`MAX_LANES`: past that the least-used pitch joins its nearest neighbor's lane, order kept; a lane of two pitches is labeled "E/F"); SNARE = two sticking lanes, L (left hand) and R (right hand): each pad in the lane of its hand (`lanes.of(n)` for every instrument; `?demo` logs a sticking self-check of the first two measures, `Arcade.Highway.stickCheck()`). STICKING (song-map.js `stickings`): the song's own `sticking` field ('RLRL RRLL…', one letter per note; `check` reports a wrong count) else the student's pattern on the song select (`gameData.sticking`, snare only, with "Follow the sticking! The lanes show which hand to use."): ALTERNATE (default: hand to hand, every measure starts with R, rests don't count) | DOWNBEATS RIGHT (on a beat R, off the beat L); a long note takes the hand its rule gives. The snare's staff is the one-line percussion staff with R/L under the notes. Each lane has a neon GATE with the lane's written letter name under it. PADS: rounded neon tail lights in the pitch's color (`--mh-c`…`--mh-b`), dark outline, the letter inside unless LETTER NAMES: OFF (the song select, `gameData.names`; the gates keep their names; stars count either way). FULL-VALUE TRAILS: every note of `trailFrom` (1: a quarter) beats or more (not the snare) trails a light for its length, ending `trailGap` (12 %) of it early (`n.tEnd`) so notes stay apart; it burns bright while held (`n.holding`). Held to `fullValueShare` (85 %; a note still sounding at its trail's end counts as held to there) = FULL VALUE (a quiet pop-up `#fv`; +`fullValuePoints` for quarter and dotted-quarter notes; long notes (≥ `holdFrom`) keep their per-beat hold bonus); the results show "Full value held" (%). Eighths and shorter: no trail. No fingering diagrams any more (fingerings.js + diagrams.js are still loaded only because song-map.js's octave fit prefers notes that have a fingering, so every song keeps its notes).
- THE BACKDROP (bg-scenes.js `S.sunset.paint`, shared with the menu scene `highway`): purple-to-pink sky, a few stars, a big striped sun on the horizon, wireframe mountains, the ground grid to the vanishing point; drawn ONCE into an offscreen canvas (`V.bg`, with the road, lanes and unlit gates); ONLY THE ROAD MOVES: the sky, stars, sun, mountains and the ground beside the road never change during a song (no sun swell); each frame draws only, ON the road, one cross line per beat (crossing the gates ON the beat; downbeats magenta) and the lane dividers' dashes rolling with them, plus trails, pads and gates; reduced motion = the road stands still too. The menu scene keeps its own gentle motion. PERFORMANCE: `perfWatch` averages draw time and frame gaps over 180 frames; over `fxSlowDrawMs` / `fxSlowGapMs` = effects 'lo' for good on this device (`gameData.fx`: 15 stars, no mountains, no halos, pixel ratio 1). Measured 54–58 fps at 4× CPU throttle (stays 'hi').

- THE STAFF IS REAL NOTATION (`music-highway/notation.js`, `Arcade.MHNotation.engrave`; also the trouble spot and the Song Board): real note values (whole … sixteenth, dotted), rests (not dotted except 6/8; a whole measure = a whole rest), ties across bar lines and awkward beats, beams by group (4/4 and 2/2 half a measure, 2/4 and 3/4 a beat, 6/8 three eighths, 3/8 the whole measure), 2/2 drawn ¢, dotted rests only in 6/8 / 3/8 (a dotted-quarter rest on a beat), a note across the middle of a 6/8 bar tied there like the book, stems up below the middle line / down on or above it (a beam follows its group's majority), accidentals that last to the bar line (♮ when needed), bar lines, a time signature, the written key signature. SPACING like printed music (settings.js `staff`): a fixed pad after/before every bar line, space = noteBase × (1 + noteGrow × log2(length ÷ the song's shortest)), never under noteMin, + room for accidentals and dots; never positioned by time. The staff scrolls by the engraving's TIME → X MAP (a monotone curve through every note/rest onset, `xAt(beats)`), so each notehead reaches the playhead exactly when its pad reaches its gate (measured ≤ 1 ms over whole songs); its speed changes a little, the pads' doesn't. Groups carry `data-x`; `.halo` = the lit-note glow.
- PAD SPACING (settings.js): two pads in a row are at least `cardGap` (40 %) of a pad apart at the gates, even for the song's quickest note step, and the road's perspective keeps that true all the way up (`spacing()` in game.js picks the lead time; too fast to read (< `readMinS`) = smaller pads for that song, never under `cardMinScale`; pads NEVER overlap). NOTE SPACING Normal | Wide (`wideMul` 1.5; `gameData.wide`, stars count in both). `Arcade.Highway.spacing()` / `cardRects(t)` (pad boxes) / `lanes()` / `trace()` + `traceLog()` (what each frame drew) / `fx()` for tests.
- THE GATE GLOW (visual only): PERFECT / GOOD / OK lights that lane's GATE (canvas, `GL.lanes`) in the pad's color, plus the staff note (`.lit`); the judgment pops up above the gate (`#judge`). A long note stays lit while held (`n.holding`, the hold-bonus check; its trail burns bright too) and fades when let go; a short one `glowLingerMs` past its end, so runs stay lit. Fades only (in 60 ms, out 200 ms), a gate goes out at most once per `glowMinCycleMs` (≤ 3 flashes a second); reduced motion = on/off with no fades. Miss / wrong note = a dim red gate outline (`badMs`). `Arcade.Highway.glow()` for tests.
- PAUSE = THE UI KIT's pause (`A.UI.pause`, `pauseOnBlur`: Esc / P, a hidden tab, `pagehide`, or the window unfocused 0.6 s; the button top-left above `#play`, whose HUD `layout()` moves past it; kit panels are z-index 60+ above `#play` (40)) with the score and accuracy so far: RESUME / RESTART / SETTINGS (NOTE SPACING and LETTER NAMES are registered there: letter names at once, a new spacing when the song resumes) / SONG MENU (`levelsLabel`; asks first only when a score would be lost). Pausing stops the scheduler + `kit.stopAll()` (drums, clicks, the headphones band), freezes the song clock (`songNow()` = `G.pausedRaw`) and the drawing, drops pending attacks and holds; nothing heard counts; no menu music. RESUME = back ONE MEASURE from the beat it stopped on (never before 0), a one-measure count-in, then on in step; notes already judged keep their result. SONG MENU = `showHub()` at once: no results, no stars, nothing saved, the song still selected. Practice loops pause the same way. `Arcade.Highway.pauses()` / `paused()` for tests (measured: 5 pauses in one song incl. the count-in, a held note and the end, drift ≤ 0.8 ms, no note judged twice).
- THE CLOCK: the arcade's AudioContext (`Sfx.output()`, read through `Arcade.AudioClock` in shared/calibration.js); drums scheduled on it `lookaheadS` ahead (backing.js `Arcade.MHBacking`: noise snare/hat/clicks + a 70 ms kick, grooves per style, no toms); the highway, staff and judge read the AUDIBLE time (`getOutputTimestamp` / `outputLatency`, smoothed), so nothing drifts (measured: 136 s at 4× CPU throttle, every note within 1 ms). Sound off = performance.now(). A one-measure count-in (clicks + hat + kick). THE CLICK (count-in + calibration): sounds.js `mh-click` (Mat's recording) else backing.js's generated woodblock (noise bands, no pitch, 55 ms, ~75 % of its energy 1.5–4 kHz, rendered once and normalized to peak 1; the downbeat ~15 % higher and louder, the others `CLICK.weak` 72 %); `kit.click()` returns its peak and the count-in's hat and kick stay in the headroom left, so `clickVol` 1 never clips. The count-in follows the EFFECTS slider; the calibration clicks use `Sfx.outputRaw()` (no EFFECTS slider, SOUND ON/OFF still mutes). `shared/sounds/mh-drums-<song id>.m4a` (optional; sounds.js makes the event for every song) replaces the generated drums (normal speed, pitched instruments). (Pause: see PAUSE above.)
- SLURS (winds only: the snare ignores them, the bells get no feedback): events carry `slur` (its group), `slurFirst`, `slurLast` (song-map.js); notation.js draws one arc per slur OPPOSITE THE STEMS (all stems up = under the heads, else over them; a row break runs it to the edge); the highway joins each slurred pad to the next with a thin ribbon (`HighwayDraw.drawSlurs`, `n.slurTo`). JUDGING NEVER CHANGES for a slur. FEEDBACK ONLY: a slurred note after the first of its slur arriving with a CLEAR new attack (the attack's `rise` ≥ `slurTongueRise` 3, within `slurAtkMs`) = "tongued", a smooth pitch change (the soft-entry path or a weaker attack) = SMOOTH, a small pop under the judgment; after the song ONE tip when more than `slurTipShare` of the slurred notes were tongued, for the slur tongued the most ("Measure 3: slur it! One air stream, move only your fingers."; trombone: "Keep the air moving through the slide change; a light 'doo' is OK.", `#slurTip`). SLUR TIPS On/Off in the Settings panel (`gameData.slurTips`, default On) hides the pops and the tip. `Arcade.Highway.slurLog()` / `slurTip()` / `slurLinks()` / `results()`, `autoPlay(ms, {slur: 'tongue' | 'smooth'})`; tests/music-highway-slurs.spec.js.
- JUDGING (`settings.js` MH_RULES): by the note's ATTACK (`Pitch.onAttack`) after the latency correction, confirmed by the right pitch class within `pitchConfirmMs` (the attack's own pitch, else the next readings); a note that starts with no attack heard (a very soft entry) counts from its first steady reading (`softEntryMs`); the closest open note with that pitch wins. PERFECT ±90 · GOOD ±170 · OK ±260 · EARLY/LATE to ±420 (counts, combo resets) · else MISS; wrong pitch = "That's D" (visual only). Score ×1–×4 by combo, hold bonus per beat held (letting go ends only the bonus). Accuracy = average of `value`; stars at 60/80/95 %. Results: counts, max combo, the TROUBLE SPOT (the 2 measures with the most misses, on a mini staff) + PRACTICE THIS PART (loops them at 60 %, `practiceLoops`). No sound effects during a song (results use the shared events).
- SNARE vs ITS OWN DRUMS: `onAttack` gets `level` (pitch.js); an attack within `bleedMs` of one of the backing's hits with no note near it (the count-in, between notes) teaches the bleed level; on a note such an attack counts only above `bleedK` × that level; the backing leaves out its own snare for a snare player. Verified with the game's output looped straight into the "microphone": trumpet, tuba and snare score 0 hits when nobody plays.
- CALIBRATION ("Play any note on each of the 8 clicks", after 4 to listen to): the CLICK NEVER COUNTS: a pitched instrument's attack needs a pitch; the snare's must be `bleedK` × louder than the attacks heard on the 4 listening clicks; a round whose offsets are machine-steady (< `calMinSpreadMs`) within `calClickMaxMs` of 0, or earlier than −`calTooEarlyMs`, is refused with "I heard the click, not your instrument. Try playing a little louder or moving the device a bit farther from you." (verified with the game's output looped into the microphone: 0 rounds accepted for trumpet, tuba, snare). Median offset of attacks to clicks, saved per device for speaker and headphones mode separately (`gameData('music-highway').calib`); THE FIRST SONG OF EVERY PLAY SESSION (per mode, `Arcade.session`) starts with it even on a calibrated device (done or skipped, it isn't forced again until a reload; a device never calibrated is still asked each song); RECALIBRATE on the song select any time. HEADPHONES MODE ("🎧 I'm wearing headphones", remembered): the SPEAKER CHECK plays three test notes while listening; heard (≥ `checkFrames` readings of 2 of them) = "It sounds like your speakers are on. Plug in headphones to use this mode." and it stays off; it runs each time the mode is turned on and before the first song of a page load with it on.
