# Blocktave (`blocktave/`)

- A side-view block-building survival game (zone adventure; `byMember`, `unpitched`, `noInstrument` for its TOUCH mode, `maxStars` 18) where PLAYING YOUR INSTRUMENT IS HOW YOU MINE AND CRAFT. Files: `rules.js` = `BT_RULES`, EVERY tuning number in one commented block (Mat tunes there, never in game.js); `recipes.js` = `BT_ITEMS` (item ids: never rename), `BT_RECIPES` (one commented table: ingredients IN ORDER, performance, output, count, `bench`) and `BT_CHAPTERS` (6 chapters × 3 milestone ids: never reorder or rename; append only); `world.js` = `Arcade.BlocktaveWorld` (the `BLOCKS` table: block ids are saved, never renumber, append only; the seeded generator; the saved format; `zone`, `light`, `room`); `challenges.js` = `Arcade.BlocktaveCard` (the challenge card); `game.js` (everything else). It loads `../rhythm-dojo/levels.js` for Rhythm Dojo's vetted 4/4 cells (Rhythm Rock, the snare's scale veins), never copied.
- FINISHED (TODAY'S PRACTICE): a night survived (`dawn()`) and a challenge card passed (`openCard`'s onDone with `ok`) call `store.noteFinished('blocktave')`.
- ORIGINALITY (hard rule): the genre is fine, copying is not. No Minecraft names or terms (no creeper, steve, ender, nether, redstone, "craft" in a name, "crafting table"), no look-alike grass/dirt texture (the ground is plum "Glow Moss" with a neon sound-wave edge), no pickaxe (MALLETS and a baton), no 3×3 grid (THE MEASURE), no blocky pixel font (titles GN Neon / Bungee, text Atkinson), no borrowed sounds. Our own look: a neon, stage-lit block world, `--bt-*` tokens in theme.css; blocks drawn once per size into an atlas (game.js `drawTile`). The player is the student's own AVATAR (`Avatar.sprites(member)`, shared/instrument-sprites.js). Creatures are original and silly, never scary (Showtime Malfunction is the spooky game).
- THE WORLD: `world.w × world.h` (256 × 96) tiles from a SEED (`?seed=` picks a new world's seed; tests use it): surface, hills, caves, water; three biomes left to right (`biomes`: Reed Marsh = reed cane, cork, pad felt, maple; Brass Mountains = Brass Ore, Spring Veins, one peak always reaching the Treble Peaks; Percussion Canyon = maple, rawhide, Rhythm Rock, clay). LAYERS (`BW.zone`): rows above `peaksY` = TREBLE PEAKS (treble clef), rows from `deepY` = BASS DEPTHS (bass clef), the local surface + `shallow` = surface, else middle; the CLEF there is the treble/bass one, everywhere else the student's own (reading the other clef is part of the adventure; its single notes are filtered to that clef's comfortable range (`otherRange`: treble 55–84, bass 36–64; if fewer than needed fit, the pool is moved by octaves first), and the first `otherClefNames` cards show the name small under the staff). Other-clef SCALES are moved by whole octaves into that clef's comfortable range (display only; playing is by pitch class), and every staff box grows to fit its notes. (`octaveShift`: the whole scale moves by the same k×12, most notes inside the range, ties to the octave nearest the staff's middle; spelling, key signature and order never change. A moved scale's first `otherClefNames` cards, counted in `gameData.otherClef`, show the line "Bass clef! Play it where it sits on your instrument." (Treble in the Peaks) under the staff, `.bt-cnote`. challenges.js `staffBox` sizes every shared-layout staff's viewBox from its note heads, stems, ledger lines and accidentals, so the card grows instead of clipping, never below `noteMinPx`; on phones the card sheet has a max height and scrolls.) NOTE POOLS by layer, only from `buildSequence` for the member: surface `first5`, middle a concert scale, Peaks/Depths `chrom`. Drawn on ONE canvas: the parallax backdrop (below), only visible tiles, the light as one pixel per tile scaled up (LIGHT below), ≤ `fps` 60, dpr ≤ `dprMax`, nothing while hidden (rAF) or paused. DAY `dayS` 360 / NIGHT `nightS` 180 (the sky, the light and the music change: `blocktave-day` / `blocktave-night` loops, mic: false). Creatures come out only in the dark: at night above ground, in unlit caves any time. THE STARTER GUARANTEE (world.js `starter` / `ensureStarter`, rules.js `starterRange` 16 + `starter`): every world has, within `starterRange` columns of the spawn, ≥ 2 Maple and ≥ 2 Cork trees on dry ground (a reed in the way is cleared, never planted in water) and ≥ 12 Tone Ore at most `oreDepth` 8 rows under the ground; a new world's spawn is a dry column with at least `starterRange` dry columns around it; marsh trees `corkChance` / `mapleChance`. Each world saves its generator version `gen` (old saves = 1) and `repaired`: THE ONE-TIME REPAIR (`BW.repair`, on load) regenerates the seed's ORIGINAL with that `gen` and, if the starter check fails, grows the missing trees and ore ONLY into tiles still equal to the original (never over or inside anything placed or dug), then the toast "New trees have grown near your camp!" (tests: 500 seeds, chapter 1 end to end on 20 seeds, the repair). The save also keeps the Band Lockers' contents (`lockers`). THE GENERATED GROUND (`w.ground`, world.js `terrain` / `groundOf` / `groundAt`): the generator's surface row per column (`heights`), kept on the world and NEVER saved: any loaded world rebuilds it from its seed (`terrain` draws the same random numbers in the same order as the generator, so it's the exact original line); a world the seed can't rebuild (another size) falls back to the live top, smoothed (the median over ±8 columns), once. It never moves when the student digs or builds. The cave backdrop and the layers (`BW.zone`'s "surface" = within `shallow` rows of the GENERATED ground, so a shaft's bottom stays Underground) count from it; the light, spawning in the dark, THE WAY UP, the cave music's depth (a median of the nearby live ground) and standing spots keep the live `top`.
- MINING = PLAYING: a tap/click on a block within `player.reach` (MINE mode; BUILD mode places the hotbar's block, or USES a door/cot/locker/composer/podium/bench). Tap blocks (dirt, sand, leaves, plants, trunks, placed blocks) break at once (slate and bricks need a Wooden Mallet); MUSIC BLOCKS open a CHALLENGE CARD beside the block (it follows the camera) while the world runs at `challengeSlow` 0.3×: Tone/Brass Ore = the written note(s) (INSTRUMENT `Pitch.onHeld` pc; TOUCH the answer pad, the name as shown, key signature included), Scale/Spring Veins = 5–8 notes of a scale in order, Sustain Stone = one steady in-tune note (`sustainS`, cents from `reading.midi`; TOUCH: "Which key signature is this?" / "Name this scale" from scales.js), Rhythm Rock = a one-measure rhythm (a SILENT count-in light: nothing plays while listening; INSTRUMENT shared/onsets.js, TOUCH the TAP pad or Space; judged by shared/rhythm-judge.js minus `Calibration.lag`), Rest Crystal = rests (silent!) then the downbeat. Notes per card = `notes[kind][tool tier]` (hands, Wooden / Brass / Silver Mallet, Golden Baton: the tier sets what can be mined; the Brass Mallet makes Tone Ore one note; the Baton shortens long tones and rolls). INSTRUMENT mode drops `instrumentBonus` 2× what TOUCH drops. A wrong answer shakes the card and the block stays; `hintAfterWrong` 3 wrong in a row show the names. SNARE DRUM: Tone Ore = "play exactly N hits, then stop" (`snareCount`), Scale Veins / Rhythm Rock = rhythms, Sustain Stone / Sour Wisps = an EVEN ROLL (`rollS`, `rollRate`, `rollMaxCv`; the bar counts REAL milliseconds of even roll, losing it drains 1.5× as fast, so a slow device's frame rate never makes a roll longer); every block is mineable, in both modes. TOUCH mode is the same for every member: the snare reads the bells' notes and taps rhythms; counts and rolls are INSTRUMENT-only. (game.js `drum()` = INSTRUMENT mode on the snare: only then are the cards drum performances (count, roll, the drum's rhythms) and the words say "play" / "hits"; in TOUCH the snare's reading member and group are the BELLS (`readM()` / `readG()`: treble clef, the bells' range, their scales and key questions, the Composer and Podium too), a Night Clam shows a note, a Sour Wisp asks its key question, and every word follows the MODE first, then the snare: the cards' subtitles, the first-time cards ("Mining = music!"), the creature intros, the recipe lines (`PERF_TOUCH`), the podium and the Settings row ("Answer by"); TOUCH never says "play" or "hits". No microphone is ever requested in TOUCH. Tests: "TOUCH mode is the same for every member".) THE MODE (INSTRUMENT | TOUCH: the title screen, the pause menu's "Switch to…", the Settings panel; `gameData.mode`, default INSTRUMENT) needs a saved instrument (requireInstrument). THE MICROPHONE LISTENS ONLY WHILE A CARD IS OPEN OR A CREATURE IS NEAR (INSTRUMENT mode; `listenSync`: `Pitch.pauseListening`), so the day/night music plays otherwise; every effect is unpitched noise and short.
