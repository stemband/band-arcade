# Blocktave (`blocktave/`)

- A side-view block-building survival game (zone adventure; `byMember`, `unpitched`, `noInstrument` for its TOUCH mode, `maxStars` 15) where PLAYING YOUR INSTRUMENT IS HOW YOU MINE AND CRAFT. Files: `rules.js` = `BT_RULES`, EVERY tuning number in one commented block (Mat tunes there, never in game.js); `recipes.js` = `BT_ITEMS` (item ids: never rename), `BT_RECIPES` (one commented table: ingredients IN ORDER, performance, output, count, `bench`) and `BT_CHAPTERS` (5 chapters × 3 milestone ids: never reorder or rename); `world.js` = `Arcade.BlocktaveWorld` (the `BLOCKS` table: block ids are saved, never renumber; the seeded generator; the saved format; `zone`, `light`, `room`); `challenges.js` = `Arcade.BlocktaveCard` (the challenge card); `game.js` (everything else). It loads `../rhythm-dojo/levels.js` for Rhythm Dojo's vetted 4/4 cells (Rhythm Rock, the snare's scale veins), never copied.
- ORIGINALITY (hard rule): the genre is fine, copying is not. No Minecraft names or terms (no creeper, steve, ender, nether, redstone, "craft" in a name, "crafting table"), no look-alike grass/dirt texture (the ground is plum "Glow Moss" with a neon sound-wave edge), no pickaxe (MALLETS and a baton), no 3×3 grid (THE MEASURE), no blocky pixel font (titles GN Neon / Bungee, text Atkinson), no borrowed sounds. Our own look: a neon, stage-lit block world, `--bt-*` tokens in theme.css; blocks drawn once per size into an atlas (game.js `drawTile`). The player is the student's own AVATAR (`Avatar.sprites(member)`, shared/instrument-sprites.js). Creatures are original and silly, never scary (Showtime Malfunction is the spooky game).
- THE WORLD: `world.w × world.h` (256 × 96) tiles from a SEED (`?seed=` picks a new world's seed; tests use it): surface, hills, caves, water; three biomes left to right (`biomes`: Reed Marsh = reed cane, cork, pad felt, maple; Brass Mountains = Brass Ore, Spring Veins, one peak always reaching the Treble Peaks; Percussion Canyon = maple, rawhide, Rhythm Rock, clay). LAYERS (`BW.zone`): rows above `peaksY` = TREBLE PEAKS (treble clef), rows from `deepY` = BASS DEPTHS (bass clef), the local surface + `shallow` = surface, else middle; the CLEF there is the treble/bass one, everywhere else the student's own (reading the other clef is part of the adventure; its notes are filtered to that clef's comfortable range, and the first `otherClefNames` cards show the name small under the staff). NOTE POOLS by layer, only from `buildSequence` for the member: surface `first5`, middle a concert scale, Peaks/Depths `chrom`. Drawn on ONE canvas: the parallax backdrop (below), only visible tiles, the light as one pixel per tile scaled up (LIGHT below), ≤ `fps` 60, dpr ≤ `dprMax`, nothing while hidden (rAF) or paused. DAY `dayS` 360 / NIGHT `nightS` 180 (the sky, the light and the music change: `blocktave-day` / `blocktave-night` loops, mic: false). Creatures come out only in the dark: at night above ground, in unlit caves any time. THE STARTER GUARANTEE (world.js `starter` / `ensureStarter`, rules.js `starterRange` 16 + `starter`): every world has, within `starterRange` columns of the spawn, ≥ 2 Maple and ≥ 2 Cork trees on dry ground (a reed in the way is cleared, never planted in water) and ≥ 12 Tone Ore at most `oreDepth` 8 rows under the ground; a new world's spawn is a dry column with at least `starterRange` dry columns around it; marsh trees `corkChance` / `mapleChance`. Each world saves its generator version `gen` (old saves = 1) and `repaired`: THE ONE-TIME REPAIR (`BW.repair`, on load) regenerates the seed's ORIGINAL with that `gen` and, if the starter check fails, grows the missing trees and ore ONLY into tiles still equal to the original (never over or inside anything placed or dug), then the toast "New trees have grown near your camp!" (tests: 500 seeds, chapter 1 end to end on 20 seeds, the repair). The save also keeps the Band Lockers' contents (`lockers`).
- MINING = PLAYING: a tap/click on a block within `player.reach` (MINE mode; BUILD mode places the hotbar's block, or USES a door/cot/locker/composer/podium/bench). Tap blocks (dirt, sand, leaves, plants, trunks, placed blocks) break at once (slate and bricks need a Wooden Mallet); MUSIC BLOCKS open a CHALLENGE CARD beside the block (it follows the camera) while the world runs at `challengeSlow` 0.3×: Tone/Brass Ore = the written note(s) (INSTRUMENT `Pitch.onHeld` pc; TOUCH the answer pad, the name as shown, key signature included), Scale/Spring Veins = 5–8 notes of a scale in order, Sustain Stone = one steady in-tune note (`sustainS`, cents from `reading.midi`; TOUCH: "Which key signature is this?" / "Name this scale" from scales.js), Rhythm Rock = a one-measure rhythm (a SILENT count-in light: nothing plays while listening; INSTRUMENT shared/onsets.js, TOUCH the TAP pad or Space; judged by shared/rhythm-judge.js minus `Calibration.lag`), Rest Crystal = rests (silent!) then the downbeat. Notes per card = `notes[kind][tool tier]` (hands, Wooden / Brass / Silver Mallet, Golden Baton: the tier sets what can be mined; the Brass Mallet makes Tone Ore one note; the Baton shortens long tones and rolls). INSTRUMENT mode drops `instrumentBonus` 2× what TOUCH drops. A wrong answer shakes the card and the block stays; `hintAfterWrong` 3 wrong in a row show the names. SNARE DRUM: Tone Ore = "play exactly N hits, then stop" (`snareCount`), Scale Veins / Rhythm Rock = rhythms, Sustain Stone / Sour Wisps = an EVEN ROLL (`rollS`, `rollRate`, `rollMaxCv`); every block is mineable, in both modes. THE MODE (INSTRUMENT | TOUCH: the title screen, the pause menu's "Switch to…", the Settings panel; `gameData.mode`, default INSTRUMENT) needs a saved instrument (requireInstrument). THE MICROPHONE LISTENS ONLY WHILE A CARD IS OPEN OR A CREATURE IS NEAR (INSTRUMENT mode; `listenSync`: `Pitch.pauseListening`), so the day/night music plays otherwise; every effect is unpitched noise and short.
- CRAFTING = PERFORMING (CRAFT / C, or a Luthier's Bench): THE MEASURE = a CRAFTING STATION (game.js `drawCraft`, `.bt-bench` in style.css, wood tokens `--bt-wood*` / `--bt-slot-bg`): a wooden workbench with a short staff carved into its top edge (decoration), 4 big labeled SLOTS in a row (`#measure`: "1st"…"4th" above, "Material 1"… under an empty one; ≥ 64 px; empty = dashed with a faint "+", filled = the item's big icon + name + a small ✕ that takes it out, the rest move up), an arrow → the RESULT box (`#recipeLine`: "?" until the slots match a recipe, then its icon, name, "× 4" and the performance: `PERF` / `PERF_TOUCH` / `PERF_SNARE`, e.g. "Play 1 note", "Concert B♭ scale", "4 steady beats"; "Needs a Luthier's Bench nearby" when a `bench: true` recipe has none within `benchRange`), and MAKE IT (`#perform`, yellow, DISABLED until a recipe matches and its bench is near) = the performance card: pass = the item is made, fail = the ingredients stay. Below: YOUR MATERIALS (`#craftItems`, item tiles: tap, or DRAG one onto the bench, = the next empty slot; the tiles block the browser's own image drag). Ingredients go IN ORDER (the same items in another order are no recipe: `BT_RECIPES` + `match()` are unchanged, only the screen is the station). ≤ 760 px: the slots 2 × 2, the result below. THE RECIPE BOOK (the RECIPE BOOK button): a recipe is FOUND the first time you hold all its ingredients (`gameData.found`); unknown ones are "?" silhouettes (`BT_ALWAYS_SHOWN` always show); tapping one fills the slots in order when you have its items, else shows what's missing in red ("Missing: 1 Maple", `fromBook`). The first bench is made by hand.
- BUILDING: Maple Planks, Soundproof Panel, Band Hall Brick, Stage Floor, Riser Steps (climb with JUMP), a 2-tall Door (BUILD mode taps open/close it), Glass, Stage Lamp (lights `light.lampRadius`: nothing spawns in light), Practice Cot (BUILD-tap: your wake-up spot, and at night sleep till morning when no creature is within `cotSafe`), Band Locker (`lockerSlots` stacks), Snack Bag (+`snackHeal` hearts: tap its hotbar slot twice), Music Stand, Metronome (creatures within its radius move at `metronomeSlow`), Tuner (Sour Wisps can't enter its radius), Composer Block + Conductor's Podium: up to `composerMax` 8 Composer Blocks in a row, each tapped (BUILD) for its note (the member's chromatic range; the snare picks from the bells'), a podium at the row's end shows the melody on a staff: PERFORM it (TOUCH: tap the names; snare: one steady hit a note) = the row is POWERED (it glows) and opens doors within `doorWire`; ▶ LISTEN plays it with `Sfx.piano` only while nothing listens (the one pitched sound; the first Composer Block's card calls it the composing corner).
- SURVIVAL: 5 hearts drawn as whole notes (no hunger). NIGHT CLAMS hop at you with a note bubble (INSTRUMENT: play that note nearby; TOUCH: tap it; snare: a count) → a Pearl (dropped, see WORLD DROPS); SOUR WISPS drain ½ heart every `wispDrainS` when close (INSTRUMENT: hold any steady in-tune note near one; TOUCH: its key question; snare: a roll) → Pitch Dust (Stage Lamps need it); RUSHERS (from night `spawn.rushersFrom` 3) charge, and a 2-beat rhythm card opens: match it before it arrives → a Valve Spring. FAIRNESS: spawn caps per night (`perNight`, `atOnce`), never within `spawn.safe` tiles, never in light, never inside a closed room with a door (`BW.room`), creatures freeze while `Pitch.isSuppressed`, and THE FAIRNESS CHECK slows a creature until its challenge × `fair.margin` fits the time it needs to reach you. LOSING ALL HEARTS: back at the Practice Cot (else the spawn point), full hearts, tools and the world kept, `dropShare` 25 % of each carried material in a floating BAG where you fell (walk into it). First-time cards (welcome, the first challenge, the first night, each creature, the first Composer Block) are `UI.intro` and pause the world (`gameData.seen`).
- WORLD DROPS AND THE PICKUP RADIUS (game.js `dropItem` / `stepDrops`): a mined block's item pops out of the broken block, and a calmed creature's (Night Clam → Pearl, Sour Wisp → Pitch Dust, Rusher → Valve Spring) out of its spot, as a WORLD ITEM (a small icon: a little arc, then gravity onto the nearest solid ground below, never inside a block, bobbing gently there; still with reduced motion); INSTRUMENT mode's bonus = more items (2, `dropSpread` apart). Nothing goes straight into the bag any more (crafting output, the Locker, your bag and the Survival Nights kit still do). Every drop within `pickupRadius` 1.5 tiles of your body (after its 250 ms pop) is yours; within `magnetRadius` 2.5 it glides to you first (`magnetSpeed`); collecting = `bt-pickup` + the "+1 Pearl" label. A FULL BAG (`invSlots` 24 different items, tools aside, or one item at its limit: `canHold`) leaves it on the ground with "Bag full!" (at most every `bagFullToastS`). Off screen a drop despawns after `dropDespawnS` 600 s (its `t` counts only while off screen, so never one you can see); the lost-hearts BAG is separate and never does. At most `maxDrops` 80: the oldest beyond that joins the nearest drop of its kind (`capDrops`: nothing is lost). Milestones that count creatures still count at the calming moment, not at pickup; the fairness check and creature counts are unchanged.
- LITTLE TOUCHES (reduced motion / the MOTION switch: no swing, chips, poof or drifting text; only a fade or nothing): PICKUP LABELS ("+1 Maple", `pickupLabel` / `drawLabels`, on the sharp overlay after the light with a dark outline): every gain (drops, crafting, the Locker, your bag, a Snack Bag eaten = "+2 Hearts") shows the item's icon + "+n Name" over your head (INSTRUMENT's bonus is in the number: "+2 Tone Shard"), drifting up and fading over `pickupLabelMs`; the same item within `pickupMergeMs` counts up in one label; at most `pickupLabelsMax` 4, stacked upward; the same words go to the `aria-live` region `#btLive`, gathered, at most every `pickupAriaMs`. TOOLTIPS on every item (`[data-item]`: the hotbar, the inventory, a Band Locker, the Recipe Book, the Measure's slots and materials; `#btTip`): hover or keyboard focus shows it after `tipDelayMs`, a touch long-press (`tipLongPressMs`) shows it and lifting hides it (that tap then picks nothing; a normal tap still selects/places); the name, the item's `desc` (recipes.js, one line for 6th graders), "Found: …" for materials (`found`), "Can mine: …" for tools (from the BLOCKS' tiers); it flips above/below/right/left to stay on screen, never covers its item, and opens through `UI.layer` (above every panel). NO REACH CIRCLE: a tile in reach shows the target highlight on hover/aim; a tap on a block OUT of reach outlines it faintly in red for `farFlashMs` with "Too far: walk closer!" (at most every `farToastS`). THE SWING (`swing` / `drawSwing`, `swingMs` 260): mining, tapping a creature or a block's challenge passed turns the avatar to the target and arcs its tool (the item's own icon per tier) from behind the head down toward it; no tool = a quick reach; a new swing restarts it; a failed answer = no swing. A block breaking throws `chips` (4–6 squares in its color, ≤ 300 ms). THE POOF (`poof`, `poofMs` 450): a calmed creature shrinks into a soft puff of cloud in its colors with a few notes floating up (never a flash, never bright white) + `bt-poof` (sounds.js, `play: true`, falls back to `bt-calm`). `Arcade.Blocktave.state()` adds `drops`, `labels`, `tip`, `slots`, `fx` ({swings, chips, poofs, labels, tips, picked, swing, far, rm}); demo `drop`, `calm`, `canHold`, `fromBook`, `addToSlot`. Tests: tests/blocktave.spec.js (labels, tooltips, reach, swing/poof/reduced motion, the Measure at 4 sizes, drops and the pickup radius).
- SAVING: ONE WORLD PER DEVICE in localStorage `bandarcade.blocktave.world` = `BW.encode` `{v: 2, seed, w, h, chunks (16 columns each, run-length encoded column by column), meta (composer notes, powered rows), lockers, bags, drops ([{x, y, item, n, t}]: v 2; a v 1 save loads with none), spawn, time, nights, survived, player, cot}`; autosaved every `autosaveS` 30 s, on pause, when the page is hidden and on leaving. It is NOT in the Arcade Backup Code (too big: storage.js never sees it). The pause menu: SAVE WORLD TO FILE (downloads `blocktave-world.json`), LOAD WORLD FILE (asks first), NEW WORLD (asks TWICE: "Your whole world will be replaced"), and once per device the note "Your world lives on this device. Save it to a file to keep a copy." Small things live in `gameData('blocktave')` (in the Backup Code): `{mode, ms: {member: {milestone: date}}, stats: {member: {ore, clams, wisps, mined}}, found, seen, otherClef, controls}`.
- STARS COME ONLY FROM MILESTONES (hard rule): `BT_CHAPTERS` 5 × 3 (First Steps: a Wooden Mallet / 10 Tone Ore / a shelter with a door; First Night: survive a night / place a Stage Lamp / calm 5 Night Clams; Workshop: place a Luthier's Bench / a Metronome / a Tuner; Deep Notes: reach the Bass Depths or Treble Peaks / mine a Sustain Stone / dispel 3 Sour Wisps; Encore: the Band Hall (`rules.js bandHall`: a closed room with a door, ≥ `minTiles` inside, its walls ≥ `brickShare` Band Hall Bricks, ≥ `stageMin` Stage Floor under it, a Stage Lamp and a Music Stand inside) / power a Composer row of 8 / the Golden Baton). `award(id)` = `setLevel('blocktave', member, chapter, {stars: its milestones done})`, per MEMBER, each once; mining or building more never adds a star, so the leaderboard's `stars` events come only from milestones. A chapter's third milestone shows its results (KEEP BUILDING / CHAPTERS). THE CURRENT CHAPTER = the lowest one not finished: the in-game goals panel (`#goals`) shows it, a "How?" line (recipes.js `hint`, chapter 1's three) under its first unfinished goal, and "+ N milestones already done in later chapters". A later chapter's milestone done early counts at once ("★ Bonus milestone (Chapter 2): Place a Stage Lamp!"), but its chapter's "complete!" results wait until the player REACHES it (every earlier chapter finished: `checkReached`, `gameData.shownCh[member]`; older saves count every finished chapter as shown); until then its card says "Complete · finished early". The chapter select starts on the current chapter. The Recipe Book always shows `BT_ALWAYS_SHOWN` (Maple Planks, Wooden Mallet, Door) with their ingredients; Tone Ore tapped without a mallet = a first-time card ("Planks, Planks, Cork"). A card's goal list is `.bt-card-goals` (in normal flow under the stars line; NEVER the HUD's absolute `.bt-goals`), the cards grow to fit. The level select = the 5 chapter cards (all open) + SURVIVAL NIGHTS (the Endless card: a fresh unsaved world, `endless` numbers, one life, score = nights survived, Top 5 per member, in `Leaderboard.ENDLESS_GAMES`; no milestones there). 3 ★ on Chapter 5 unlocks the Blocktave Builder hat (head `blocktave`) and the Band Hall background (`bandhall`, bg-scenes.js `S.bandhall`); never at the Token Booth.

- THE NOTE LAYOUT (challenges.js `layoutNotes` / `staffRows`: ONE shared layout for EVERY Blocktave staff with several notes: creature cards, Scale Veins, every note card, "Name this scale", Composer rows and the Conductor's Podium; never per-card numbers; rules.js `staffLead` 40, `staffGap` 56, `staffAccRoom` 20, `staffMinW` 420, `staffTail` 12, `noteMinPx` 14, in staff units: the lines are 16 apart): the first note's box (its ledger lines, or its ♯/♭/♮) starts `staffLead` after the right edge of the clef + key signature (`headRight`, measured with GN Music: clef 60 treble / 66 bass, the first sign 70 / 76, +12 a sign); notes are `staffGap` apart center to center, + `staffAccRoom` in front of an accidental; a staff is at least `staffMinW` wide when the screen has room: 1–3 notes CENTERED in the space after the clef, 4+ SPREAD evenly across it (the gaps only grow). The card grows to fit (`fitCard`: up to 92vw; on ≤ 760 px the sheet along the bottom, its padding trimmed); a notehead is never drawn under `noteMinPx` tall on screen: notes that can't fit across at that size WRAP onto more staff rows (`.bt-srow`, each with its own clef and key signature, all one width; two on a phone, three on the narrowest phones with a long key signature) instead of shrinking; a resize lays the card out again.
