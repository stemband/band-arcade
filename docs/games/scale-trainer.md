# Scale Trainer (`scale-trainer/`)

- RENAMED: this game was Scale Audition (id `scale-audition`); it is Scale Trainer, id `scale-trainer` (folder, GAME_ID, progress keys, `gameData`, `menuMusic` `scale-trainer-menu`, `Arcade.ScaleTrainer`). The AUDITION mode, the "Audition Room" level and avatar background, the `AUDITION` table, the `audition` cabinet/scene/marquee/bg ids and the `audition-count-*` / `sa-adjudicator` sounds keep their names: they describe the audition, not the game. The one-time migration `scale-trainer-rename` in storage.js `migrate()` (`renameGame(old, new, {gameData})`) moves every `scale-audition` / `scale-audition:…` progress key, `gameData('scale-audition')` (ALL-STATE READY, best clean runs, chromatic best times, options), the level select's memory, the lobby's CONTINUE, the activity log and queued leaderboard events to the new id, keeping the better value on a clash; `importAll` runs it too, so an old Backup Code restores into the new keys. `scale-audition/index.html` is ONLY a redirect (noindex, no game code, nothing links to it) to `../scale-trainer/index.html` with the query string and hash. A menu track uploaded under the old name (`scale-audition-menu.m4a`) still plays: sounds.js `legacy` → the `scale-trainer-menu-oldfile` entry, tried by `Sfx.gameMenuMusic` after the game's own file. tests/scale-trainer.spec.js checks the migration, the old backup, the redirect and that no other file mentions the old name.
- The GMEA All-State (First Round) / District Honor Band scale audition (Technique Lab; the two auditions have identical requirements, and the first screen says so: "GMEA All-State (First Round) & District Honor Band: same scales, same rules."), in THREE SECTIONS. LISTENS (pitch.js + mic-gate.js); `byMember: true` (each member's GMEA scales differ). Snare: refused (no `unpitched`; `fit` excludes only snare). THE UI KIT: level select (the section cards, a mode row + the cards; `LevelSelect.show` with `gameId` `scale-trainer:<mode>` (Middle School, as always) or `scale-trainer:<section>:<mode>`), pause (the clock stops), results `actsFirst` with THE JUDGE'S SCORE SHEET as the extra.
- THE THREE SECTIONS (`Scales.SECTIONS`, `{id, name, grades, memory}`): MIDDLE SCHOOL `ms` (6th–8th grade · 4 scales · memory: everything below as it always was), CONCERT BAND `cb` (9th–10th grade · 8 scales · music allowed), SYMPHONIC BAND `sb` (11th–12th grade · all 12 scales · music allowed). THE SECTION PICKER = three big cards at the top of the level screen (`#secPick`, `.sa-sec` with `aria-pressed`; each: name, the grade line, its stars `n/12 ★` for this instrument, its ALL-STATE READY badge, and "GMEA All-State (First Round) & District Honor Band"); remembered in `gameData('scale-trainer').section` (default `ms`). Inside a section everything works the same (PRACTICE, AUDITION levels, CHROMATIC CHALLENGE, score sheet, fingering cards), built from that section's data (game.js `secData` = `SEC`: scales, chrom, sheet time, tempo, progress key, levels, the mic range).
- THE AUDITION TABLE = `AUDITION[section][member]` in `shared/scales.js` (next to `START`, which is unchanged; a commented header names the source sheets): `{time (the sheet's seconds), scales: [[concert key, the WRITTEN starting note, octaves 1 | 2 | 3], …] in the order printed, tempo? (the sheet's ♩ =)}` or `{time, sameAs: '<member>'}` (barisax = altosax, Baritone T.C. = trumpet, Euphonium B.C. = trombone in cb/sb). THE GMEA SHEETS ARE THE SOURCE: EDIT AUDITION, NOT THE GAME. The written start note also fixes the written key's spelling (`majorScale(midi, spelling)`): trumpet concert B = written D♭ major (5 flats), horn concert B = G♭ major, clarinet concert E = F♯ major. `Scales.AUDITION_ORDER[section]` = ms F, B♭, E♭, A♭; cb G, C, F, B♭, E♭, A♭, D♭, G♭; sb G, C, F, B♭, E♭, A♭, D♭, G♭, B, E, A, D ("must be performed in the order printed"; not `Scales.LIST`'s order). `Scales.CONCERT` = the 12 keys' names + pitch classes. MS times: clarinet and bassoon 75, bells 75 (the 2025-26 GMEA mallet sheet; its F scale from F3), the rest 60. Helpers (every reader takes a section id, default 'ms'): `auditionEntry(m, sec)` (sameAs resolved), `audition(member, key, sec)`, `auditionScales(m, sec)` (in order), `auditionChromatic(m, sec)`, `auditionTime(m, sec)`, `auditionTempo(m, sec)` (cb ♩ = 132 except the horn / tenor / oboe sheets, which print none; sb only horn ♩ = 144), `auditionRange(m, sec)` (the widest of its scales and its chromatic, written midi). `audition` = the scale up and down + its arpeggio for ANY NUMBER OF OCTAVES (1 octave: 21 notes, 3-5-8-5-3-1; 2 octaves: 41, 3-5-8-10-12-15-12-10-8-5-3-1; 3 octaves (flute C, clarinet G/F/F♯/E, bassoon B♭): 61, 3-5-8-10-12-15-17-19-22 and back), spelled by scales.js (`majorScale`, `keySignature`, `shown`: never a second speller), each note with `beats` + `measure` = the sheet's 4/4 rhythm picture (`rhythmOf(octaves)` = 2n scale bars + n arpeggio bars + the whole note; only a picture, never judged): the SAME note values for every octave count, more octaves = more measures: scale bars = quarter + 2 eighths + 4 eighths (7 notes), arpeggio bars = quarter, 2 eighths, quarter, 2 eighths (6 notes; the first starts on the scale's last note), a final whole note. 1 octave = 4 bars `| 1–7 | 8–2 | 1 3 5 8 5 3 | 1 |`; 2 octaves = 7 bars `| 1–7 | 8–14 | 15–9 | 8–2 | 1 3 5 8 10 12 | 15 12 10 8 5 3 | 1 |`; 3 octaves = 10 bars. Never sixteenths: the staff wraps to more lines instead. ARTICULATION as on the sheets: tongue going up, slur coming down: `slurs` = [first, last] note indexes, the scale's top note down to the tonic where the scale ends (n octaves: 7n→14n) and the arpeggio's top note down to the final whole note (17n→the last); the arpeggio going up stays tongued; mallets (`family: 'percussion'`) have none (mallets can't slur); the chromatic none (tongued or slurred is the student's choice). THE CHROMATIC: Middle School = `Arcade.chromaticScale` over the member's GMEA range (instruments.js `chromatic` STAYS THE MIDDLE SCHOOL RANGE; sharps up, flats down; `AUDITION_CHROM` = the MS-only mallet range F4–F6, the bells member is unchanged; the GMEA mallet sheet says from C: Mat: decide); high school (cb and sb share one sheet set) = `Scales.CHROMATIC_HS[member]` = [lowest, highest] WRITTEN (flute C4–C7, oboe and saxes B♭3–F6, clarinet E3–G6, bass clarinet E3–C6, horn F3–A5, trumpet / Baritone T.C. F♯3–C6, bassoon B♭1–B♭4, trombone / Euphonium B.C. E2–B♭4, tuba E1–B♭3, bells C4–C6 (the mallet sheet: "2-octave chromatic starting on C, after the major scales, not in the time limit")): sharps going up, flats coming down, the lowest note too (A♯3 up, B♭3 down; F♯3 up, G♭3 down), the top note played once. THE SECTION'S RANGE: the mic range (`Pitch.setRange`, Middle School: the member's GMEA range as always; cb / sb: `auditionRange`, sounding), the staff and the fingering card follow the section. Mallets: 2 octaves each; no range check (they audition on a bigger instrument than the bell kit): `Pitch.setRange(MALLET_RANGE)` (a wide sounding range, never the bells range), judged by pitch class. MAT: CONFIRM (in the table's comments): SB tenor sax 2:15 (the sheet's footer), the CB trombone / tuba times (2:00 assumed: no time printed on the older sheets), the CB oboe sheet's year (2024-25: not in the 2025-26 folder), the bells' MS chromatic (C vs F).
- ARTICULATION (winds): the sheet draws each slur as a curve above the notes (it follows the line of the notes and bends just enough to clear every note and stem, `sa-slur`, split at a line break: the part on each row runs to the row's edge, `data-part` start / middle / end / whole); "Tongue going up, slur coming down." (`#artLine`) under the scale's name in Practice and on levels 1–2 (not mallets, not the chromatic). FEEDBACK from `Pitch.onAttack` (every attack's time, `G.attacks`): a note the follower COUNTED (only those: never a mistake on a note that wasn't counted) had an attack if one falls between the previous note's count and its own, both shifted back by `ATTACK_LAG_MS`; tongued notes (not under a slur) want one, notes under a slur after its first want none (the first may have one: not judged); ✓ when at least `ARTICULATION_TOLERANCE` (80 %) of each kind matches. The score sheet has one line per scale: "Articulation: ✓" or "Articulation: check tonguing going up / slurring coming down" (whichever went wrong). `ARTICULATION_COUNTS = false`: attack detection at speed isn't reliable enough on every mic to cost a student stars, so it never changes stars, clean notes or the badge (true = 3 ★ also need every scale's ✓). ?demo Space tongues where the music says (an attack going up, none under a slur).
- HEARING (game.js, every number in the TUNING block at its top): the expected notes are followed IN ORDER from `Pitch.onFrame` (never `onHeld`: students play up to ~2 notes a second and may slur): a new note = the same pitch class for `STABLE_FRAMES` (3) frames AND a change from the last counted pitch, or a fresh `Pitch.onAttack` (a repeated note), or `GAP_FRAMES` of silence first. Winds: `Pitch.setRange(member.soundLow, soundHigh)`; judged by pitch class (the scale's order catches octave mistakes). Right = green (`ok`), advance; wrong = the expected note red (`bad`) once, still waited for, then amber (`fix`, not clean) when played; the NEXT expected note instead of this one = this one red ("skipped"), advance two; the note just counted, held or played again = ignored. No sound per note; the clock pauses during `Pitch.isSuppressed`, the pause and a hidden tab.
- MODES (`gameData('scale-trainer').mode`): PRACTICE (a card per scale in audition order + Chromatic, labeled like `Scales.build`'s label; no clock, no stars; LOOP (a running count of clean runs), SLOW GUIDE ♩ = 60/80/100 + the sheet's own tempo where it prints one ("♩ = 132, audition tempo" in Concert Band, 144 for the Symphonic Band horn: `auditionTempo`, a `data-sheet` button added per section) (a silent moving highlight along the rhythm picture; never judges; no metronome: a click would mute the mic), NOTE BY NOTE (one note big + its name + the fingering from `shared/fingerings.js` via `Arcade.Masher` when the member has one); "Best clean run" per scale = `gameData.practice[member][scale id]` (tenths; cb / sb: `practice.cb[member]…`)). AUDITION (the levels): the adjudicator's line ("Please play your scales in order, from memory."; optional voice `sa-adjudicator`) + a CLASSIC countdown (`voicePrefix 'audition'`, falls back to Dojo Duel's voice) while the mic waits, then ONE clock for every scale of the section, the next staff when a scale's last note is heard, no restarts. Levels (`LEVELS` in game.js): LEVELS 1–2 NEVER ASK FOR MEMORY OR TIME: the full staff (notes, key signature, slurs) for EVERY scale, the adjudicator says "Please play your scales in order." (no "from memory"), NO TIMER (no time bar, no amber, no "Time. Thank you.": the run ends when the last scale's last note is heard; a stopwatch that pauses with suppression and PAUSE; the score sheet shows "Your time 1:48 · Audition limit 1:00" as information only, never ✗): Warm-Up Room ("Music shown · No timer"; the current note's name under its notehead, `.sa-name`) · Practice Room ("Music shown · No timer"; the staff only); LEVELS 3–4 = (MIDDLE SCHOOL) from memory (only "Concert F (your G Major)"; "Please play your scales in order, from memory.") and TIMED: Hallway ("Memory · Timed", the sheet time × `mult` 1.25: the first timed level) · Audition Room ("Memory · Audition time", × 1.0 = the sheet time). TIME (levels 3–4): a time bar, amber in the last `LOW_TIME_S` 15 s, NO alarm; at 0 the scale being played may finish (the GMEA rule), then a calm "Time. Thank you." (`TIME_UP_IDLE_S` 12 s with nothing heard ends it too); scales not started score 0. Stars: 1 = every scale finished (in time on levels 3–4), 2 = + ≥ 90 % of the notes clean, 3 = every note clean. 3 ★ on level 4 = ALL-STATE READY, per section (`gameData.ready`: Middle School `ready[member]` = the date, where it always was; Concert Band `ready.cb[member]`, Symphonic Band `ready.sb[member]`; member ids never clash with cb / sb; game.js `readSec` reads without adding anything, `bySec` writes): a badge on the results, the level screen and its section card. MIDDLE SCHOOL's unlocks the avatar background Audition Room (`auditionroom`, scene `audition`; rule `{game: 'scale-trainer', level: 4, stars: 3, exact: true}`: `exact` = the plain 'scale-trainer' key only, `store.bestLevelStars(game, lvl, suffix, exact)`); CONCERT BAND's the "Concert Band Ready" name plate (plate `cbready`, rule `suffix: ':cb'`), SYMPHONIC BAND's the "Symphonic Band Ready" plate (`sbready`, `suffix: ':sb'`) (`.av-plate-cbready/-sbready` in theme.css, avatar-code.js TABLE). THE HIGH SCHOOL MEMORY RULE: the HS sheets say "Scales do not have to be memorized, but must be performed in the order printed", so Concert Band and Symphonic Band levels 3–4 KEEP THE MUSIC SHOWN (`LEVELS_HS`: "Music shown · Timed", "Music shown · Audition time"; "Please play your scales in order."); the optional FROM MEMORY switch (`#memSw` in `#auditionOpts`, cb / sb audition mode only, `gameData.fromMemory`) hides the staff on levels 3–4 (`memoryOk`; the cards say "From memory · Timed"; the adjudicator adds "from memory"): such a run adds "★ From memory" to its score sheet (`#memMark`), its best line and its level card (`gameData.memRuns.<cb|sb>[member][level]`); the same stars either way. Middle School is unchanged (levels 3–4 always from memory, no switch). Time: levels 1–2 no timer, level 3 = the section's sheet time × 1.25, level 4 = the sheet time. PROGRESS: Middle School under the plain key as always (`setLevel('scale-trainer', member id, level 1–4, {stars, best: % clean})`, no migration), Concert Band `scale-trainer:cb`, Symphonic Band `scale-trainer:sb` (same shape); `maxStars` 36 = 3 sections × 12 (`allStars` counts every `scale-trainer:…` key, so the floor, the leaderboard and the Prize Counter's star sources count them normally). CHROMATIC CHALLENGE: the section's chromatic up and down, a stopwatch, no limit; "Tongued or slurred, any rhythm you choose — the judges listen for evenness, accuracy and speed."; stars (1 finished, 2 = ≥ 2.5 clean notes a second + ≤ 2 misses, 3 = ≥ 4 a second + no misses: `CHROM_STARS`) and the best time in `gameData.chrom[member]` (cb / sb: `chrom.cb[member]`, `chrom.sb[member]`) (outside `games`, so they never count in `maxStars`).
- THE FINGERING CARD (game.js `Card`, `CARD` numbers; `.fc*` in style.css, tokens `--sa-tap`, `--sa-bar*`): tap ANY NOTE where a staff shows (Practice with every option, levels 1–2, the Chromatic Challenge; NOTE BY NOTE's note and the live dots under it) or a DOT on the results' score sheet (levels 3–4 hide the staff while playing: there only the score sheet): a small card next to the note with the written name in the scale's spelling ("F♯4" going up, "G♭4" coming down), the concert pitch small, the PRIMARY fingering drawn with shared/diagrams.js (Button Masher's Chart drawing, display only: no pressable keys) and the alternates in words like the Chart ("Also: 3"); trombone: its position ("4th position", alternates "or 6th"; the euphonium B.C. shows its valves); MALLETS: no fingering, a small SVG of the bars (`barsSVG`: about two octaves centered on the note, naturals below, accidentals raised, shorter as the pitch rises, like Chime Heist's kit; no other game's keyboard drawing is shared code) with that bar lit + "Mallets: the lit bar". A TAP picks the NEAREST note in the row it lands on, in a band at least 44 px tall (`CARD.hit`; the staff's notes are as wide as their spacing), on pointerdown with preventDefault + `Arcade.holdGuard` + `touch-action: manipulation`, so taps never move the page. The tapped note gets a ring (`.fc-ring`, a dot `.fc-on`); another note moves the card; the same note, a tap outside, Esc (window capture: before the pause menu) or ✕ closes it. KEYS: every staff note (`g.fc-note`) and score-sheet dot is focusable, one tab stop per staff / dot row (roving tabindex), Enter/Space opens (never the ?demo Space), ←/→ (↑/↓, Home/End) move with the card following. PLACEMENT (`place`): never over the tapped note or the current (blue) note, on screen: below/above the note's whole row first (its other notes stay tappable), else below/above/right/left of the note; redone on every judged note (`drawNow`), scroll and resize. IT NEVER GETS IN THE WAY: the microphone keeps listening, nothing pauses, restarts or is judged, NO SOUND (it would mute the mic); pausing closes it; NOTE BY NOTE keeps its own fingering, a tapped dot's card shows on top without changing the note it waits for. `Arcade.ScaleTrainer.card()` / `openCard(kind, i, run)` / `closeCard()` for tests.
- THE SCORE SHEET (the results' extra): per scale its name, ✓ finished / ✓ finished after time / ✗ time, clean notes out of the total, a row of note dots (green clean, amber fixed, red missed/skipped, dim not played) and the time used; then the total time against the limit.
