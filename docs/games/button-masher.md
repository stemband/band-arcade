# Button Masher (`button-masher/`)

- THE UI KIT: the kit pause freezes the match timer and every delayed step (taunt, ROUND, FIGHT!, counters, K.O.: a pausable `later()`); BACK TO RIVALS; results hero = your still fighter + the rival.
- Fingering / slide-position trainer as a neon versus fighting game. NO microphone (never load `pitch.js` or `mic-gate.js`); loads `sfx.js`. No NOTES × ORDER picker: its 8 rivals ARE the modes (first 3, first 5, the four concert scales from `Scales.build(member, id).up`, then all four mixed), all under the plain key `button-masher`.
- Uses the saved player (`requireInstrument` + `Arcade.currentMember()`). THE FIGHTER IS THE STUDENT'S AVATAR (`Arcade.AvatarFight.mount`, as tall as the old fighter, feet on the arena floor) holding their instrument: idle → FIGHT-IDLE, a right combo → STRIKE + the sound-wave blast from the instrument to the rival (`FX.mouth()`), a counter after a timeout → HIT, after a wrong combo → DIZZY, the counter that empties the energy → KO, the rival K.O.'d → VICTORY, time over → BOW; the results show a still VICTORY/BOW (`stillHTML`); the pet cheers combos and worries at counters. Pose timing only: the rules, damage and timers are unchanged. The HUD shows the avatar's chip + its name (`#youName`, `data-av-name`). NO AVATAR SAVED YET (`!store.avatar`): the generic fighter (`fighterSVG`, unchanged) + a "Make your avatar!" button (`AvatarBadge.edit`; saving switches to the avatar at once), the instrument's portrait and "You" in the HUD (nothing in the game makes an avatar by itself). The rival screen draws the fight poses in the background (cached). Progress is keyed by MEMBER: `setLevel('button-masher', member.id, rival 1–8, {stars, best})` (`byMember: true` in games.js). Percussion (`bells`) gets "Percussion: try Chime Heist!" instead of a diagram, in the game and on the floor (`noPlay`).
- `shared/fingerings.js` is THE fingering table (moved out of button-masher/: Button Masher, Arcade Quest's FINGERING challenge, Scale Trainer's fingering card + NOTE BY NOTE load it; Music Highway loads it only for its octave fit, and filling the table moved no song: all 416 song × instrument octaves were compared before/after). It covers EVERY NOTE of every member's GMEA chromatic range (instruments.js `chromatic`, bells excepted) AND of the high school ranges (scales.js `CHROMATIC_HS`) (tests/scale-trainer.spec.js checks both); the notes added for Scale Trainer are listed under "ADDED FOR SCALE TRAINER, PLEASE CHECK" and "ADDED FOR SCALE TRAINER HS, PLEASE CHECK" in its top comment (filling the HS ranges moved no Music Highway song: all 736 song × instrument maps compared before/after). Button Masher and Arcade Quest still draw only from their own note pools (`MASHER_FINGERINGS[memberId] = {diagram, notes: {'Bb4': [primary, alternates…]}}`): brass `'1-3'`, `'0'`, horn trigger `'T1-2'`, 4th valve `'4'`; trombone positions `[1, 6]`; woodwinds = the diagram's key ids separated by spaces (`'|'` ignored, `'1h'` = half-hole). A combo is right only when the pressed keys EXACTLY match one entry. To fix a fingering, edit that list (primary first); every note used by rivals 1–8 must be listed for every member (a missing note or unknown key id warns in the console). Its top comment lists the fingerings to verify against the 6th Grade Honor Band charts; keep that list current.
- `shared/diagrams.js` (+ `shared/diagrams.css`; `Arcade.Masher` = `Arcade.Fingerings`) draws each family (`flute`, `oboe`, `clarinet`, `sax`, `bassoon`, `trumpet`, `horn`, `euph`, `tuba`, `trombone`) as SVG with real tappable keys (pointerdown). Key ids are what fingerings.js uses: move keys freely, never rename ids; a note that needs a key the drawing lacks gets a NEW id (added for the full ranges: flute `C#` `C` footjoint rollers, oboe `RC#` `RC` and (high school) `LB` `LBb` (low B / B♭, left pinky), clarinet `Sl` = the C♯/G♯ sliver key, bassoon `LBb` `LB` `LC` `LD` low keys on the left thumb). tests/diagrams.spec.js checks that no key or caption on any diagram overlaps another or leaves the picture. Half-hole keys (`half: true`) cycle open → half → closed. Clarinet (bass clarinet too) and sax: the LEFT pinky keys sit ABOVE the body (between the throat / palm keys and the side keys, over the gap between fingers 3 and 4, their label above them), the right pinky keys below (tests/diagrams.spec.js).
- Rivals in `levels.js` (`MASHER_RIVALS`: name, look, colors, taunt, pool, notes, health, time, showName, hint); rules in `MASHER_RULES`. `MASHER_RULES.timeByFamily` multiplies every rival's `time` by the player's family (woodwind 1.5, brass 1: woodwinds have many more keys to find); the rival cards and the match timer both use `timeFor(V)`, never `V.time` directly. Stars: 3 = no mistakes (wrong or timeout), 2 = 1–2, 1 = won. Cartoon only: energy blasts, a "boing" counter, dizzy stars; no weapons or injury. The fighter and the rivals are original drawings in `game.js`.
- CHART view (rival screen only, never during a match) shows every note with its primary fingering drawn and alternates listed. The `#chart` overlay itself scrolls (wheel, trackpad, touch, and arrows/Page Up/Down/Space: it gets focus on open); its header (the instrument's name + a big CLOSE) is `position: sticky`; Esc closes; `A.lockScroll(true/false)` holds the page behind still and restores it exactly. Bassoon key 1 is a half-hole key: tap once = half, twice = closed. `A.Masher.game()` = the match in progress (tests).
