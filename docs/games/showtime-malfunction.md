# Showtime Malfunction (`showtime-malfunction/`)

- THE UI KIT: the kit pause uses its own flag `G.held` (never the special card's `G.paused`); scare, walk-home and fizzle timers run on a pausable show clock; the spooky level is also registered in the Settings panel; the Jump Scare warning is `UI.confirm` (with the "Visual scares only" checkbox).
- Articulation game, LISTENS (pitch.js + mic-gate.js + sfx.js): glitching animatronics lurch toward the student in 2–3 lanes (pseudo-3D DOM: translate + scale from the feet, stop-motion lurch steps every `lurchMs`); each shows a VOICE BOX (the item's `show` on the student's clef with `sig`, and × N). The target = the closest walking one. THE TARGET PANEL (`#tpanel`, `drawPanel`) always shows the target's note BIG (one note on the student's clef with `sig`; the viewBox fits the whole pool so the staff never jumps, except Chromatic, where each note gets its own box; no note name) and "× N" in huge numbers, redrawn at once when the target or the Maestro's phase changes (a short flash/slide; a color fade under reduced motion); snare = a big drum icon + count. SNARE players face the same band, special machines and Maestro Moose as everyone else (only the counting differs: hits; `howSnare` lines and the Lurker's roll). It sits BESIDE the arena in landscape and BELOW it in portrait and on phones (≤ 760 px), outside the arena, so static, vignette, emergency light and lean-in never cover or dim it; the arena shrinks to make room. The target gets a yellow outline, floor ring and a dashed tether to the panel's side; the next one's sign says NEXT. Signs are previews: tight single-note staffs that never shrink below 0.8× (0.7 on phones), slide down instead of leaving the top of the arena, dark notation on a light card, glitches only on the frame (`.sign::before`). Every staff in this game has thicker lines, stems, ledger lines and outlined note heads (style.css). `Pitch.onAttack`: pc === item.pc counts one down (snare: any attack); wrong pc = a `sour` glitch; a note held > `holdHintMs` with no attack = "Tongue each note!". 0 = reboot (eyes blue `.fixed`, walks back, joins `#band` on the stage). THE LINEUP (`lineup(box, {maxH})` in game.js, `LINEUP`): the stage band and the results band always fit, however many (up to 10 + 20 Split Sprocket minis + the Maestro): overlapping, smaller and up to 4 staggered rows (the front row on top), Maestro Moose in the middle of the front row; redone on resize. The results show it BELOW the stars (`onShow`, `#resBand`, at most `resBandMax()` tall). Reaching the front = a spotlight out; 3 out = SHOWTIME'S OVER (0 stars). Stars 3/2/1 = spotlights lost 0/1/2. The band freezes while `Pitch.isSuppressed()`.
- `levels.js` = `SHOWTIMES` (bots, count, snare counts, atOnce, lanes, walk s, pool, boss {count, phases, snare, walk}) + `SHOWTIME_RULES`. Showtime 8 = Maestro Moose in phases (a new note each phase, staggers back). Notes ONLY from `ModePicker.sequence` (count = bots + boss phases).
- THE SNARE DRUM'S JOBS (`showtime-malfunction/snare.js` = `Arcade.ShowtimeSnare`, created by game.js ONLY when `snare`; every number in levels.js `SNARE_RULES`, one commented table, each feature a `from` showtime + per-showtime arrays; wind players never touch any of it, and their rules and tests are unchanged). THE MICROPHONE HEARS ONLY WHEN A HIT LANDS AND HOW LOUD IT IS: it can't hear which hand played, so NEVER judge sticking. Hits = `Arcade.Onsets.listen` (never pitch; `onAttack` is ignored for the snare), each hit's time minus the device's saved delay (`Calibration.lag('clap')`: Rhythm Dojo's timing check, shared; the snare card's "Check your timing" runs the same `TimingCheck` flow). THE SNARE CLOCK `H.clk` = real time added up only while nothing holds the show (suppression, PAUSE `G.held`, a card `G.paused`, a scare, a hidden tab: `still()`); any still frame during a rhythm/accent/tempo job restarts it from its count-in. A still frame sets `stillNow`, which clears only on the next moving frame: until then a hit counts for nothing (and a timed job restarts on that frame). `Arcade.Showtime.snare()` (tests) reports `stillNow`, `trail` (the last 20 'still' / 'go' moments, perf ms) and `hits` (the last 12 recorded hits: game time, perf ms, level), and tests/showtime-snare.spec.js waits for `!stillNow` (its `quiet()` and `freshCountIn()`) before hitting or planning a measure: a loaded WebKit can leave 400+ ms between frames, and a measure planned while the band stood still moved ~0.1 s when the job restarted. THE SILENT BEAT: no clicks or metronome during play; the stage and the target panel's border swell on each beat (`--pulse`, ≤ `beat.maxSwells` 2.2 a second, steady under reduced motion) + beat dots on the panel. JOBS (`job()` per machine, shown on the panel and the voice box: rhythms with `RhythmStaff.engrave` + Mr. Graham's Counting, p / f bold italic, accents `>`): EXACT COUNTS (every showtime): at 0 a machine waits `exact.confirmMs` 350 (CONFIRM; the panel says "…and STOP" at 1); a hit then = an OVER-HIT (+`overhitAdd` 2, a sour glitch, "Too many! Count your hits and stop."), silence = reboot; then the next target listens only after `gapMs` 400 of silence. FREEZE (from 2): on a `freeze.chance` share of count machines Maestro Moose's hand (✋, `#stHand`) rises partway (`at`) for `freeze.beats` beats: every machine walks at `walkMul` .5, a hit = +`penalty` 2 ("Freeze means FREEZE!"), "GO!" after (visual); never within `guard` 2 s of the front. RHYTHM MACHINES (from 3): `rhythm.share` of machines ask for `rhythm.measures` measures of a rhythm from `SNARE_RHYTHMS` (a filter over Rhythm Dojo's 4/4 `RD_LEVELS` cells, growing by showtime: 3 quarters/eighths … 8 everything incl. syncopation; half of each draw from the showtime's new rhythms) at `rhythm.bpm` (× `nightmareBpm` 1.1), after a silent one-measure count-in (big beat numbers + dots); judged by `RhythmJudge` with `rhythm.window` ms (rests included: a hit in a rest or an extra fails); pass = the measure turns green, fail = a glitch + the same rhythm again with early/late/extra ticks on the staff. SOFT AND LOUD (from 5, needs THE SOUNDCHECK: 4 soft + 4 loud hits, the split halfway on a log scale, `gameData.snareDyn` `{soft, loud, split, at}`; a pair under `dyn.minRatio` apart = "Make the soft hits softer and the loud hits louder" and again; runs before the first showtime ≥ 5 and on "Redo soundcheck"): p machines count only soft hits (loud = +1, "Softer!"), f only loud ("Louder!", nothing counted); ACCENT machines (from 6): eighths from `accent.patterns`, accented hits loud, the rest soft ("Accents louder!" / "Keep the others soft!"). ROLLS: the Lurker's roll must be fast enough AND even (the gaps' CV ≤ `roll.maxCv` .35, NIGHTMARE .25; clumpy = fills × `clumpyMul` .3, "Smooth it out: even hits!"); NIGHTMARE's Glitch Lurker = a CRESCENDO roll (each third of the ring louder than the last by `crescK`; the ring shows a hairpin; no mid-roll glitch). THE TEMPO-LOCK MAESTRO (NIGHTMARE, showtime 8): each phase "Match my tempo!" at `maestro.bpm` (84/96/108): steady eighths for `beats` beats, a hit within `window` ms of the grid fills it, the average drift over the last beat drains it ("You're rushing!" / "You're dragging!"); on Normal the snare Maestro keeps its exact count. FAIRNESS: `fair()` counts every requirement (measures × beats, count-in, freeze beats, roll time) and slows a machine until its job fits in `fair.margin` of its walk. NEW DRUM CHALLENGE cards (`#specialCard.drum`, one sentence + a tiny animated example; `gameData.files['drum-*']`, the first time a device meets each; a card already open for a special machine gets it as one more line), the snare card lists what each showtime adds, the Malfunction Files have DRUM CHALLENGES (`#drumFilesSec`).
