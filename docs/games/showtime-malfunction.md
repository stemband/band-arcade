# Showtime Malfunction (`showtime-malfunction/`)

- THE UI KIT: the kit pause uses its own flag `G.held` (never the special card's `G.paused`); scare, walk-home and fizzle timers run on a pausable show clock; the spooky level is also registered in the Settings panel; the Jump Scare warning is `UI.confirm` (with the "Visual scares only" checkbox).
- Articulation game, LISTENS (pitch.js + mic-gate.js + sfx.js): glitching animatronics lurch toward the student in 2–3 lanes (pseudo-3D DOM: translate + scale from the feet, stop-motion lurch steps every `lurchMs`); each shows a VOICE BOX (the item's `show` on the student's clef with `sig`, and × N). The target = the closest walking one. THE TARGET PANEL (`#tpanel`, `drawPanel`) always shows the target's note BIG (one note on the student's clef with `sig`; the viewBox fits the whole pool so the staff never jumps, except Chromatic, where each note gets its own box; no note name) and "× N" in huge numbers, redrawn at once when the target or the Maestro's phase changes (a short flash/slide; a color fade under reduced motion); snare = a big drum icon + count. SNARE players face the same band, special machines and Maestro Moose as everyone else (only the counting differs: hits; `howSnare` lines and the Lurker's roll). It sits BESIDE the arena in landscape and BELOW it in portrait and on phones (≤ 760 px), outside the arena, so static, vignette, emergency light and lean-in never cover or dim it; the arena shrinks to make room. The target gets a yellow outline, floor ring and a dashed tether to the panel's side; the next one's sign says NEXT. Signs are previews: tight single-note staffs that never shrink below 0.8× (0.7 on phones), slide down instead of leaving the top of the arena, dark notation on a light card, glitches only on the frame (`.sign::before`). Every staff in this game has thicker lines, stems, ledger lines and outlined note heads (style.css). `Pitch.onAttack`: pc === item.pc counts one down (snare: any attack); wrong pc = a `sour` glitch; a note held > `holdHintMs` with no attack = "Tongue each note!". 0 = reboot (eyes blue `.fixed`, walks back, joins `#band` on the stage). THE LINEUP (`lineup(box, {maxH})` in game.js, `LINEUP`): the stage band and the results band always fit, however many (up to 10 + 20 Split Sprocket minis + the Maestro): overlapping, smaller and up to 4 staggered rows (the front row on top), Maestro Moose in the middle of the front row; redone on resize. The results show it BELOW the stars (`onShow`, `#resBand`, at most `resBandMax()` tall). Reaching the front = a spotlight out; 3 out = SHOWTIME'S OVER (0 stars). Stars 3/2/1 = spotlights lost 0/1/2. The band freezes while `Pitch.isSuppressed()`.
- `levels.js` = `SHOWTIMES` (bots, count, snare counts, atOnce, lanes, walk s, pool, boss {count, phases, snare, walk}) + `SHOWTIME_RULES`. Showtime 8 = Maestro Moose in phases (a new note each phase, staggers back). Notes ONLY from `ModePicker.sequence` (count = bots + boss phases).
- THE SNARE DRUM'S JOBS (`showtime-malfunction/snare.js` = `Arcade.ShowtimeSnare`, created by game.js ONLY when `snare`; every number in levels.js `SNARE_RULES`, one commented table, each feature a `from` showtime + per-showtime arrays; wind players never touch any of it, and their rules and tests are unchanged). THE MICROPHONE HEARS ONLY WHEN A HIT LANDS AND HOW LOUD IT IS: it can't hear which hand played, so NEVER judge sticking. Hits = `Arcade.Onsets.listen` (never pitch; `onAttack` is ignored for the snare), each hit's time minus the device's saved delay (`Calibration.lag('clap')`: Rhythm Dojo's timing check, shared; the snare card's "Check your timing" runs the same `TimingCheck` flow). THE SNARE CLOCK `H.clk` = real time added up only while nothing holds the show (suppression, PAUSE `G.held`, a card `G.paused`, a scare, a hidden tab: `still()`); any still frame during a rhythm/accent/tempo job restarts it from its count-in. THE SILENT BEAT: no clicks or metronome during play; the stage and the target panel's border swell on each beat (`--pulse`, ≤ `beat.maxSwells` 2.2 a second, steady under reduced motion) + beat dots on the panel. JOBS (`job()` per machine, shown on the panel and the voice box: rhythms with `RhythmStaff.engrave` + Mr. Graham's Counting, p / f bold italic, accents `>`): EXACT COUNTS (every showtime): at 0 a machine waits `exact.confirmMs` 350 (CONFIRM; the panel says "…and STOP" at 1); a hit then = an OVER-HIT (+`overhitAdd` 2, a sour glitch, "Too many! Count your hits and stop."), silence = reboot; then the next target listens only after `gapMs` 400 of silence. FREEZE (from 2): on a `freeze.chance` share of count machines Maestro Moose's hand (✋, `#stHand`) rises partway (`at`) for `freeze.beats` beats: every machine walks at `walkMul` .5, a hit = +`penalty` 2 ("Freeze means FREEZE!"), "GO!" after (visual); never within `guard` 2 s of the front. RHYTHM MACHINES (from 3): `rhythm.share` of machines ask for `rhythm.measures` measures of a rhythm from `SNARE_RHYTHMS` (a filter over Rhythm Dojo's 4/4 `RD_LEVELS` cells, growing by showtime: 3 quarters/eighths … 8 everything incl. syncopation; half of each draw from the showtime's new rhythms) at `rhythm.bpm` (× `nightmareBpm` 1.1), after a silent one-measure count-in (big beat numbers + dots); judged by `RhythmJudge` with `rhythm.window` ms (rests included: a hit in a rest or an extra fails); pass = the measure turns green, fail = a glitch + the same rhythm again with early/late/extra ticks on the staff. SOFT AND LOUD (from 5, needs THE SOUNDCHECK: 4 soft + 4 loud hits, the split halfway on a log scale, `gameData.snareDyn` `{soft, loud, split, at}`; a pair under `dyn.minRatio` apart = "Make the soft hits softer and the loud hits louder" and again; runs before the first showtime ≥ 5 and on "Redo soundcheck"): p machines count only soft hits (loud = +1, "Softer!"), f only loud ("Louder!", nothing counted); ACCENT machines (from 6): eighths from `accent.patterns`, accented hits loud, the rest soft ("Accents louder!" / "Keep the others soft!"). ROLLS: the Lurker's roll must be fast enough AND even (the gaps' CV ≤ `roll.maxCv` .35, NIGHTMARE .25; clumpy = fills × `clumpyMul` .3, "Smooth it out: even hits!"); NIGHTMARE's Glitch Lurker = a CRESCENDO roll (each third of the ring louder than the last by `crescK`; the ring shows a hairpin; no mid-roll glitch). THE TEMPO-LOCK MAESTRO (NIGHTMARE, showtime 8): each phase "Match my tempo!" at `maestro.bpm` (84/96/108): steady eighths for `beats` beats, a hit within `window` ms of the grid fills it, the average drift over the last beat drains it ("You're rushing!" / "You're dragging!"); on Normal the snare Maestro keeps its exact count. FAIRNESS: `fair()` counts every requirement (measures × beats, count-in, freeze beats, roll time) and slows a machine until its job fits in `fair.margin` of its walk. NEW DRUM CHALLENGE cards (`#specialCard.drum`, one sentence + a tiny animated example; `gameData.files['drum-*']`, the first time a device meets each; a card already open for a special machine gets it as one more line), the snare card lists what each showtime adds, the Malfunction Files have DRUM CHALLENGES (`#drumFilesSec`). Stars and progress keys unchanged. `?demo&snarejob=count|p|f|rhythm|accent|roll|tempo|freeze` forces a job; Space = a hit at the level the job wants. `Arcade.Showtime.snare()` / `snarePlan()` / `snareJob()` / `soundcheck()` / `timingCheck()` for tests: tests/showtime-snare.spec.js.
- Characters are original (characters.js: Tubby Tusk walrus/tuba, Professor Hoot owl/flute, Snapjaw Sal gator/snare, Rico Bandit raccoon/sax, Maestro Moose conductor). NEVER: bears, rabbits, chickens, foxes, a pizzeria, nights/clock hours 12–6 AM, security cameras, doors or power meters, or anyone trapped inside. They are just malfunctioning machines. Colors = `--anim-*` tokens.
- Progress per MEMBER (`byMember`): `setLevel(picker.state.progressKey, member.id, lv, …)`; snare = `'showtime-malfunction:count'` under `snare`. `gameData`: `{spooky: 'mild'|'spooky'|'jump', jumpDay, scareVisual, files: {<special id>: {seen, beaten}}, storySeen, diff: 'normal'|'extra', extraVisuals, extraSeen: {member: true}}`.
- DIFFICULTY: NORMAL | NIGHTMARE (`.diff-set` toggle, remembered). Normal = levels.js as written (never change it for NIGHTMARE); NIGHTMARE = each row's `x` column (`count`, `snare` ≤ 16, `speed` walk multiplier, `blurb`, `boss: {count, snare}`) merged by `rowFor(lv)`; same bots, lanes, atOnce, pool. Locked until THIS member has ≥ 1 ★ on The 5:00 Show on Normal in any mode (`?demo`: open); the first unlock shows `#resUnlock` + plays `nightmare-unlocked`. Saves under the Normal key + `':extra'` (`showtime-malfunction:extra`, `…:scale-Eb:extra`, `…:count:extra`); the picker reads them via `ModePicker.mount({keySuffix, max: 24})`. `allStars` counts every `<game>:…` key, so NIGHTMARE stars are in the totals (games.js `maxStars` 48 = 24 + 24). Difficulty and SPOOKY LEVEL are separate: the first switch to NIGHTMARE sets Spooky once; Mild still works and only changes visuals. `body.extra-mode` + `spooky-mode` = redder emergency light (`.emergency`, slow pulse), more cabinet static, eyes flicker more; Mild + EXTRA looks like Mild. Mild = glitch/static only; Spooky = darker + a silent lean-in at game over. Flicker always slow and gentle, never rapid or full-screen; reduced motion = none (and no twitch/lean-in).
- Sounds (sounds.js, screen `showtime-malfunction`): `showtime-start` (falls back to level-start), `attack-tick` (`echo: 60`, keep < 0.1 s so fast tonguing counts), `reboot`, `spotlight-out`, `showtime-over`, `nightmare-unlocked` (results, first unlock; falls back to `skin-unlocked`); results use the shared events. No music or ambience during play. Achievements: defeat Maestro Moose (level 8) → the `animatronic` skin; on NIGHTMARE → `nightmare` (Nightmare Animatronic: unlock rule `suffix: ':extra'`). Specials: `special-alert`, `special-intro`, `special-<id>` (8, fallback `special-alert`); Jump Scare: `scare-sting-1/-2/-3` (`cap: 3`). All unpitched noise.
- SPECIAL MACHINES (levels.js `SHOWTIME_SPECIALS`, one commented table: `chance` per showtime (0 in 1–2, .15 in 3–4, .25 in 5–7, .30 in 8), `nightmare` +.10, per machine `speed`/`count`/`snare`/`points` + its own settings; characters.js draws them, `SPECIAL_IDS`, tokens `--anim-tin`…`--anim-ollie`): an animatronic walking on (`specialize` in game.js, never the boss) may become one; it REPLACES that regular one (same note from `ModePicker.sequence`; extra notes for the Duet Dolls / the Jester / the minis come from the same result's `pool`), never more than one special on the floor at once (Split Sprocket's minis excepted). Turbo Tin (2× speed, 1–2 plays), Tuba Tank (0.6×, 2× plays, `.a-plate`s pop off per counted play), Long Tone Lurker (attacks don't count; the note held steadily fills a ring over `hold[lv]` s, draining at `drain` when it stops/changes; its "Hold it! One long, steady note." replaces the tongue hint; SNARE: a roll of ≥ `rollRate` hits/s for `roll[lv]` s), Duet Dolls (two different notes in turns A, B, A…; the other doll's note does nothing; snare = a normal count), Glitch Jester (after `switchAt` of its plays its note changes, a slow 0.7 s skew/fade; snare = normal), Split Sprocket (rebooted = two minis in the adjacent lanes, 1.5× speed, 1 play each; `G.total` += 2), Blackout Bot (voice box + panel dark until z ≥ `revealAt`, then a fade, no flicker), Oil Can Ollie (every `every` s +1 play on the nearest other walking machine, max `maxAdd` each, oil-drop icons; not the Lurker). A special's FIRST appearance on a device (`gameData.files[id].seen`) pauses the showtime (`G.paused`: the band, `G.t` and scares stop; attacks ignored) for the "NEW MALFUNCTION DETECTED!" card (`#specialCard`, `special-alert` + `special-intro`); the card (`.overlay.special-ov`, z-index 700, the arena `isolation: isolate`) is always above every animatronic; later ones play `special-<id>` (fallback `special-alert`). Rebooting one = `points.special` + its `points` (score only: stars are unchanged) and `files[id].beaten++`. The MALFUNCTION FILES (`#files`, the level screen's button with "n/8"): a silhouette until met, "Spotted!" once met, the whole card (how to beat it, times rebooted) once rebooted; saved in gameData (so in the Arcade Backup Code). `?demo&special=<id or part of it>` makes every animatronic that may be special that one, from Showtime 1 (tests). HYBRIDS (`hybrid: [A, B]` entries in `SHOWTIME_SPECIALS.machines`, ids never renamed): Glitch Lurker (hold; halfway the note glitches: switch and keep holding, the ring keeps its fill; snare: the roll speeds up from 6 to 8 hits a second halfway), Blackout Jester (dark until 40 %, then a glitch; snare: a count), Sprocket Dolls (a duet pair that splits into two minis, each keeping one doll's note; snare: two 1-hit minis), Oil Tank (1.5× plays, 4 plates popped by its first plays; it oils the others only while a plate is left), Turbo Blackout (fast, 1–2 plays, dark until a third of the way), Duet Tank (two notes in turns, 1.5× plays, a plate per play). A special's `traits` = its parents (a plain special: its own id): every trick's code asks `has(b, '<parent>')`, `get(id, parent, key)` = the hybrid's own setting else the parent's, so a hybrid runs both parents' code paths. `hybrids` in levels.js: `from` 5, `share` by showtime (0.3 at 5–6, 0.4 at 7–8) of the special chance, NIGHTMARE too; one special on the floor at a time, so never two hybrids. THE FAIRNESS CHECK (`fair()`, `hybrids.fair`): a hybrid is slowed until its plays ÷ `rate` (snare `snareRate`) + its hold + `glitch` fit in `margin` of the time its note shows. Drawn from both parents (characters.js HYBRIDS: A's head on B's body, both felts, `.a-stitch` seams, an odd right eye from the other parent, the trick's parts); the card adds "HYBRID!" + both parents' names (`#spHybrid`); the Malfunction Files have a HYBRIDS section ("n/14"); a hybrid's walk-on sound is its first parent's `special-<id>`. `Arcade.Showtime.choose()` / `specialize(spec)` for tests (tests/showtime-hybrids.spec.js).
- JUMP SCARE (the third SPOOKY LEVEL, `data-spooky="jump"`): hidden unless `shared/teacher-settings.js` `Arcade.TEACHER.JUMP_SCARE_ALLOWED` (a device that had it plays Spooky); turning it on ALWAYS asks first (`#jumpWarn`: "This mode has sudden jump scares with loud sounds. Are you sure?" YES / NO + "Visual scares only (no scare sounds)" = `gameData.scareVisual`); never the default; `jumpDay` = the day it was turned on: on any other day (`store.today()`) it is back to Spooky (`drawSpooky`, at load, on the hub and at each showtime start). It has every Spooky visual (`body.spooky-mode` + `jump-mode`) + 1 scare a showtime, 2 on the longer ones (≥ `longFrom` 7 animatronics, or the boss) (levels.js `SHOWTIME_SCARES`: planned by PROGRESS, not seconds: `at` = the share of the show's animatronics that have walked on (short [.4], long [.25, .55]; `planScares`/`armScares`, fired `delay` 1 s after), so a quick player gets them too; never before `notBefore` 6 s, ≥ `apart` 6 s apart, postponed while the Maestro is within `bossGuard` 5 s of the front or has ≤ 2 plays left in a phase; skipped at the very end of a show (nothing left to come and the last ones near the front)). The Settings panel says "Jump Scare resets to Spooky each new day." under the spooky buttons (only when it's allowed). A scare (`scare()`, `#scare` over the arena, ~1.3 s + a 0.7 s beat): LUNGE (dark, a machine rushes out), EYES (lights cut to black, two eyes, then the lunge), POPUP (a head from the bottom edge), BAND (the stage band's heads snap toward you, the stage leans in; only with someone on the stage); reduced motion = FADE (darkness + eyes, nothing moves). During it `G.scare` freezes everything, `Pitch.suppress` ignores the mic, it never costs a spotlight. Only darkness, motion and scale: no bright or white flash, no strobe, no shake (measured: at most one light change per spot per scare). Sound: one of `scare-sting-1/-2/-3` (unless visual only), sounds.js `cap: 3` = THE LOUDNESS CAP (sfx.js: peak and loudest 50 ms never > 3 dB over the arcade's normal loudest effect, a file scaled + a hard limiter; verified with a deliberately over-loud file: +3.0 dB). `?demo&scare=lunge|eyes|popup|band` (with Jump Scare on) = that scare 3 s in; `Arcade.Showtime.scare(kind)` for tests.
