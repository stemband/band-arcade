# Keys to the City (`keys-to-the-city/`)

- THE UI KIT: the intro is `UI.intro` (the Mayor, the mini keyboard, LET'S GO! / CITY MAP); the kit pause freezes the round clock (`G.t0` shifted) and the wait between rounds (`later()`); BACK TO THE CITY MAP ends a Night Shift with keys right as SHIFT OVER; results hero = the golden key; the finale shows over the results (z 65) and announces after its button.
- Piano-keyboard note reading in a neon city at night (zone note-reading). The keyboard is the SKYLINE: a real piano layout (white keys, black keys in groups of 2 and 3, real width ratios, black keys `BHK` 0.63 of the white length, off-center in their groups like a real piano; on short screens the keys get SHORTER, never narrower than `RULES.minKeyPx` 46 px), black keys drawn as skyscrapers with windows and a rooftop, white keys as lit streets. A key being asked about switches its windows on (`.lit`); a right answer lights its block gently (`lightBlock`: the C–E block or the F–B block, one soft fade); wrong = coral outline, the right key gold. (No Main St lamp any more: nothing marks middle C.) C/F PLATES (levels.js `labels`, like `signs`: 1, [from, to] or none): a small dark sign with "C" / "F" in the Chopsticks' / the Fork's color at the bottom of every C and F key (`.kt-cf`, `setLabels`): Downtown C on, Restaurant Row fading to 0 with the signs, none from Sharp Street on; SHOW SIGNS brings the plates back too. The keyboard covers every clef the district uses and PANS (0.45 s, none under reduced motion) to the asked keys; FIND THE KEY pans to a random spot so the key's position never gives it away.
- THE BLACK-KEY GROUPS HAVE EXACTLY TWO NAMES, EVERYWHERE (hints, the Mayor, help text, docs): THE CHOPSTICKS (2 black keys; "C is right next to the Chopsticks") and THE FORK (3 black keys; "F is right next to the Fork"). Neon line-drawing signs (`signSVG`) over EVERY group on the keyboard, bright on early districts, fading by `signs` ([from, to] across a district), SHOW SIGNS brings them back for one round (−`signsCost` points). TAP A SIGN (`signTap`): while a sign is visible (brightness ≥ `RULES.signTapFrom` .2: the `.tap` class; a big invisible `.sg-hit` area in the sky over its group that stops above the black keys) a tap plays its hint `kttc-mayor-chopsticks` / `-fork` (mic: true, so instrument mode mutes the detector while it speaks) and the Mayor's bubble shows the words; never an answer, never costs points; the same hint never restarts or stacks, the other sign's hint stops it (`Sfx.hush`); the wrong-answer sign hints go through the same `hintVoice`. THE LEVEL INTRO shows the MINI KEYBOARD (`miniKeyboard()`: one octave C–C, the two signs over their groups with dashed arrows to C and F, lit in the signs' colors, "C is right next to the Chopsticks" / "F is right next to the Fork") on every district with signs (not The Mayor's Challenge). Never any other name for them, and no cultural fonts or imagery.
- `levels.js` = `KTTC_LEVELS` (the CITY MAP, 9 districts: Downtown C, Restaurant Row, Sharp Street, Flat Iron District, Ledger Line Heights, Bass Clef Harbor, Key Signature Square, Sharp Signature Summit, The Mayor's Challenge; saved by number: never reorder), `KTTC_RULES` (stars 70/85/100 % of rounds right, points, timings, `minKeyPx`), `KTTC_NIGHT` (the endless). TIMED ROUNDS (The Mayor's Challenge, Night Shift): base time + `RULES.perTap` 1.5 s for every tap after the first (a black key's name, FULL CIRCUIT's placing: `tapsFor`); SCALE BUILDER is never timed (only Key Signature Square and Sharp Signature Summit). THE QUESTION STAFF (game.js `staffLayout` / `drawStaff` / `sizeStaff`): laid out from the DISTRICT, never the round's note (so nothing jumps): width = the district's widest key signature + the open space (`OPEN`, `SCALE_OPEN` for Scale Builder), height = the district's range in that clef (stems, ledger lines, a ♯ under the lowest note, the clef; Scale Builder up to a 4th above the range) with even padding (`VPAD`); the note sits in the middle of the open space after the clef and key signature (`noteX`: a ♯/♭ counts as part of it), and Full Circuit's placing note uses the same x. Sized in px as large as the width, `STAFF_MAXH` (27 % of the screen height) and the room above the keyboard at its smallest (`KB_MIN`) allow, never smaller than the old staff (`OLD_MAXH`, `oldHeight`); the white box hugs the drawing and the space under it (`#stage` margin) makes up the district's tallest layout, so the keyboard never moves; a round that doesn't use the letter pad (Find the Key, Scale Builder) lends the pad's space to the staff (`STAFF_MAXH_LENT`, the pad `display: none`). The pad is reserved in the shape the district uses (♭ ♮ ♯ only where black keys or key signatures come). The key-signature hint text (`#keyName`) sits at the top of the box. shared/ui.js `staffSVG` takes `box: [top, height]` and `capY` for this. `quiz.js` (`Arcade.KTTC`, testable alone) = the music model (concert = piano) and the round maker: FIND THE KEY (octave-exact), NAME THE KEY (AnswerPad), FULL CIRCUIT (name it, then place it on the staff: a finger moves a note over the lines and spaces, letting go places it, ledger lines drawn), SCALE BUILDER (8 keys up from the tonic, in order). SPELLING (`nameOk`): no key signature = a white key's plain letter, a black key either name unless the district says `sharps` / `flats` (`flats-any` = flats, then either); with a key signature (F, B♭, E♭, A♭, G, D, A) only the key's own spelling, and staff notes mean the altered key (a B in F major is B♭); `sigHint` rounds light the key signature's ♯/♭ that applies. Early districts' wrong answers show the sign hint (`signHint`).
- TOUCH mode (default, no instrument, no microphone, `player: 'all'`): taps play `Sfx.piano`. INSTRUMENT mode (the level screen's toggle; needs a pitched saved instrument, else the floor's pick mode `index.html?pick=keys-to-the-city`): FIND THE KEY is answered by playing the concert pitch (`Pitch.onHeld`, any octave); `helper` shows "Piano B♭ = your C" (the member's written note, `sounds`); NO piano or other pitched sound plays (every effect is unpitched noise, mic: true, short). Name/circuit/scale rounds stay touch. Stars count in both modes: `setLevel('keys-to-the-city', 'all', district, {stars, best})`, the best kept. Clef choice Treble / Bass / Both (default: the saved instrument's clef) for districts with `clefs: 'pref'`.
- NIGHT SHIFT (the Endless tile): stages every `every` right answers add keys, clefs, key signatures, less time per round (`time`), 3 lives, Top 5 per device (`instKey 'all'`, `setKey 'night-shift'`; not saved in `?demo`). THE MAYOR: an original cat in a top hat and sash (`mayorSVG`, `--kt-fur*` tokens); gives a golden key for each district (the icon on its card), and clearing The Mayor's Challenge sets `gameData.achievements.mayor` = the Golden City Key hand item + the City Skyline background (bg scene `keys-city`) + the KEYS TO THE CITY finale (`kttc-keys-to-city`).
- Sounds (screen `keys-to-the-city`, all unpitched): `keys-to-the-city-menu`, `kttc-window-on`, `kttc-correct`, `kttc-wrong`, `kttc-block-lights` (every 5 in a row), `kttc-golden-key`, `kttc-keys-to-city`, optional voice slots `kttc-mayor-hello` (silent until recorded, never while listening) and `kttc-mayor-chopsticks/-fork` (the sign hints; they may play while listening). Cabinet `shape: 'keys'` (a skyline over the marquee, the two signs painted on the sides, a real little keyboard on the kick plate), `screen: 'keys'`, marquee scene `keys` (GN Neon), 3D profile `keys` with a `skyline` topper, menu background `keys-city`. Tokens `--kt-*`. `?demo`: every district open, Space = the right answer, W = a wrong one (instrument mode: hold Space to "play" it); `Arcade.KeysCity.state()` (incl. `labels`, `signTap`, `limit`, `hint`) / `signTap(kind)` / `answer()` / `tapKey(midi)` / `name(letter, acc)` / `place(step)` / `begin(lv | 'endless')` for tests.
