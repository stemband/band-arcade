# TUNE UP (TOOLBOX) (`note-checker/`)

- THE TOOLBOX (`toolbox.js`, loaded BEFORE the tools: `requireInstrument('note-checker')` + `mountTopbar`, `Arcade.TuneUp` = `{inst, member, tab, data() (= gameData('tuneup')), save(), register(id, {listens, enter, leave, micOn}), go(id), reward(title, text), state()}`): three TABS at the top (`.tu-tabs`, role tablist, ≥ 48 px, tinted cyan / pink / yellow, ←/→ Home/End): NOTE CHECKER (`checker.js`) · TUNER (`tuner.js`) · METRONOME (`metronome.js`); styles `style.css` (the checker) + `tools.css`, tokens `--tu-*` in theme.css. The last tab is remembered (`gameData('tuneup').tab`); `?tool=checker|tuner|metronome` opens one; the top bar's TUNE UP button opens the toolbox. SWITCHING TABS calls the old tool's `leave()` (its sound stops: the metronome's kit, the tuner's tone; its range/a4/demo keys put back), then the mic: a LISTENING tab (the Note Checker; the Tuner for pitched instruments) = `requireMic` → `Pitch.pauseListening(false)` (the tab tap is the gesture); the METRONOME (and the snare's Tuner tab) = `Pitch.stop()`: it never opens the microphone. The mic sensitivity block shows on listening tabs only. Every handler in a tool acts only while its tab shows. TOOLS: no stars, no setLevel, no leaderboard events (only the two avatar items below). `T.reward` = a small panel with `Skins.announce` (nothing new = nothing shows), only between notes / after the click has stopped.
- SNARE DRUM: the TUNER tab says "Drums don't need tuning! Try the Metronome." + a button to it; the METRONOME works for everyone; the NOTE CHECKER keeps ARTICULATION.

## The Note Checker (`checker.js`)

- Modes FIRST 5 | B♭ | E♭ | F | A♭ | CHROMATIC | ARTICULATION (stored as 'five', 'Bb'…'Ab', 'full', 'art'). ARTICULATION: counts `Pitch.onAttack` (big number + flash, note name, per second, Reset, the hold hint); the only mode for the snare (not saved); Mat's check of attack detection on real instruments. Scale modes show the scale up and down with its key signature; a held pitch lights the first dark place with that pitch, octave-exact, "11 of 15 notes".
- FIRST 5 NOTES: the original checker (letter names, any octave; its staff is unchanged). CHROMATIC (FULL RANGE): the member's whole chromatic scale on wrapped staff rows (≥ 50 px per note so accidentals never touch; the notes shared out evenly between the rows), each note captioned. SCALES: the way UP on one row and the way DOWN on the next (each split in two when a row would get under 40 px a note, e.g. on phones). EVEN SPACING (checker.js `drawFull`): every gap between two notes is equal, counting the room a ♯/♭/♮ takes in front of its note (`ACC_L` vs `HEAD_L`), with one gap for every row so the rows line up. Key signatures use `staffSVG(…, {sigStyle: 'big'})` (ui.js `SIG_STYLES.big`: further from the clef, larger ♯/♭; `keySigWidth(sig, 'big')`): only the Note Checker; every other game keeps the normal style.
- The octave rule: a note lights gold only when held in the octave written on the staff (exact sounding pitch via `sounds`). A same-letter note in another octave never counts; if the student replays a found note while its other octave is still missing, show "That's a D, but an octave lower. Try the higher one." Readings an octave outside the whole range are folded in by the pitch engine (mic error), so they do count.
- The note being heard gets a glowing outline; "Going down" shows the same pitches descending with flats (one set of found notes). `?demo` in full range: ↑/↓ pick a note, hold Space to play it, Shift+Space an octave low.

## The Tuner (`tuner.js`): THE HOT-AIR BALLOON

- Numbers in the commented `TUNER` block at its top (Mat edits): `maxCents` 50, `smoothMs` 120, `inTune` 5, `close` 15, `holdS` 4, `drainS` 2, `tipAfterS` 1.5, `wobbleCents` 12, `quietMs`, `rest`, `topRoom` .8, `confettiGapS`, `toneMs` 2000, `listenAfterMs` 400, `rewardHolds` 5.
- THE SCENE: an original evening sky (`--tu-sky-*`), the student's AVATAR in the basket of a striped balloon (`avatarHTML` three times: calm, HAPPY = mouth `beam` + eyes `cheerful`, WORRIED = mouth `surprised` + brows `raised`; `data-face`), a LANDING PLATFORM (a floating island with a flag, `.tn-platform`, 58 % down: its TOP edge is where the basket lands). THE MAPPING: the basket's bottom = the platform − cents/maxCents × (platform − `topRoom` × balloon height) when SHARP (above), + |cents|/maxCents × (bottom − platform) when FLAT (below), clamped; IN TUNE (|c| ≤ inTune) = on the platform. Cents are smoothed (exponential, `smoothMs`); nothing heard for `quietMs` = it drifts back to a resting spot just below the platform + "Play a long note!".
- THE STATES: IN TUNE = landed, happy face, confetti ONCE per landing (≤ one burst per `confettiGapS`, none with reduced motion / MOTION off: the landing is a simple settle), "IN TUNE!" green; CLOSE (≤ close) "Almost! A little lower" / "Almost! A little higher" amber; FARTHER "Too high (sharp)" / "Too low (flat)" coral, worried face, a small wobble, ▲/▼ ARROWS on both sides pointing the way to MOVE. Direction never by color alone (height + arrows + words). `.tn-words` `data-kind`.
- THE NOTE: the big WRITTEN name (the reading's sounding note + the member's `sounds`, spelled like the arcade's parts: `NAMES`), on a small staff in their clef, "concert F" under it; "Show concert pitch first" (`gameData('tuneup').concertFirst`) swaps them; "+12 ¢" in the corner ("Show cents", `showCents`, on by default).
- HOLD IT: a ring around the platform fills while the note stays within `close` (`holdS` to fill), drains over `drainS` when it wobbles out or stops; full = "Steady! 4 seconds in tune" + "Best: 7.2 s" (the longest run within `close` this page load). Each fill (once until the ring empties) adds to `gameData('tuneup').holds['YYYY-MM-DD']` (30 days kept).
- WHAT TO DO: `TUNING_TIPS` (member id → [sharp, flat], Mat edits) after `tipAfterS` sharp or flat (`#tnTip`); a note jumping around (the cents' spread over 2 s > `wobbleCents`) = "Steady air, relaxed embouchure."; bells: "Bells don't need tuning!".
- FREE mode (default) = cents from the nearest note (`reading.cents`). TARGET mode (`tunerMode`, the target picker `tunerTarget`: the TUNING NOTE = concert B♭ nearest the first-five tonic inside the member's range, + the first five) = cents from the TARGET in any octave (`reading.midi`); another note = "That's an A, try a B♭!" (written names); the target's name on the flag. HEAR THE NOTE: TARGET mode, `Pitch.pauseListening(true)` + `Sfx.sync()`, `tones.play([target], {noteMs: 2000})` (shifted by the reference A), `Pitch.suppress` too; when the tone has faded + `listenAfterMs`: listening again, `ignoreCurrent()`, "Now you play it!". The tone's pitch can never count (the mic is paused).
- THE MIC: `Pitch.setRange(member.soundLow, soundHigh)` (octave folding as usual), `Pitch.a4` = the reference A while the tab is open, the sensitivity in the block below and in Settings. Settings → Tuner: "Reference A" 438–445 Hz (`gameData('tuneup').a4`).
- THE GOLDEN TUNING FORK: the 5th full HOLD IT ring of one day → `T.reward` once the note has ended (≥ 1 s of nothing heard), never mid-hold.
- `?demo`: hold Space = in tune, U = sharp (+25 ¢), J = flat (−25 ¢), W = a wrong note; `Pitch.demoJitter` .02 on this tab. `Arcade.TuneUp.tuner.state()` (`status`, `dir`, `cents`, `basket`, `platform`, `pos` rest/landed/above/below, `words`, `arrow`, `big`, `small`, `hold`, `filled`, `best`, `tip`, `mode`, `target`, `phase` listen/tone/yourturn, `face`, `confetti`, `flag`) / `hear()` / `setMode()` / `TARGETS` / `TUNING_TIPS` for tests.

## The Metronome (`metronome.js`): THE AVATAR ON THE BEAT + THE TEMPO LADDER

- NEVER USES THE MICROPHONE (the tab turns it off). Numbers in the commented `METRO` block (Mat edits); THE TEMPO WORDS = `TEMPO_WORDS` ([from BPM, word]: Largo < 60, Larghetto 60–65, Adagio 66–75, Andante 76–107, Moderato 108–119, Allegro 120–167, Presto 168+).
- THE SCHEDULER: the click = music-highway/backing.js's kit (`Sfx.buffer('mh-click')`, else the generated woodblock; unpitched noise) on `Sfx.output()`, scheduled on the AudioContext clock `lookaheadS` ahead by a `tickMs` timer; tick j of a SEGMENT is at `t0 + j × tick length` (never added up), a new segment only when the tempo or the meter changes (a control's tempo on the next beat, the ladder's on a DOWNBEAT). Beat 1 = the accent click (`vol.one`), 6/8's beat 4 accented too (`vol.accent`), other beats `vol.beat`, subdivisions softer (`vol.sub`). SILENT MODE: the same schedule, no click (`sound: false`); the audio clock is still used when the audio is on. Sound off / not unlocked = a performance.now() clock.
- METERS: 2/4, 3/4, 4/4, 2/2 (¢, 2 half-note beats), 6/8 = 6 eighths (accents 1 and 4; "Feel in 2" = 2 dotted-quarter beats with the eighths as soft ticks); BPM = the counted beat. SUBDIVISION none / eighths / triplets / sixteenths (disabled in 6/8).
- THE STAGE: the avatar's full-body FIGHT stills (`AvatarFight.stillHTML`: idle, and VICTORY = arms up), else the bust; the beat counter (accented numbers underlined), beat dots, subdivision ticks. THE BOP follows the AUDIBLE time (`Arcade.AudioClock`: `audAt`): on the ground and squashed at each beat, a bounce in between (`hop`), beat 1 a bigger hop (`hopOne`) with arms up for 60 % of the beat; reduced motion / MOTION off = no bobbing (the counter still lights). Silent mode: the lit number glows gently, at most once per `flashMinMs` (≤ 3 a second), never full-screen.
- CONTROLS: −/+ (±1, held = ±5 after `holdMs`, repeating), the slider 40–208, TAP TEMPO (the average of the last 4 taps; > 2 s idle starts over), the meter, subdivision, SILENT, START/STOP; keys Space start/stop, ↑/↓ tempo (Shift ±5), T tap (never inside a box or the slider); every control ≥ 44 px. A HIDDEN TAB stops it: "Paused — tap START".
- THE TEMPO LADDER (`#mtLad`): START (60), GOAL (100), STEP (4), MEASURES (4), STOP AT GOAL; rungs = start, +step… up to the goal (60→100 step 4 = 11 rungs, 10 steps). START LADDER = a one-measure count-in, then every MEASURES measures +STEP on a downbeat; at the goal "You climbed to 100! 🎉" (`#ldMsg`) and keeps going (or stops after MEASURES measures at the goal); `tuneup-ladder-top` (falls back to `level-complete`) plays once the click has STOPPED, never on a beat. HOLD (stay on this rung: "Practice this speed until it's easy"), STEP BACK (−STEP at the next downbeat; HOLD stays as it was). The ladder graphic (rungs, the avatar's chip climbing), "Step 3 of 11 · 2 measures to the next step". The tempo controls are disabled during a ladder. A ladder of ≥ `ladderPlate` 8 steps climbed to the goal adds 1 to `gameData('tuneup').ladders` (3 = the Ladder Climber name plate, `.av-plate-ladder`).
- SAVED: `gameData('tuneup').metro` {bpm, meter, feel2, sub, silent} and `.ladder` {start, goal, step, measures, stopAtGoal}.
- TODAY'S PRACTICE (the lobby's card, [page-flow.md](../engine/page-flow.md)) reads three daily logs here (`'YYYY-MM-DD'` keys, `store.dayKey()`, so `?demo&today=` works; the last 30 days kept): the Tuner's `holds` (one ring = the winds' warm-up), the Metronome's `metroS` (the seconds it REALLY ran that day: metronome.js `countRun`, on its own clock (the audio clock with the sound on, else performance.now), counted only while the Metronome tab shows; a gap over 1 s (a suspended clock) never counts; saved every 5 s and when it stops; 120 = the snare's warm-up) and `ladderTop` (ladders that reached their goal that day). The lobby's practice card opens `?tool=tuner` / `?tool=metronome` and comes back to the lobby. `metronome.state()` adds `today` (seconds) and `ladderTop`.
- `Arcade.TuneUp.metronome.state()` / `log()` (every scheduled tick: t, bar, beat, sub, accent, one, vol, bpm, sound, countIn) / `bops()` (each beat's click time vs. the bop's landing on the audible clock) / `start({ladder})` / `stop()` / `setBpm()` / `tap()` / `tempoWord()` / `times(bpm, n)` / `rungs()` for tests.
