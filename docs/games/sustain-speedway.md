# Sustain Speedway (`sustain-speedway/`)

- THE UI KIT: a kit pause during the countdown restarts it from 3 on RESUME (like a hidden tab); results are wide, levels button `#resLevels`, `actsFirst`: place + car, stars, title, message, tiles → THE BUTTONS (NEXT TRACK yellow + focused, else TRY AGAIN; TRY AGAIN, TRACKS, the plain GARAGE) → NEW IN THE GARAGE! (its filled GARAGE; then no plain one) → "Race details" (`#resMore`, a small heading: the teacher ghost line, the longest hold, "Your tuning", the difficulty, the per-lap chart + lap notes). The buttons are on screen without scrolling on a phone in portrait, an iPad both ways and a laptop (`.ui-acts-first` shrinks the place, the car and the stars on short screens).
- Long-tone and intonation racing game, LISTENS (pitch.js + mic-gate.js + sfx.js). The car moves only while the lap's target note sounds (`reading.pc === item.pc`, any octave; cents are measured from the TARGET with `reading.midi`, not `reading.cents`, because the engine's hysteresis can hold a pitch class up to ±65 cents away). Speed = `SPEEDWAY_RULES.weights` × (intonation credit: 1 within the difficulty's `tol`, sliding to `low.credit` at `low.cents`) + (pitch steadiness: std of cents over `windowMs`) + (volume steadiness: std of dB). Wrong note (`wrongFrames` readings in a row) = hard `brake` + "Target: B♭" flash; silence = `coast`. NITRO: within `nitro.cents` and both steadiness scores ≥ `nitro.steady` for `holdMs` → ×`boost` while it lasts ("IN THE ZONE!"). Difficulties Rookie/Pro/Virtuoso (tol 20/12/6), remembered in gameData `diff`. The UI says IN TUNE and STEADY only; never claim to measure tone quality.
- `levels.js`: `SPEEDWAY_TRACKS` (8: name, blurb, laps, lap (s at full speed; `[first, last]` = growing), pool, scene, sky, tunnels, rivals {name, color token, pace}) + `SPEEDWAY_RULES`. Notes ONLY from `ModePicker.sequence` (count = laps). A lap = a distance (full-speed seconds). PIT STOP between laps (`pitSec`, next note shown): THE PIT COUNTDOWN (`pitCoach`) splits it into 4 equal beats (beat = pitSec / 4, on the race clock, so it freezes with the pause menu, suppression and a hidden tab): "Breathe in…", then a big 3, 2, 1 (`.coach-num`, styled like the start countdown, with "Breathe in… get ready!" small above; each number pops in gently, `coach-pop`, none under reduced motion / Motion off), then leavePit()'s "GO!" banner as the race resumes; no sounds (tests/speedway-pit-countdown.spec.js, `Arcade.Speedway.pitCoach()`); rivals pit too (`paceProgress`). Race clock stops while `Pitch.isSuppressed`. Stars: 1st 3, 2nd 2, 3rd 1, last 0; winning (3 ★ on the previous track) opens the next; `?demo` opens all.
- Progress per MEMBER (`byMember`): `setLevel(picker.state.progressKey, member.id, track, {stars, best: finish time in tenths})`. `gameData('sustain-speedway')` = `{diff, ghosts: {'<progressKey>|<member>|<track>': {t, p: [progress every ghostEvery s]}}, bestLap: {same key: s}, achievements: {'virtuoso-win': true, 'perfect-lap': true}, wins, garage: {body, paint, decal, number}, garageSeen: ['body:muscle', …]}`. Ghost = best total time.
- The road is a canvas (requestAnimationFrame, dpr ≤ 1.5, simple polygons/gradients, colors from `--sw-*` tokens); the HUD is HTML/SVG (tuning speedometer, note card on a light `--screen`, STEADY meter). Reduced motion: no speed lines, no sway, no pulses. THE CARS (`sustain-speedway/cars.js`, `Arcade.SpeedwayCars`): 2D, from behind, each drawn ONCE per LOOK (body, paint, FINISH, two-tone roof, decal + its color, number, rims, spoiler, plate text) × size bucket × sky × brake state into a cached SPRITE (gradient body, shoulder highlight, the sky's rim light, valance, soft shadow, tires with tread and the rim style's edge, diffuser, exhausts, plate, tail lights with a soft halo), then only copied each frame; per frame only the underglow (a cached glow sprite; a flat ellipse in LITE), the Dune Buggy's bounce, the hovers' shimmer and the nitro flames in the chosen color (none with reduced motion). BODIES (17): coupe, mini, muscle, wagon, buggy, openwheel, hover, maestro (rival only) + pickup, rally (mud flaps, roof vent, light pod), stock (number panels, blade spoiler), hotrod (blower + stacks over the roof), kart (driver on show), supercar, monster (huge tires, one lane wide), retrohover, bumper (round, a pole). FINISHES (`fillPaint`): gloss, matte (soft highlight), metallic (a baked flake pattern), pearl (a sheen toward the track's light), chrome (the sky reflected), neon (glowing edges). DARK PAINTS (luminance < `DARK` .16) get a light RIM around the whole sprite (`--sw-rim-light`) + a brighter highlight on every track. SPOILERS: 'stock' = the body's own (`OWN_SPOILER`), else none / lip / wing / ducktail / tall on the body's `DECK`. `Cars.side(ctx, x, y, w, o)` = THE SIDE VIEW from `SIDE` profiles (rims drawn: classic, five-star, disc, whitewall, turbine, off-road, gold, neon rim), `rimURL(style)`, `sideHeight(body)`. THE GARAGE (`sustain-speedway/garage.js`, `Arcade.SpeedwayGarage`; the GARAGE button just left of START in the track select's sticky row (`LevelSelect.show({beside})`: a secondary button as tall as START, its car icon + "new" dot, icon-only with aria-label "Garage" at ≤ 560 px), never the arcade's menus): tabs BODY · PAINT (color (~26: the neon tokens, Gloss Black free, Matte Black (always matte), silver, gold, teal, navy…: `--sw-paint-*`), FINISH, TWO-TONE roof) · DECALS (16: stripes single / double / offset, flames, stars, checker, lightning, an original swoosh, music notes, treble clef, number 1–99, camo, polka dots, tiger stripes, gradient fade; + DECAL COLOR = any unlocked paint or its own colors) · PARTS (wheels, spoiler, underglow, nitro flame color incl. a slow rainbow, trail: none / streak / sparkles / notes / stars, drawn at speed in game.js, never in LITE or with reduced motion) · PLATE (a preset word from `plateWords` or 4 digit steppers: NO typed text anywhere); the preview = the rear view on a turntable with a gentle sway, or the SIDE VIEW (`#gView`); RANDOMIZE (`randomize()`: unlocked items only); 3 SAVED LOADOUTS (`gameData.loadouts` [{name, car}]: the name is tapped through `loadoutNames` (Car 1/2/3, Race Day, Showtime, Cruiser), SAVE HERE / WEAR); ←/→ between tabs, Tab stays inside, Esc closes. Saved per device in `gameData.garage` `{body, paint, finish, paint2, decal, dcolor, number, rims, spoiler, glow, flame, trail, plate}` (`clean()` puts a locked or unknown choice back to the default); an old save = Coupe + the instrument's color with decal AUTO (null: stripes when the equipped skin has them, i.e. Racing Stripes). THE UNLOCKS = `SPEEDWAY_GARAGE` in levels.js (Mat edits; rules `{free}`, `{stars}` (Speedway stars, device-wide), `{tracks}` finished, `{wins}` different tracks won, `{winCount}` races won (`gameData.wins`, seeded from old wins), `{track: n}`, `{achievement}` ('virtuoso-win', 'perfect-lap' = a lap averaging within `nitro.cents`, 'teacher-ghost'), `{bestLap}`, `{cleanLaps}` (`gameData.cleanLaps`: in the zone ≥ `cleanLapShare` of a lap after the nitro wait), `{breath}` (the best steady hold, any instrument), `{dynZones}` (`gameData.dynNailed`: the right dynamic ≥ `dynNailShare` of a zone), `{slurs}` (`gameData.slurSmooth`), `{arcadeStars}` (every game), `{any}`), checked from saved progress so old progress counts; locked = a dark silhouette + how to earn it + progress ("2 of 3 clean laps so far"), never pickable; a locked saved choice falls back. THE NEXT UNLOCK (`nextUnlock()`: the locked item closest to being earned) shows on the track select (`#garageNext`). NEW IN THE GARAGE!: `gameData.garageSeen`; the results screen shows the EARNED ones since (free items are never announced) (a card, amber centered heading, with a FILLED amber GARAGE button that breathes twice, `.garage-fill`) and the hub's button a "new" dot. EVERY results screen has exactly ONE GARAGE: the card's when something is new, else an outline one (the track menu's look + car icon, `#resGarage`, the kit's `more`) after TRACKS, NEXT still the yellow one; the garage opens over the results and closes back to them. The chosen car races, the ghost is the same car see-through, and the results show it. `?demo&unlockall` opens everything. Tests: tests/speedway-garage.spec.js + tests/speedway-garage-deep.spec.js. Cars and cabinet are original. NO TWO CARS OVERLAP ON SCREEN, AND NOTHING GLITCHES (`placeCars`, drawing only: progress, positions, times and standings never change): three lanes, yours the middle; the others keep to a side lane (rivals left, right, left; the ghost right). THE STARTING GRID: before GO and for the first `GRID.hold` s every rival sits in a fixed slot (2 a row), then the grid offset fades over `GRID.blend` s so the cars leave it smoothly; the ghost joins once the grid is gone. STABLE LANES: a car changes lane only after `LANE_BLOCK_MS` (400) blocked in a row, then stays `LANE_STAY_MS` (1.5 s); moves slide at `LANE_SPEED`; cars are placed in the order they were drawn last frame (ties by index), so nothing flips. NO JUMPS: a car that must be held back (never drawn over another car or yours) eases its drawn distance there and back (`EASE`, at most `EASE_MAX` road units a second). Reduced motion: no sliding, still at most one lane change per 1.5 s. `G.carsDrawn` (name, idx, lane, grid, d, drawnD, off, box) for tests: tests/speedway-lanes.spec.js.
- THE COUNTDOWN: `A.countdown` (shared/countdown.js), CLASSIC 3·2·1 one second apart + a spoken GO, voice prefix 'race' (`race-count-3/-2/-1/-go`, mic: true because the mic is already listening; missing = the Dojo Duel voice, then the tick), on the race's own countdown clock (`G.cd`, `countTick` in the loop, only while the page is visible). Each number shows as its clip plays. The countdown's sounds are `Sfx.prefer`red when the track screen shows and `whenReady` (≤ 0.8 s) before the first beat. THE RACE STARTS WHEN THE MIC IS LIVE: after the GO voice (`G.goHeard`) the loop waits until `Pitch.isSuppressed()` is over, then shows GO!, starts the car, the clock and scoring (`G.liveAt`); a note started during GO counts from then on. Hiding the page during the countdown stops it (`Sfx.hush`); it restarts from 3 when the page comes back (`G.restarts`). `race-countdown` (the old one-file countdown) is no longer used.
- PITCH STEERING (drawing only: `driveStep(dt)` = the speed from what is heard, never from the steering): while the lap's note sounds the car's sideways place shows the pitch (`steerOf(cents)`): within the difficulty's `tol` = the center line, beyond it FLAT drifts LEFT, SHARP drifts RIGHT, reaching the road's edge at `RULES.steer.edge` 50¢; smoothed `smoothMs` 250, back to the center over `backMs` when the note stops (breath, pit, a wrong note: wrong notes still brake and never steer). Past `rumble` .75: the red-white rumble strip on that side glows (a soft 2 Hz pulse), the car wobbles a little (none under reduced motion) and `#steerWord` shows "FLAT ◀" / "▶ SHARP". Rivals/the ghost avoid the drifting car's box (placeCars as before); the ghost replays its own steering (`ghosts[key].s`, one sample per `ghostEvery`; older ghosts drive straight). The tuning meter's ends say "◀ flat" / "sharp ▶". One-time hint at the first GO (`gameData.steerHint`, `#steerHint`). THE INTONATION REPORT ("Your tuning", `#resTuning` on the results, above the per-lap chart): per target note (written + octave, e.g. "D4": `noteKey`), only holds ≥ `RULES.report.reportHoldSec` 1 s count (`holdReading`/`endHold`): average signed cents, wobble (the holds' std), zone time, the longest run within `tol`; a centered bar (flat left, sharp right, the in-tune band shaded) + "right on!" / "a little high (sharp)" / "a little low (flat)"; up to `maxTips` 2 TIPS from `sustain-speedway/tips.js` (`SPEEDWAY_TIPS`, Mat edits: `general` by member then family for an overall ≥ `tendency` 10¢ lean, note-specific `notes` (member + written notes + dir), else the `air` tip; `dynamics` tips for DYNAMICS ZONES); every note in tune = "Right on pitch! Great ears."; history per member `gameData.tuning[member]` = the last 10 races `[{d, n: {note: cents}}]` → a trend per note ("▲ getting closer!"). `?demo` D = drifting sharp (+25 → +45¢ over 4 s, wobble + jitter only ±2¢ each so it never crosses 50¢ into the next note, which the detector's hysteresis can then hold as a wrong note), F = drifting flat. `Arcade.Speedway.steer()` / `tipsFor` / `driveStep` for tests: tests/speedway-steering.spec.js (+ tests/speedway-helpers.js).
- THE LOOK OF A RACE (`sustain-speedway/scenery.js` = `Arcade.SpeedwayScene` + game.js `render`): each track's TIME OF DAY (levels.js `time`: sunset / golden / dusk / dawn / noon / night / neon; `TIMES` = sky tokens, sun or moon, stars, streetlights, ground, the cars' rim light; `sky` stays the fallback) + optional `weather` ('mist' | 'haze' | 'rain'). LAYERS: the sky (cached per size + time, slides with the bends), FAR and MID scenery canvases (`buildScenery`: drawn once per size, parallax with the bends; the Grand Prix's MID = grandstands of fans + banners), and the NEAR layer = roadside sprites every `PROP_GAP` road units (`SCENE_PROPS`: streetlights with light pools when the time has lights, palms, pines, cacti, rocks, buoys, grandstands, banners; each sprite drawn once at 96 px). SPEED FEEL (scaled by the speed): edge speed lines, rumble-strip ridges, tire smoke at GO and while braking; NITRO = a light shake + long soft streaks (no canvas self-redraw: too slow on old iPads); none of the shake/streaks/speed lines with reduced motion or Motion off. THE FINISH: a checkered gate + line on the road on the last lap (6 road units per second), the checkered flag waving as you cross (`FINISH.flagMs` 1.5 s; still under reduced motion), confetti for 1st / sparkles for 2nd–3rd, then THE PODIUM (`#podium`, `FINISH.podiumMs` 2.2 s; a tap, Enter, Space or Esc skips it): steps 2-1-3 with cars (`Cars.thumbURL`), your avatar (`avatarHTML`) + car on your step (4th = beside the podium), then the results; no new sound (race-finish, then the results' podium). SEASONAL TOUCHES in the race (the one game page that follows the seasonal look, on request): `SEASON_FX` by `Seasons.look()` (spooky: jack-o'-lanterns, bats, fog; winter: string lights, snowy pines, snow; frost: snow; spring: flowers, petals; friendship: hearts; miosm/concert: notes; harvest: hay, leaves; school: pennants), off with the Seasonal look switch unless `?season=` previews. GRAPHICS (the Settings panel's "Graphics: Auto / Full / Lite", `gameData.gfx`, default Auto): AUTO averages the frame time `PERF.from`–`PERF.to` s into a race and, over `PERF.ms` 22 ms, switches to LITE for good on the device (`gameData.gfxSlow`), saying nothing. LITE = sky + far layer only, no roadside things, no particles/weather/fog, no shake/streaks, fewer speed lines. Measured at 4× CPU throttle (headless): Full ≈ 36 ms, Lite ≈ 32 ms a frame. `Arcade.Speedway.fx()` / `podium()` for tests: tests/speedway-visuals.spec.js.
- DYNAMICS ZONES (levels.js `dyn: {zones, len, kinds}` from track 3; `RULES.dyn`): per lap `zones` stretches of `len` of the lap (none on slur laps; markings in turn from `kinds`, a random start per race: `makeZones`), each a colored band on the road + a big roadside sign (p / f in bold italic serif, the cresc. / decresc. hairpins; `roadSign`) and the `#dynBadge` (the marking + "softer!" / "louder!"). THE VOLUME CHECK (`volumeCheck`, `#vcheck` in the stage: "Play your note SOFT… now LOUD", `checkSec` 3 s each; RETRY; "I didn't hear…" = no LET'S RACE; too close = "Try a bigger difference", CONTINUE then uses `fallbackSpread`) runs before the countdown of the first dynamics race of a PLAY SESSION (`Arcade.session` 'sw-dyn' + sessionStorage `bandarcade.sw-dyn` {who, soft, loud} dB); tracks without `dyn` never ask; the Settings panel's "Volume check: Redo" clears it. In a zone the loudness (dB of the reading's level, smoothed `smoothMs`, as 0 = soft … 1 = loud) must be p ≤ `softMax`, f ≥ `loudMin`, cresc / decresc within `rampWindow` of a rising / falling ramp; wrong for `graceMs` = speed × `slow` .8 (S.dynMul in driveStep). Right dynamics = full speed, so best times stay comparable (the tracks keep their numbers: no new track versions). SLUR LAPS (levels.js `slurLaps` from track 5; `RULES.slur`): the target switches halfway (`slurTick`: "SLUR TO F!", the note card shows both notes with a slur arc, `slurStaff`; the pit card too); the old note still counts for `graceMs`; a silence of `gapMs` or more around the switch = a BREAK (counted from the last sound heard, so a slow frame between two readings never hides a real break) (speed × `slow` for `slowMs`, "Slur it!"), else "Smooth!". The second note (`slurPartner`) comes from the NOTES pool only: brass = the same primary fingering (shared/fingerings.js, now loaded here) when the pool has one, else 1–5 half steps; woodwinds 1–5 half steps (2–4 preferred) never across the break (clarinets B♭4|B4, saxes C♯5|D5). THE REPORT adds "Pitch in soft / loud zones" (≥ 20 readings each; soft = p and the soft ends of the hairpins) with tips.js `dynamics` tips (softFlat, loudSharp…) and "Slurs: 3 of 4 smooth". `?demo`: Space plays each zone's dynamic by itself, S = soft, L = loud, B = like Space with a break at the switch (silent at least 300 ms and until the game has heard the gap, at most 1.5 s: held by the game's state, not by frames, so a slow WebKit frame can't skip the silence) (shared/pitch.js `Pitch.demoLevel` = the demo note's loudness). `Arcade.Speedway.dyn()` / `slur()` / `skipChecks()` for tests: tests/speedway-dynamics.spec.js.
- BREATH STATS (`RULES.breath`): the longest STEADY HOLD = unbroken tone within the difficulty's tolerance (each hold's in-tune run, `holdReading`). Results: "Longest steady hold: 7.4 s (new record!)" (`#resBreath`) + a gentle "🌬️ Breath record!" (`#brBadge`) when a saved record is beaten. The track select's PERSONAL RECORDS panel (`#records`, this instrument): the longest hold, a small bar chart of the last 10 races + a trend word, and each written note's best. Saved in `gameData.breath[member]` = `{best, notes: {<note>: {s, m}}, hist: [{d, best}]}`. THE TEACHER GHOST CHALLENGE (`sustain-speedway/teacher-ghosts.js`, Mat edits: `SPEEDWAY_TEACHER_GHOSTS = [{name, week, code}]`, how-to at its top; empty = no challenge): with `?teacher` the results offer SAVE AS TEACHER GHOST (`#resTeacherGhost`) = a UI.confirm with a read-only copy box (a ready line `{name: "Mr. Graham", week, code}` + COPY). THE CODE `SSG1-<payload>-<check>`: payload `1~track~notes~order~difficulty~total tenths~progress` (ghost samples every `ghostEvery` as thousandths of a lap, steps in base 36 with `step*count` runs), check = FNV-1a base 36; `decodeGhost` refuses a bad checksum, an unknown track/difficulty, or a run that doesn't finish its track (ignored; a console warning in ?demo). The newest `week` per track wins. A challenged track's card shows BEAT MR. GRAHAM (`.tg-badge`); racing it adds a gold see-through ghost (open-wheel, `--sw-teacher-gold`) with the name tag over it (joins after the grid, like your own ghost); the results say "You beat Mr. Graham's ghost by 2.3 s!" / "Mr. Graham's ghost won by 1.1 s: try again!" (`#resTeacher`); beating it sets `achievements['teacher-ghost']` = the garage paint Teacher's Gold (`sw-teacher-gold`). Nothing leaves the device. `Arcade.Speedway.breath()` / `ghostCode()` / `decodeGhost()` / `teacher(lv)` for tests: tests/speedway-breath-teacher.spec.js.
- SOUND: nothing during a race. The countdown before GO, `pit-in` in the pit (a rest), `race-finish`, then results `podium`/`star-earned`/`new-best-lap`. Skins: The Grand Prix won (level 8, 3 ★) → `stripes`; any win on Virtuoso → `helmet`.
- Bells/snare: blocked (games.js `noPlay.block`). `?demo`: hold Space = in tune, D = drifting sharp, W = wrong note, E = centered but wobbly; release = breathing. `Arcade.Speedway.debug()` for tests.
- Cabinet: `shape: 'speedway'` (a sit-down racer: SHAPES `wheel: [x, y, r]` draws a steering wheel instead of the joystick), `marquee: 'speedway'`, `screen: 'speedway'`; 3D profile `speedway` (`wheel: true`, topper `wing`).
