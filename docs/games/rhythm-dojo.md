# Rhythm Dojo (`rhythm-dojo/`)

- Rhythm reading (zone ninja-dojo; `player: 'all'`, `unpitched: true`, no instrument needed; `maxStars` 33 = 11 levels × 3; progress `setLevel('rhythm-dojo', 'all', level, {stars, best: score})`). A rhythm on the ONE-LINE STAFF with MR. GRAHAM'S COUNTING underneath (shared/rhythm-staff.js + counting.js: RHYTHMS AND COUNTING in [rhythm.md](../engine/rhythm.md)); the student PERFORMS it: TAP (THE DEFAULT on a device with no saved mode, first of the mode buttons and pressed in the markup: a big drum pad or Space, no microphone: a tap counts on pointerdown / Space keydown; letting go never matters; its timing check is the tap one, so nothing asks for the microphone until CLAP or SNARE is picked), CLAP (the microphone, shared/onsets.js: claps as onsets, never pitch), or SNARE (the microphone, like CLAP). NOTHING IS HELD in any mode (claps, taps and snare hits can't sustain): every note is judged only by when it starts; students SAY the small underlined counts of a long note (never clap/tap them), and no text may tell them to hold a note. `gameData('rhythm-dojo')` = `{mode, counting, speed, hp, calib: {clap, tap}}` (the snare uses clap's timing check).
- A ROUND: the rhythm shows (study) → HEAR IT (optional, the microphone PAUSED: the woodblock (`kit.block`) over the click, a playhead, each syllable lighting as it sounds) → PERFORM: `rd-count-in` ("Ready!") ends, then a ONE-MEASURE COUNT-IN (clicks + the big beat number ON THE TAIKO's barrel (`#countBig`, aria-live, sized to the drum: `78cqh` of its box, a clamp fallback) + a row of beat dots under it (`#countDots`, one lit per beat: 4, 3 in 3/4, 6 in 6/8); NEVER over the notation; 6/8 counts all six), the playhead and THE PULSE LIGHT (the taiko's rim: brightest on each beat, fading smoothly: 1 swell a beat, ≤ 2.2 a second; steady with reduced motion). CLAP/SNARE: no clicks after the count-in (HEADPHONES mode, after the headphones check proved the microphone can't hear the clicks, keeps the beat going); TAP: the beat keeps going and each tap knocks the woodblock. EVERY ROW ON SCREEN: `fitRows()` scales the rows (all at one scale) until the sheet fits between the dojo and the bottom of the screen, never below `FIT_MIN` 0.6 px per staff unit (the small syllables ≥ 11 px); a pass scrolls the dojo + sheet into view (`inView`). Everything is scheduled on the arcade's AudioContext and judged in AUDIBLE time (`AudioClock`), minus the device's timing check (`calib[clap|tap].ms`, default `defaultLag`).
- JUDGING (levels.js `RD_RULES`, one commented block): each attack goes to the nearest unjudged note within `lateMs` 360: PERFECT ±70 · GOOD ±140 · OK ±220 · else EARLY/LATE; a note with none = MISS; an attack near no note (in a rest, or one too many) = EXTRA (counted like one more missed note). Attacks before the first note's window (the count-in) never count. Accuracy = the average `value`; a round's best attempt counts; stars from the level's average (`oneStar` 60 / `twoStar` 80 / `threeStar` 95 %); SLOW (−20 %) = no stars; Counting HIDE from the start = +10 % score. THE FEEDBACK: the counting shown again with every group colored (`--rd-ok` on time, `--rd-near` early/late, `--rd-bad` missed/extra; an extra in a rest = the rest red, in a held note = its small syllables red), ONE tip ("Shh! Beat 3 is a rest…", "You missed the & of 2…", "You rushed beat 3!", "You dragged…"), TRY AGAIN / NEXT; `rd-perfect` (≥ 95 %) or `rd-miss` (< 60 %) after the microphone stops.
- `levels.js` = `RD_LEVELS` (11: First Steps, Long Tones, Three and Two (3/4 + 2/4), Eighth Pairs, Lone Eighths, Dotted Quarters, Off the Beat (syncopation + ties), Sixteenth Storm, Triplet Temple, Six-Eight Swing (6/8), Master's Scroll (ties across bar lines, everything, 2–4 measures): time, tempo, `measures` [first, last round], `cells` (ONE-MEASURE rhythms in the text format; a round joins random cells, `tieAcross` ties measures (only between plain notes an eighth or longer), never the same rhythm twice in a level), `rounds` 8; saved by level number: never reorder) + `RD_RULES` + `RD_MARATHON`. The COUNTING setting: SHOW | AUTO (rounds 1–3 shown, 4–6 faded, 7–8 hidden: `RD_RULES.fade`) | HIDE; also in the shared Settings panel.
- DOJO MARATHON (the Endless card): random rhythms from a growing pool of levels (`poolEvery`), 1 → 4 measures, ties across from round 14, the tempo creeping from 80 to 120; study `studyMs`, then an automatic count-in; a rhythm under `loseBelow` 70 % = a life (3); a perfect one = double points. Top 5 per mode (`instKey` 'clap' | 'tap' | 'snare', `setKey` 'marathon'); in `Leaderboard.ENDLESS_GAMES`.
- THE TIMING CHECK (the level select's button; the first performance in a mode opens it): 4 clicks to listen to, then clap/tap on 8 (`Calibration.analyse`, `Sfx.outputRaw` clicks); "I heard the click, not your claps" when the round is the click itself. HEADPHONES (CLAP/SNARE): the headphones check plays `headCheckClicks` clicks while listening; heard `headCheckMax` or more = "It sounds like your speakers are on…" and it stays off.
- PAUSE (the shared kit): pausing stops the clicks and the attempt; RESUME goes back to the rhythm (press PERFORM again; the marathon counts in again by itself).
- Look: THE BAMBOO DOJO = JADE GREEN + GOLD everywhere (never pink, purple or cyan): games.js `color: 'green'`, cabinet `trim: 'green'`, `trim2: 'yellow'` (the gold lettering on its marquee: marquees.js draws a `taiko` title in trim2; the 3D drum's rim is trim2), the art's jade = `--rd-jade`/`-hi`/`-ink` (`--glow-rd-jade`), a teal jade kept apart from the results' green (`--rd-ok`, `--green`), `--rd-night` behind it, a warm-wood drum (`--rd-body`), gold rims and studs; the page's toggles, outlined buttons, panels (jade frame, gold titles), the sheet's frame, the pad (gold rim, jade glow) and the sounding syllable (`--rd-lit` = the jade ink, 5.1:1 on the screen) follow it; tests/rhythm-dojo.spec.js checks the art (no pink/purple/cyan, no flashes). The Sensei (`senseiSVG`, his belt = the level's belt: levels 1–10 = the Band Ninja belts, 11 = Diamond), THE TAIKO (an original neon drum on a stand, `--rd-*` tokens; it bounces on every clap/tap), belt-colored headbands on the level cards and the HUD.
