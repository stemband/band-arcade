# The Prize Counter and the shared wallet

- THE PRIZE COUNTER + THE SHARED WALLET: ONE WALLET, TWO COUNTERS. `shared/tokens.js` = `Arcade.Tokens`, loaded on EVERY page (shared/version.js writes its tag with the UI kit's; it reads nothing until called) and THE ONLY THING THAT READS OR WRITES THE WALLET: `tokens`, `converted` ({'m:<member>' | 'g:<game>': stars turned in}) and `convertedLeft`, stored where they always were, in the Arcade Quest save (`gameData('arcade-quest').save`), so old balances, QUEST CODES and Backup Codes keep working; a device that never opened Arcade Quest gets only `{wallet: true, tokens, converted}` there (no progress), which Arcade Quest's save.js `upgrade()` turns into a fresh save KEEPING them; Arcade Quest's NEW GAME (`Q.save.reset()`, also the Test Arena's reset) keeps the wallet too (tokens + `converted`), so no star is counted twice. API: `balance/add/spend/restore` (a save code; `add` is also TODAY'S PRACTICE's daily bonus, [page-flow.md](page-flow.md), and the WEEKLY CHAMPIONS card's CLAIM: `CHAMPION_REWARDS` tokens per 1st place, [leaderboard.md](leaderboard.md)), `starSources()` (the saved member's `allStars` + fixed-player games its groups don't cover, exactly as the booth did), `freshStars()`, `waiting()` (= fresh × RATE), `turnIn()` → {stars, tokens}, `catalog()` (every `unlock.shop` avatar item: `{key, field, id, name, full, questOnly}`; never event items or Band Ninja gear), `item/owned/price(key)` → {price, full, weekly}, `canBuy(key, {counter: 'prize' | 'quest'})` / `buy(...)` → {ok, why ('unknown'|'questOnly'|'owned'|'short'), text, need, price} (buying = `spend` + `store.ownItem`; wearing is the caller's), `weekly(date)`, `wish()/setWish(key)/wishProgress()`, `resultLine()`; every change fires window `arcade:tokens`. THE SETTINGS block at its top (Mat edits): `RATE` 5 tokens a star, `featuredDiscount` 0.2. Nothing can be counted twice: both counters use the same `converted` record, balance and `ownItem`. WHAT'S SOLD: every {shop} item at BOTH counters for the same price, EXCEPT THE MANOR COLLECTION = avatar-parts.js's `MANOR_COLLECTION` list at its end (Mat edits: Pirate hat 200, Magic wand 250, Fireflies Night 200, Royal crown 400), which sets `unlock.booth = 'quest'`: sold ONLY at Arcade Quest's booth; the Prize Counter shows them on their own shelf behind glass with a lock tag "Only at the Token Booth in Arcade Quest!" (a try-on, no BUY; `Tokens.buy` refuses with `why: 'questOnly'`); owners keep them. Requirements read "250 tokens at the Prize Counter" / "… at the Token Booth in Arcade Quest". CHARMS stay Arcade Quest only (the counter's foot says where they are). THE SEASONAL SHOP: NEW items with unlock `{shop: <price>, season: '<event id>'}` (avatar-parts.js's SEASONAL SHOP section + its one commented `SEASON_SHOP` table of ids, events and prices (Mat edits); 3 per event: Spooky Season Jack-o'-Lantern Treat Bucket 150 / Spiderweb Cape 250 / Black Cat 350; Winter Fest Hot Cocoa Mug 150 / Cozy Knit Sweater 250 / Snowy Owl 350; Friendship Week (only 8 days: cheaper) Heart Balloon 150 / Candy Heart name plate 150 / Teddy Bear 300; MIOSM Marching Shako 250 / Conductor's Tailcoat 300 / Music Note Sparkles 350 (avatar-fx.js `FX.notesparkles`, slow, still with reduced motion); Spring Bloom Rain Boots 150 / Polka-Dot Umbrella 200 / Ladybug 250; Summer Send-Off Ice Pop 100 / Hawaiian Shirt 250 / Hermit Crab 300; every id also at the END of its avatar-code.js TABLE list and of `QUEST_V5.cosmetics`): sold at BOTH counters ONLY while `Seasons.active()` is that event (so `?season=<id>` previews it), owned forever once bought; outside it `canBuy` says `why: 'offSeason'` ("The Black Cat is only sold during Spooky Season."); never the Prize of the Week (the weekly pool leaves them out), never discounted, never Manor Collection, never identity items. `Tokens.seasonal()` → {ev, daysLeft, left ("gone in 12 days!" / "gone tomorrow!" / "Last day!"), items}, `nextSeason()` → {ev, from, items}, `seasonOn(id)`. THE SEASONAL SHELF at the Prize Counter: during an event a decorated shelf at the top of the wall (`.pz-s-season`, the event's `colors` as `--ev1/--ev2`, a row of its `deco` emoji while the Seasonal look is on) with its sign "🎃 SPOOKY SEASON SHELF · gone in 12 days!", try-on and BUY like every prize, and a light touch on the rest of the counter (`.pz-touch`: a two-color garland along the glass case; still); between events no shelf but a small card "Coming soon: Winter Fest shelf, Dec 1" with the next event's 3 items as silhouettes (`.pz-soon`). Arcade Quest's booth lists them first during the event, tagged "SEASONAL: gone in 12 days!" (`.q-season`), and not at all otherwise. A seasonal WISH shows "gone in 12 days!" on the lobby's wish bar; if the event ends unbought, `wish()` clears it and the lobby says "The Black Cat will be back next Spooky Season." for 14 days (`wishGone()`, gone as soon as a new wish is set). The Locker / Create Your Player requirement (avatar.js): "Prize Counter: 350 tokens (Spooky Season only)" during the event, else "Returns to the Prize Counter next Spooky Season". The lobby's EVENT PANEL (season-lobby.js `shopLine`) adds "New on the Prize Counter: 3 Spooky Season prizes" under the ladder (opens the Prize Counter). Tests: tests/seasonal-shelf.spec.js (+ the smoke page "Prize Counter: seasonal shelf", `?season=spooky`, at iPad sizes and on a phone: a pages.js entry may give its own `sizes`). THE PRIZE OF THE WEEK (`weekly(date)` → {itemId, key, item, price, full, endsAt, daysLeft}): the same on every device, weeks from Monday (by calendar date, `store.today()`, so `?demo&today=` works), picked from the items sold at BOTH counters by the week's number: one seeded shuffle per round through the whole list (a round never starts with the last round's final prize), so every prize comes round before any repeats; price = round5(full × (1 − featuredDiscount)) at both counters. THE PRIZE COUNTER SCREEN (`shared/prizes.js` = `Arcade.Prizes.open({onClose})/close()/state()/openCard(key)` + `shared/prizes.css`, loaded by the floor page; tokens `--pz-*` in theme.css): an overlay (`#prizes`, `UI.layer` min 72, trap, Esc) in an original retro prize-counter look: neon PRIZES (a slow glow), the wallet, TICKET the counter bot (a boxy robot, a token-slot mouth, one antenna bulb; one line per opening from a short list, rotating per session; optional voice `prize-hello`, silent until recorded) with a counter bell, THE TOKEN COUNTER machine (the star sources in a `<details>`, "12 new ★ = 60 tokens", TURN IN: stars drop in, tokens (the season's picture; a seasonal one tumbles as it falls) spill into the tray, the balance counts up, `prize-tokens` (falls back to `quest-tokens`); no button with 0 new: "Earn stars in any game, then come back!"), the PRIZE OF THE WEEK spotlight ("PRIZE OF THE WEEK · 20 % OFF", the old price crossed out, "New prize in 3 days"; owned: "You got this week's prize!"), then the pegboard wall with shelves by full price: 400+ hanging from a rail at the top, 300–399 upper, 200–299 lower, the Manor Collection behind purple glass, 50–199 in the glass case at the front; each prize = `LockerUI.picOf` (the Locker's own thumbnail) + a paper price tag on a string, OWNED, ★ on the wish. TRY-ON: a tap opens a card (`#pzCard`, UI.layer min 76) with the avatar wearing it (live preview), name, price, BUY (yellow; disabled "Need 40 more tokens"; + a line when new stars are waiting), ☆ WISH (one wish, `gameData('prizes').wish`), CLOSE; BUY → `UI.confirm` "Buy the Jetpack for 300 tokens?" → `Tokens.buy` + `prize-win` (falls back to `skin-unlocked`) → WEAR IT NOW / KEEP SHOPPING. ENTER A CODE (Band Ninja belt codes: `BandNinja.redeem` + `Skins.catchUp`, the same as the booth's). Keys: arrows move between prizes (and the spotlight) by position, Enter opens, Esc closes the card then the counter; every control ≥ 44 px. Phones (≤ 760 px): one column, the machine + spotlight (`.pz-top`) sticky on top while the shelves scroll. Reduced motion or MOTION off (`.pz-still`): nothing moves (the count-up jumps). WHERE IT OPENS: the lobby's PRIZE COUNTER sign (lobby.js `prizeSign`: the last `.zsign`, amber, same size and flicker; the balance + "n new ★ to turn in"; the WISH bar "Wish: Jetpack · 180 / 300" with a lighter part for the stars waiting ("+60 waiting at the counter"), "You can get your wish!" + the token icon with a gentle pulse once affordable; redrawn on `arcade:tokens`), the avatar badge's menu ("<token icon> 142 tokens · Prize Counter": over the floor, or from a game page `index.html?prizes`, which arcade.js opens once and drops from the address), and the zone view's counter: 3D = a textured counter + lit PRIZES sign on the back wall (arcade3d.js, layer 1, drawn once) with a see-through `.prize3d` button placed over it (hidden when off screen or < 44 px), 2D = `.prize2d` in the aisle's top left (hidden ≤ 700 px). RESULTS: every `UI.results.show` adds "+3 ★ = 15 tokens at the Prize Counter" (`#resTokens`: the token icon + the line) when stars were earned since the page opened or the last results screen (`Tokens.resultLine`). Tests: tests/prize-counter.spec.js.
- THE TOKEN ICON: `Arcade.Tokens.iconHTML({size, pixel})` is the ONLY way to show a token (never an emoji, never a class of a page's own): `<i class="tk-ic tk-<skin>" aria-hidden="true"></i>`; `size` = px or a CSS length (default: the place's own CSS, e.g. `.pz-ov .tk-ic` 20 px, `.zs-prize .tk-ic` 18 px, else 1.15em); `pixel: true` = Arcade Quest's pixel variant (`tk-px`, sized by `#stage .tk-ic` in arcade-quest/style.css). It's decoration only: the words beside it always say "N tokens". The pictures: `shared/tokens.css` (loaded on every page with tokens.js: shared/version.js writes both tags), small original SVGs as CSS masks, every color a theme token; the plain `.tk-ic` is the coin, so an unknown skin draws the coin. `Arcade.Tokens.skin()` = the running event's `token` (shared/seasons.js, see SEASONAL TOKENS in [seasons.md](seasons.md)), else 'coin'. Used by the Prize Counter (wallet, card price, the TURN IN tray), the lobby's sign and wish line, the WEEKLY CHAMPIONS card, the badge menu, every results screen, Arcade Quest (HUD, Token Booth, Rusty, charms). A new event gets a token picture: add an SVG to tokens.css and its id in seasons.js. Tests: tests/tokens.spec.js.
