# Rhythms and counting (incl. onsets and calibration)

- RHYTHMS AND COUNTING (Rhythm Dojo; any game that shows a rhythm uses these, never its own):
  - `shared/counting.js` = MR. GRAHAM'S COUNTING. `Arcade.Counting.parse(text, time)` reads rhythms as TEXT (`w h q e s`, `.` dotted, `r` rest, `_` tied to the next note, `[e e e]` a triplet, `|` bar lines, checked) into TICKS (a quarter = 12; 6/8's eighth-note beat = 6); `groups(parsed)` → one counting group per note (a tie chain is ONE group, across bar lines too) or rest: `{big (the starting syllable), small: [{text, t}] (every grid point held through, strictly inside), rest, grid}`; `text(groups)` → `'1^(& 2) & (3^(4))'` (plain text: `^( )` = small, `( )` = rest). Syllables: 1 2 3 4 · 1 & 2 & · 1 e & a · 1 la le (triplets); 3/4 and 2/4 the same with fewer beats; 6/8 = 1 2 3 4 5 6 (the eighth gets the beat), its sixteenths 1 & 2 & …. THE RULE: a note's small syllables use the COARSEST grid (quarter → eighth → triplet → sixteenth; 6/8: eighth → sixteenth) on which both its start and its end fall. `rhythm-dojo/examples.js` = the Counting Board's examples with the counting each must show (the tests check every one).
  - `shared/rhythm-staff.js` = THE RHYTHM STAFF: `Arcade.RhythmStaff.engrave(parsed, {from, to, counting, showTime, id, groups})` → `{svg, w, h, xAt(tick), xs, bars, t0, t1}`: a ONE-LINE PERCUSSION STAFF drawing exactly the written values (stems up, beams by beat: 6/8 by dotted quarter, rests break them, sixteenths' secondary beams and stubs; flags, dots, ties under the heads (half ties at a row's edges), triplet 3s / brackets, rests, time signature, bar lines, final bar) with the counting underneath: the starting syllable full size, the held ones SMALL + RAISED with ONE underline under them only, rests in parentheses. Spacing grows with the note's length (log) and widens wherever a note's counting needs room. `rows(parsed, perRow)` splits long rhythms into rows (a tie or a counting group continues on the next row); `refine(root)` re-measures the counting with the real font once the SVG is in the page. Classes for a game's CSS: `g.rn` (a note, data-g = its group), `g.rc` (a counting group), `.rc-big`, `.rc-small` (data-t = tick), `.rc-par`, `.rc-u`; colors are currentColor. (Music Highway's notation.js is different: it spells durations itself from a melody.)
  - RUDIMENT NOTATION (sticking, accents, grace notes, rolls; for the Rudiment Trainer, added to the two files above; every existing rhythm parses, counts and draws byte-for-byte as before: `tests/rudiment-notation.spec.js` checks it against `tests/fixtures/rhythm-staff-before.json`). THE TOKEN: `{graces} value dots rest roll accent HAND tie`: HAND = `R` / `L` at the end (`sR`, `e>L`; optional); `>` = accent; `{L}` before the note = one grace (a flam: `{L}e>R`), `{RR}` = two (a drag), each letter a grace's hand in playing order; graces take NO time (no ticks, no syllable, not in the bar's total); `/` = a DOUBLE STROKE (two equal strokes, same hand, half the value each: `s/R` = R R as two 32nds); `//` and `///` parse and draw, but **their stroke meaning is undecided** (`strokes()` treats them like `/`; the Rudiment Trainer's list settles it if a rudiment needs it); `z` = a buzz stroke (one hand, not countable). ERRORS: `{X}`, a lone `{L}` (with a space before its note), `z` with `/`, more than `///`, and graces / a hand / an accent / a roll / a buzz on a rest. `parse` adds to every note `hand` ('R' | 'L' | null), `accent`, `graces` ([] / ['L'] / ['R','R']), `roll` (0–3), `buzz`. `Counting.strokes(parsed, {rolls})` → every stroke in playing order `[{n, s, t, hand, accent, grace, buzz}]`: graces s = -2, -1 with their note's `t`, the note s = 0, a roll's second stroke s = 1 at `t + d/2`; rests and tied-into notes aren't struck; the same for both roll views; no ms, no audio. ENGRAVE: `sticking: true` (R / L under each head, as large as the counting's big syllables, grace letters ~60 %; replaces the counting row with `counting: false`, else under it and the staff is 40 px taller), `rolls: 'slash'` (default: each `/` a bold diagonal about a beam thick, rising left to right, centered on the stem halfway between the head and the beam (or the stem's end; a whole note: above its head); `//` and `///` stack parallel, evenly spaced, still clear of the head and the accent) | `'written'` (each `/` note drawn as its two strokes, beamed by the beat, 32nds with a third beam and stubs; each stroke its own `g.rn` with `data-s`; in this mode the returned `notes` / `xs` are the drawn strokes); accents above the beam (above a triplet's 3: the drawing moves down, `lift`); grace notes small, stems up, left of their note with room made (one = a slashed eighth, two+ = beamed sixteenths), each group with a small SLUR under the heads from its first grace head to its own main note's head (`path.rs-gslur`, data-n): **DRAWING ONLY**, never a tie (not the `_` logic): the counting groups, `strokes()`, ticks and the parse fields never see it; `z` = an italic "z" the size of a grace sticking letter (60 % of a big one), centered on the stem at a slash's height, in both views; `strokes: [{n, s, x}]` = every drawn head (graces s < 0). Classes: `text.rs-hand` (data-n, data-s), `text.rs-hand.grace`, `path.rs-acc`, `g.rs-grace` (data-n), `path.rs-slash`, `text.rs-buzz`, `path.rs-gslur`; all currentColor. `rows()` splits by measure, so a grace never ends a row apart from its note. THE TICK DECISION: `TPQ` stays 12. A written-out 32nd is 1.5 ticks, drawn by the engraver alone at fractional positions (and `strokes()` gives that fractional `t`). Raising TPQ to 24 would have changed the drawn SVG itself (the counting's `data-t` are ticks) and needed edits in three games that time ticks as 12 a quarter (Blocktave's rhythm card `beatS / 12`, the snare's `/ 12` and `% 12`). `GALLERY=1` saves `docs/gallery/rudiment-notation.png` (the syntax examples + stacked `//` / `///`, both roll views, sticking alone and with the counting, iPad width) for Mr. Graham's review.
  - `shared/onsets.js` = CLAPS AND HITS: sharp onsets from the energy of the pre-emphasized signal in 128-sample frames (`RULES`: a frame ≥ `rise` × the background of `bgFrom`–`bgTo` ms before, above the gate = `gateK` × the sensitivity slider's gate, `refractoryMs` 85), NEVER pitch. `Arcade.Onsets.analyse(samples, sr)` (recordings, tests), `listen(fn)` → `{stop()}` (fn `{time (performance ms, sample-accurate: counted from the stream), level}`), reading the same microphone as pitch.js (`Pitch.source()`) through a ScriptProcessor, only while `Pitch.listening()` and never during `Pitch.suppress`; `watchNode(node)` (tests: the game's own output looped back), `fake(time)` (?demo keys), `log`. CLASSROOM MODE (`Pitch.classroom()`): it keeps its own room floor of the emphasized signal (frozen while not listening / suppressed) and an onset must also reach `ROOM.attackOverFloor` × that floor and `ROOM.hitShare` × the room check's `ownOnset` (`Onsets.analyse(…, {classroom, own})` for tests; `Onsets.tap(fn)` = every onset even while a game isn't listening: the room check only). See [pitch.md](pitch.md).
  - `shared/calibration.js` = THE AUDIO CLOCK + THE TIMING CHECK (moved out of Music Highway, which uses it too): `Arcade.AudioClock.create()` → `{start(), sample(), audAt(perfMs) (the AUDIBLE context time), now(), ctx, out, off}`; `Arcade.Calibration.analyse({clicks, lead, attacks, audAt, rules, accept})` → `{offs, median, spread, accepted, bleed, clickLike, ok, why}` (the median attack-to-click offset; a round that is machine-steady near 0 ms or far too early is the click itself: refused). THE SAVED DELAY (one per device, shared by Rhythm Dojo and Showtime Malfunction's snare): `Calibration.saved(key)` / `store(key, res)` = `gameData('rhythm-dojo').calib[key]` ('clap' | 'tap'; the snare uses 'clap'), `lag(key)` = the saved ms, else `RD_RULES.defaultLag`, else `DEFAULT_LAG`.
  - `shared/rhythm-judge.js` = THE RHYTHM JUDGE (moved out of Rhythm Dojo, same results): `Arcade.RhythmJudge.match(targets, attacks, {perfectMs, goodMs, okMs, lateMs})` → each attack claims the nearest unjudged note within `lateMs` (PERFECT / GOOD / OK / EARLY / LATE), leftover notes MISS, leftover attacks EXTRA. Rhythm Dojo and the snare's rhythm/accent machines use it; never a second judge.
  - `shared/timing-check.js` = THE TIMING CHECK FLOW (moved out of Rhythm Dojo): `Arcade.TimingCheck.open({els: {panel, title, msg, dots, say, pad, go, skip}, tap, first, rules, clock, kit, key, gameId, words, onClose(ok)})` (4 clicks to listen to, then 8 to clap/tap on; `Sfx.outputRaw` clicks; `Calibration.analyse`, then `store(key)`), `state()` = `{clicks, last, running}`. Each game passes its own panel's elements (Dojo `#cal*`, Showtime `#stCal*`); the words default to Dojo's.
