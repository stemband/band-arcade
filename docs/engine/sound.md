# Sound

## Sound while listening mutes the detector (the full hard rule)

- **Sound while listening mutes the detector.** Every sound goes through `shared/sfx.js` (loaded by EVERY page, after `sounds.js`) and respects the mute and volume settings. A sound played while a game listens to the microphone (`Pitch.listening()`) automatically calls `Pitch.suppress(sound length + 250 ms)`: nothing heard in that window counts as a hit, a wrong note or toward a hold (`onHeld` never fires, `onFrame` gets `null`), and a note still sounding afterwards needs a fresh attack (the `ignoreCurrent` behavior). Every game timer or reaction clock PAUSES during the window (`Pitch.isSuppressed(now)` each frame: Ghost Notes' note clock; Neon Face-Off shows the receiver's note only after `Pitch.suppressedUntil`, stretches the puck so they still get their full window, and freezes the puck + reaction clock during later windows; Vanishing Ink's answer clock (`Echo.create({pauseSuppressed: true})`); its study clock does not pause, since nothing is answered while the ink shows). EXCEPT MUSIC HIGHWAY, whose backing groove plays WHILE listening and never mutes the mic (it plays straight into `Sfx.output()`, never through `Sfx.event`; the drums are unpitched noise + a 70 ms kick, a hit counts only with the right pitch, a snare player's hit on a backing drum hit must beat the learned bleed level; no sound effects during a song), EXCEPT RHYTHM DOJO, whose CLICK (the count-in, and in TAP or headphones mode the whole beat) and the TAP pad's woodblock play straight into `Sfx.output()` through music-highway/backing.js's kit, never through `Sfx.event`, and never mute the mic: claps heard before the rhythm's first note (count-in) never count, and a clap within `bleedMs` of a click (shifted by how late the count-in's clicks were heard: the median of the onsets within `bleedLearnMs` of a click, `clickLag`) must beat `bleedK` × the loudest click-time onset heard during the count-in (both unpitched noise; its "Ready!" `rd-count-in` ends before it listens), and EXCEPT NOTE STORM, which never pauses (see its section: [note-storm.md](../games/note-storm.md)): it sets `Sfx.muteMax` (only the first ms of any sound mutes the mic) and `Pitch.softSuppress` (a note started during the window counts as soon as it ends; a note already counted and still held never counts twice). "During play" sounds (`play: true` in sounds.js: note-hit/wrong/missed, life-lost, puck sounds, your-turn…) must be under 0.5 s. Never play a sound per note in the Note Checker (it would deafen the detector mid-scale; only `all-notes-found`). Mic-free games (Note Ninja, Chime Heist, Ancient Ninja Scrolls, Button Masher, Dojo Duel, Keys to the City and Blocktave in touch mode) play freely: no suppression happens when nothing is listening. `mic: false` sounds (the ambience, the floor's) never play while listening.

## The sound engine

- SOUND (`shared/sounds.js` = THE SOUND LIST, `shared/sfx.js` = the player; both on every page, sounds.js first; Mat's files in `shared/sounds/<file>.m4a|.mp3`, listed with names, screens, timing and lengths in `shared/sounds/README.md`, which must list every event). sounds.js: `Arcade.Sounds.LIST[event] = {file, vol, loop, mic, play, screen, when, len, gen?, fallback?}`, `Sounds.add({...})` (a game adds its own events without editing sfx.js; `gen` = its built-in fallback beeps), `Sounds.get(name)` (makes `select-<gameId>` for every game in games.js, falling back to `select-default`), `Sounds.names()`, `Sounds.SCREENS`. sfx.js: `Arcade.Sfx.event(name)` → seconds played (0 = muted / before the first tap / not allowed): the file (.m4a then .mp3, fetched + decoded, or an `<audio>` element on file://) → its `fallback` event's file → the built-in sound for that action (`EVENTS`, the old generated sounds; `CURRENT` maps new names to old sounds: wheel-* = whoosh, select-default = coin, player-continue = player-select, ui-toggle = blip, lobby-ambience = the hum) → a generic retro beep (`GENERIC`). Missing files: `console.info` only (once per tab). An EFFECT's 404 (or undecodable file) is remembered in sessionStorage for the tab for 10 minutes (`MISS_MS`, then asked again: iPad home-screen apps keep a "tab" for days); MUSIC (loop) files are never remembered as missing, and a music 404 is asked again with `cache: 'no-cache'` (a file uploaded after a browser cached its GitHub Pages 404 must still play: music has no built-in fallback). Network errors are never remembered; wanted music retries after one (3 tries). A file is missing only after BOTH .m4a and .mp3 fail. After the first unlock the wanted track loads first, then the other preloaded music one file at a time. The unlock listeners re-arm whenever the AudioContext stops running (iPad: another app, a refused resume), so the next tap resumes everything. `?debug` on any page = the SOUND LOG (console + a small box on the page: the track wanted, every URL tried for music AND effects, loaded/decode/404/skipped, start/stop, why it's waiting, and why an effect played its fallback: not downloaded yet / still downloading / missing; `Sfx.musicLog`). `Sfx.sequence([names], gap = 80, {channel, maxStep})` (each when the last ENDS: an effect's length is where its sound ends, trailing silence not counted (`audibleEnd`; the Sound Board flags > 0.5 s of silence); a VOICE line waits until any voice speaking has finished; `maxStep` ms caps a wait; returns `{cancel()}`), `Sfx.cancelAll(channel)` (drops pending sequences; a channel includes its `'<channel>:…'` sub-channels), `Sfx.pending(channel)`, `Sfx.hush()` (fades out, 0.15 s, any VOICE still speaking: a new scene), `Sfx.preloadScreen(screen)` (load + decode every effect of that sounds.js screen now: a game's setup screen). NOTHING PLAYS LATE: a sound not decoded yet plays its generated fallback at once, and while the AudioContext is stopped (iPad interruptions) sounds are SKIPPED (except `NOW_MS` 400 ms after a tap asked it to start), never scheduled to burst out on resume; on file:// an `<audio>` copy that can't start within 400 ms is dropped. sounds.js `voice: true` (the Sensei, the countdown voice, the announcer) = never over itself (`maxInstances` 1; any sound can set `maxInstances`), `busy('voice')`, `Sfx.use(...screens)` (preload this page's sounds after the first tap, 2 at a time; mountTopbar calls `use('game', gameId)`), `Sfx.playThenGo(name, href)` (changes page when the sound ends, ≤ 1.5 s: the floor's START plays `select-<gameId>` THERE, never on the game page; it waits for a still-downloading file like `eventSoon`), `Sfx.prefer(names)` (small effects loaded NOW, ahead of the queue; before the first tap they're downloaded on their own, a missing .m4a remembered, and decoded on unlock: the floor prefers the front cabinet's `select-<id>` and its two neighbors on every turn, because the `select-<id>` events come LAST in the normal preload order), `Sfx.duck(ms, {level = 0.4, down = 0.2, up = 0.5})` (voice lines: dips the MUSIC and AMBIENCE channels to `level` over `down` s, holds ms, back up over `up` s; its own gain node after each channel's slider, so mute/sliders never undo it; overlapping ducks keep the later end; `musicState()` reports `duck`), `Sfx.busy()` (ms until the last effect played ends), `Sfx.started` (a tap on this page has started the audio), `Sfx.eventSoon(name, ms = 600)` (like `event`, but waits up to ms for a file still downloading instead of playing the fallback: the floor's START), THE MUSIC MANAGER (`CH` in sfx.js: two channels, MUSIC on `musVol` and AMBIENCE on `ambVol`, each with a WANTED track and what's playing): `Arcade.sfx.setMusic(name | [names best first] | null, {builtIn})` / `setAmbience(...)` any time (before the first tap, before the file has loaded); `apply()` starts it with a fade-in as soon as the audio is unlocked AND the file is decoded (files wanted before the first tap are fetched early and decoded on unlock), never drops a request, never restarts the track already playing, crossfades (0.5 s) to a different one, falls back to the next name / the built-in version (`builtIn`: the room hum, the original chiptune `CHIP` rendered once with OfflineAudioContext) / silence, respects mute and the sliders, stops while listening (`Sfx.sync()` after listening starts or stops) and pauses with a hidden tab (the context is suspended). `Sfx.preloadMusic([names])` fetches likely next tracks; `Sfx.musicState()` for tests. Who wants what: the floor `setAmbience('lobby-ambience', {builtIn: true})` (+ preloads `select-music` and the front cabinet's `select-music-<id>`), the select view `setAmbience(null)` + `setMusic(['select-music-<id>', 'select-music'], {builtIn: true})` (null as the game opens), Arcade Quest file-only tracks per scene/room; Lost Signal `lost-signal-music` on its level screens only; every other game its GAME MENU MUSIC: `Sfx.gameMenuMusic(gameId, on = true, {afterEffects})` = games.js `menuMusic` (the 9 `<game>-menu` loops), else `select-music` (else the built-in chiptune), fading in 0.8 s (`MENU_XF`) on menu screens (hub, mode picker, intro panels, results after their sounds: `afterEffects`); a listening game's mic is PAUSED on menus (`Pitch.pauseListening`) so music can play; `false` at every level start: the music fades out 0.5 s (`MENU_FADE`) while the mic is back but hears nothing new (`Pitch.suppress`). Games with `menuMusicOwn` (Dojo Duel, Lost Signal, Vanishing Ink, Arcade Quest) run their own music and never call it. `playThenGo` fades music + ambience 0.8 s as the page changes (the wish stays for a Back-button restore); the select view's music fades 0.8 s as a game opens. The Sound Board's MUSIC section lists every menu track (✓ uploaded / missing — what plays instead) with Play/Stop. File loops get a baked crossfade so they wrap seamlessly (`select-music-<id>` counts as a loop file), `Sfx.mountControls(el, {lobby})` (the top bar's SETTINGS button, a speaker showing sound on/off: it opens THE SETTINGS PANEL of the UI kit; mountTopbar mounts it on every game; `Sfx.settingsChanged(k, done)`, `loopNote()`, `refreshControls()` are what the panel calls), `Sfx.outputRaw()` (the effects output WITHOUT the EFFECTS slider, SOUND ON/OFF still mutes: only Music Highway's calibration clicks), `Sfx.bell(soundingMidi)` (Chime Heist's bars: ALWAYS generated, never files), `Sfx.piano(soundingMidi)` (Keys to the City's touch-mode keys: a generated piano note with a hammer tap and a natural decay; returns false and plays nothing while a microphone listens), `Sfx.history` (last 60 plays: tests), `Sfx.board` (the Sound Board page, `sound-board/index.html`: not linked from the arcade; every section (Music, the Arcade Quest rooms, one per `Sounds.SCREENS` screen) is a `<details>` that starts COLLAPSED, its summary = the name, "· 24 sounds" and a "⚠ 2 missing, 1 too long" badge from the rows' statuses; EXPAND ALL / COLLAPSE ALL; a SEARCH box (name, what it's for, file, screen: matching sections open, the rest hide, "No sounds match", clearing restores what was open); the open sections are remembered for the browser session (sessionStorage `bandarcade.sb-open`); `sound-board/index.html#<sound name>` opens its section and scrolls to its row, highlighted; `Arcade.SoundBoard.state()` for tests (tests/sound-board.spec.js); it checks every file on open and with RELOAD ALL SOUNDS, always bypassing caches: `board.load(name)` = `fetch(url + '&t=<now>', {cache: 'reload'})`, one download per file per check). CACHING: every sound URL is `<file>.<ext>?v=<SOUNDS_VERSION>` (the `var` at the very top of sounds.js, `Sounds.VERSION`); games use the normal browser cache (never no-cache/reload there), and Mat bumps SOUNDS_VERSION by 1 after replacing or deleting sounds. The per-tab missing-file memory is keyed by the versioned URL, so a bump clears it too. The AudioContext starts on the first pointerdown/keydown (the one exception: `Sfx.autoStart(ms)`, PRESS START's check on load: it makes the context, which stays suspended until the first tap unless the browser already allows sound, and resolves true if it is running within ms). Settings: `store.sfx`/`setSfx(on)` (default on), `store.sfxVol` / `store.musVol` / `store.ambVol` / `setVolume(k, 0–1)` (defaults 0.6, 0.4 and 0.3; `store.ambience` = ambVol > 0). Events wired today: floor `wheel-left`, `wheel-right`, `cabinet-focus`, `select-<id>`, `zone-select` (a zone sign tapped), `zone-enter` (its cabinets appear), `zone-back` (BACK / ← LOBBY), `all-games-open`, the Prize Counter's `prize-hello` (optional voice), `prize-tokens`, `prize-win`; Select Player `select-music` (loop), `choose-instrument` (the announcer, as the view opens), `tile-move`, `player-select`, `player-continue`, `player-ready`, `player2-join`, `skin-equip`, `skin-unlocked`; `ui-back` (← ARCADE), `ui-toggle` (sound, horn, NOTES × ORDER); Create Your Player `avatar-open` (the editor opens), `avatar-change` (any option), `avatar-randomize` (SURPRISE ME, SHUFFLE, random name), `avatar-save` (DONE); `item-unlocked` (an UNLOCKED! card with player items; falls back to skin-unlocked), Arcade Quest `item-purchase` (Token Booth: a player item or a charm; falls back to quest-tokens) and `charm-equip` (CHARMS panel, a charm reward; falls back to skin-equip); every game's `level-start`, `note-hit`, `note-wrong`, `note-missed`, `level-complete`, `level-failed`, `star-earned`, `new-high-score`; Note Storm `life-lost`, `game-over`; Endless mode `endless-start`, `speed-up`, `endless-life-lost`, `endless-game-over`, `endless-high-score`; Note Checker `all-notes-found`; plus each game's own (sounds.js). A new sound event goes in sounds.js AND shared/sounds/README.md.

## More of the sound engine

- MUSIC THAT RESUMES: a loop with `resume: true` in sounds.js remembers where it was when it stopped (this page's session: `Sfx.trackPos()`), and starts there next time (Arcade Quest's room music after a battle or the microphone). `setMusic(names, {fade})`: the crossfade for this change (s).
- `Arcade.Sfx.announce(name, {key, gap, delay, alive(), hold(), onPlay})` (sfx.js): THE ANNOUNCER used by choose-instrument and select-level (see [page-flow.md](page-flow.md) and [press-start-and-level-select.md](press-start-and-level-select.md)); returns {cancel()}.
- THE COUNTDOWN (`shared/countdown.js`, loaded after sfx.js by Dojo Duel, Neon Face-Off and Sustain Speedway): `Arcade.countdown({style, voicePrefix, later, show, onGo, steps, go, delay})` = the beats and their sounds only (each game draws its own numbers in `show(label, kind)`, kind 'count'|'ready'|'go', and runs the timers on its own clock with `later(ms, fn)`). Styles: CLASSIC (3-2-1, `steps.classic` 1000 ms, spoken `<prefix>-count-3/-2/-1`), QUICK (0.35 s steps, the `<prefix>-count` tick, else `dojo-count`), READY-GO ("READY…" + `<prefix>-ready`, then "GO!", `steps.ready` 450 ms each), OFF (a `steps.off` pause). `go: true` = a spoken "GO!" beat (`<prefix>-count-go`) whose voice ENDS before `onGo` (at most `steps.goMax`), so it never overlaps the moment a player must play; Dojo Duel uses `go: false` (its note's own "Go!"). Voice prefixes 'dojo', 'faceoff' and 'race' (every faceoff-* / race-* sound falls back to the matching dojo-count-* file, then the dojo-count tick; a new prefix needs only its sounds.js entries). `countdown.sounds(prefix)` = every sound a CLASSIC + GO countdown can play, fallbacks included (for `Sfx.prefer` / `Sfx.whenReady`). Beats never exceed 3 a second; games only scale numbers in (no flash), steady under reduced motion.
- sounds.js `cap` (dB, the LOUDNESS CAP; Showtime Malfunction's scare stings): sfx.js scales the file so its peak and its loudest 50 ms (RMS) stay within cap dB of the arcade's normal loudest effect (the loudest uncapped file played on the page, at least `REF_PEAK` .9 / `REF_RMS` .38, measured from shared/sounds), then a hard limiter (WaveShaper) at the peak limit; the generated fallback goes through the same limiter; `Sfx.loudness()` for tests. TEACHER SETTINGS: `shared/teacher-settings.js` (`Arcade.TEACHER`: switches Mat edits, never saved on a device; today `JUMP_SCARE_ALLOWED`).
- `Sfx.event(name, {muteCap})`: this play mutes the microphone at most `muteCap` ms, echo included (Neon Face-Off's in-rally sounds); the sound itself plays on. sounds.js `maxLen` (s): the Sound Board warns when the uploaded file is longer ("Trim this sound to under 0.3 s for the snappiest rallies").

## The sound files: automatic compression

- `tools/compress-sounds.py` (ffmpeg) shrinks `shared/sounds/*.mp3|.m4a` IN PLACE: same name, same container (.mp3 → LAME CBR with the gapless delay/padding header; .m4a → AAC with the priming edit list), so no code or sounds.js change. TARGETS (constants at the top of the script): MUSIC (`loop: true` in sounds.js, `select-music-<game id>`, or over 20 s) 112 kbps, its channels kept; VOICE (`voice: true`) 64 kbps mono; every other EFFECT 80 kbps mono when its left/right correlation is > 0.98, else 96 kbps stereo. 48 kHz → 44.1 kHz; a lower rate is kept. Music and effects keep their treble to 19 kHz (the encoders' default low-pass made bright clicks quieter).
- RULES: a file at or below its target (+5 %) is never touched (byte-for-byte); a new encode replaces it only if ≥ 15 % smaller AND its decoded length is within 30 ms AND its total energy within 1 dB (never normalized: the loudness cap does levels) AND, for music, neither end gains more than 10 ms of silence (sfx.js `loopPoints` trims each end's silence below 0.0015, so more would change the loop's length at the seam) and an end that had sound in its first/last 20 ms still has. A file failing at its target gets one retry a step higher (80 → 112, 96 → 128, 64 → 96, 112 → 160); still failing = left as uploaded, and reported. A compressed file carries the comment tag `compressed by tools/compress-sounds.py` and is never re-encoded (no generation loss; `--force` overrides after changing a target).
- `--dry-run` (encodes to a temporary folder and prints the table), `--check` (exits 1 if any file would still be replaced), `--verify` (every file decodes; a processed file is within 30 ms of its upload's length; loop ends), `--changed-since <commit>`, or file names. A real run writes `tests/fixtures/sound-durations.json`: each sound's UPLOAD length (and, for music, `head`/`tail`: sound in its first/last 20 ms), measured before compressing, plus `sha` (a fingerprint of the file afterwards) so the tests can tell a processed file from a new upload.
- AUTOMATIC (`.github/workflows/compress-sounds.yml`): a push to main changing `shared/sounds/**.mp3|.m4a` → ffmpeg → the script on ONLY the changed files → commits "Compress uploaded sounds [skip ci]" as github-actions[bot] (no loop: [skip ci], and the job skips the bot's commits) → `gh workflow run pages.yml`. pages.yml's `sounds` job skips its own deploy for such a push (the same file patterns), so the live site only ever gets the small file. tests.yml's `sounds` job: compresses the change's new sounds in its workspace, then `--check` + `--verify`.
- THE ONE-TIME RE-ENCODE (this change): 221 files, 77.9 MB → 42.2 MB; 195 compressed. Left as they were: `blocktave-menu.mp3` and `quest-credits.mp3` (loops whose edges sit right at sfx.js's silence threshold, so 112/160 kbps moved the trim point 14 / 95 ms), the 128 kbps AAC menu loops (112 kbps saves only 8–13 %), and a few tiny effects. Re-export those two loops by hand at about 112 kbps to shrink them.
- Devices: the service worker's FILES fingerprints change with the files, so an installed app downloads each smaller sound once, the next time it is used after the new version ([version-and-app.md](version-and-app.md)). SOUNDS_VERSION wasn't bumped: the old and new files sound the same.

## Tones

- TONES (`shared/tones.js`, LOST SIGNAL ONLY, see the Hard rules in [CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md)): `Arcade.tones.play(soundingMidis, {noteMs, gapMs, vol, onNote(i, midi)})` → `{dur, done (Promise), stop(), muted}` (a soft triangle + a touch of square, A440 equal temperament, via `Sfx.output()` = `{ctx, out}` once unlocked and sound is on, else `muted`), `tones.test()`, `tones.freq(midi)`, `tones.history` (tests). sounds.js `gen` entries may use type `'noise'` (`[freq | [from, to], at, len, vol, 'noise', q]`: band-passed noise, unpitched).
