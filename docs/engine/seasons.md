# Seasonal events and seasonal looks

- SEASONAL EVENTS (`shared/seasons.js`, loaded after skins.js on every page with skins; `season-lobby.js` on the floor; styles `.ev-*` in arcade.css): EVENT ITEMS ARE EARNED, SEASONAL SHOP ITEMS ARE BOUGHT: NEVER MIX THEM (an event's gift and ladder items, `unlock: {event}`, are never sold at either counter; the seasonal shop's own items, `unlock: {shop, season}`, are never a gift or a ladder step: see THE PRIZE COUNTER in [prize-counter.md](prize-counter.md)). Limited-time windows when special avatar items can be EARNED; earned items are owned forever (`store.ownItem`, like the Token Booth's) and wearable all year; each event returns every year. THE CALENDAR = `Arcade.SEASONS` at the top of seasons.js (Mat edits it): `{id (never rename), name, emoji, start, end ('MM-DD' every year, inclusive, device's local date, may cross New Year; 'YYYY-MM-DD' = a one-time event such as the commented Concert Week example, which wins over a yearly one on the same days), colors [main, accent] (tokens), deco, token (THE TOKEN'S PICTURE during the event, see SEASONAL TOKENS below), jingle, gift: '<field>:<id>', ladder: [{do: 'stars'|'levels'|'games'|'endless'|'days', n, item}] (3–5 steps), bonus (optional), bonusEmoji (optional)}`. THE BONUS LADDER (`bonus`, OPTIONAL, any event can have one; same step format as `ladder`, harder): it OPENS when every step of the main ladder is done, and its progress COUNTS THE WHOLE EVENT (the same activity log from the event's first day), so nobody starts again from zero; `Seasons.bonusOpen(o)`, `bonusSteps(o)` (with `bonus: true`, open or not), `allSteps(o)` (main + bonus once open: what `check()` pays out and the banner counts, "7 of 11"); a bonus item's requirement during the event is "Spooky Season bonus: Earn 50 ★" (the same "Returns next Spooky Season!" after). The EVENT PANEL shows "BONUS CHALLENGES" + `bonusEmoji` under the challenges: locked = one line ("Finish all 5 challenges to unlock 6 bonus challenges!") + the items as dark silhouettes (`.ev-sil`, the avatar wearing each one, no background), open = the same step rows. Spooky Season's bonus (👻): Play 8 different games → Glowing jack-o'-lantern (`hand:jacklantern`), Earn 30 ★ → Mummy wraps (`top:mummywraps`), Clear 15 levels → Vampire cape (`back:vampcape`), Play on 8 different days → Floating bats (`effect:floatbats`), Score 1,500 in any Endless mode → Candy Corn name plate (`plate:candycorn`), Earn 50 ★ → Lil' Reaper (`pet:reaper`). Step labels write big numbers with commas (1,500). Events: Spooky Season 10-01→11-01, Winter Fest 12-01→01-07, Friendship Week 02-07→02-14, Music In Our Schools Month 03-01→03-31, Spring Bloom 04-01→04-30, Summer Send-Off 05-01→05-22. THE ITEMS are parts in avatar-parts.js's SEASONAL ITEMS section with `unlock: {event: '<id>'}` (avatar.js: owned = unlocked; `requirement` = "Spooky Season: Play 3 different games" during it, "Returns next Spooky Season!" otherwise; never sold at the Token Booth; identity items are never event items) and in avatar-code.js TABLE. PROGRESS = THE DAILY ACTIVITY LOG in storage.js (`store.activity` `{'YYYY-MM-DD': {s stars, c levels cleared, g {game: 1}, e best Endless score, p plays, f {game: 1} rounds FINISHED (Today's Practice only, never counted by events: see [progress-and-saving.md](progress-and-saving.md))}}`, 400 days, in the Arcade Backup Code): `setLevel(game, inst, lvl, p, run)` (every game passes `run` = THIS play's stars: a level with ≥ 1 is cleared), `addEndless` (score), `noteActivity({game, play})` (LevelSelect.played, Dojo Duel / Neon Face-Off match start, an Arcade Quest battle). An event's progress = the log's days inside its dates, so only what happens during it counts and every year starts fresh. `Seasons.check()` earns finished steps (the running event + one that ended ≤ 14 days ago); skins.js calls it before every UNLOCKED! card (results screens, Select Player), so the card celebrates it (its line: "🎃 Spooky Season: yours forever"). The FREE GIFT is claimed in the lobby's EVENT PANEL (`gameData('seasons').claimed[occurrence key]`). THE LOBBY (season-lobby.js): the banner ("🎃 SPOOKY SEASON: 6 days left!", FREE GIFT! / "2 of 5"; a `countdown: true` event: "🎻 Concert in 5 days!" … "Concert tonight!", `Seasons.leftText`) above the lobby cards opens the EVENT PANEL (gift + CLAIM, the ladder with progress bars, every item previewed on the student's avatar, the SEASONAL LOOK switch); the event's jingle (sounds.js `event-<id>-jingle`, built-in fallback) plays on the banner tap and CLAIM only; a touch on each sign (`.lobby[data-deco]`, the event's `deco`) shows with the look. TESTING: `?season=<id>` previews an event today (banner, panel, look, items; progress = today's activity; claims/earned items/seen cards in sessionStorage only: nothing saved); `?demo&today=YYYY-MM-DD` pretends that date (saved normally); `?demo&unlockall` opens every item. `Arcade.Seasons` (`active/steps/progress/claim/check/requirement/owned/occurrences/look/backdrop/lookOn/setLookOn/leftText`) and `Arcade.SeasonLobby.state()` (+ `bonus`, `bonusOpen`, `count` = the banner's "10 of 11") for tests: tests/spooky-bonus.spec.js.
- SEASONAL LOOKS (`season-look.js`, the floor page only; styles "SEASONAL LOOKS" at the end of arcade.css; a few `--sl-*` tokens in theme.css): while a season runs, the arcade's MENUS get a full themed backdrop: the lobby, a zone, ALL GAMES, the FULL ARCADE (2D and 3D), Choose Your Instrument and PRESS START; NEVER a game page. WHICH look = `Seasons.look(date)`: the running EVENT's `look` (every event has one: spooky, winter, friendship, miosm, spring, summer; a concert: concert), else a BACKGROUND-ONLY SEASON's (`Arcade.SEASON_BACKDROPS` in seasons.js, Mat edits it: `{id, name, look, dates: [['MM-DD', 'MM-DD'], …]}`: Back to School 08-01→09-30 (school), Fall Harvest 11-02→11-30 (harvest), Frost 01-08→02-06 + 02-15→02-28 (frost)); an event always wins. CONCERT SEASON = the commented template at the end of `Arcade.SEASONS` (one-time 'YYYY-MM-DD' dates, `end` = the concert day, `look: 'concert'`, `countdown: true`, challenges without items). THE SWITCH "Seasonal look: On / Off" (`Seasons.lookOn()`/`setLookOn(on)`, `gameData('seasons').look`, ON by default, fires window `arcade:seasonlook`) is in the shared Settings panel (ui-kit.js, on any page with seasons.js) and the event panel; it REPLACED the DECORATIONS switch: an old `deco: false` reads as Off, and setting it deletes `deco`. Off = the normal arcade everywhere (the banner, panel and items still work). `?season=<id>` previews an event, a background-only season or a look by name (`?season=concert`), nothing saved, even with the switch off. THE DRAWING: one `.slook` (`sl-sky` gradients, `sl-p` props = small inline SVGs at the edges, `sl-fx` a few drifting bits, `sl-dim` darkening the middle so every text keeps its contrast) behind the floor (before `.room`; `html[data-slook]` makes the body transparent and drops the room's haze; the 3D canvas is see-through) and a copy inside `#pressStart` and `#selectView`; THE FRONT LAYER: beside each `.slook` a `.slook-front` (body: right AFTER `.room`; the copies: right after their `.slook`), the same fixed full-screen box at `z-index: -1`: later in the page than `.room`, so it paints over the room's checkered floor (and the backdrop's fog), and under everything else (signs, cards, the top bar, ALL GAMES, the carousel, panels, toasts are all in the normal flow or above 0); `pointer-events: none`, `aria-hidden`. It holds only the props marked `{front: true}` (the 4th item of a prop: `[svg, css, class, {front: true}]`): the things that stand ON the floor, so its lines and the fog never cross them. Front props today: spooky's three jack-o'-lanterns, winter's snow hills, Music In Our Schools Month's two drums, spring's grass, summer's Ferris wheel, palm, boardwalk and wave, the concert's footlights, harvest's three wheat stalks and two gourds (friendship, school and frost have none: nothing of theirs stands on the floor). WHERE IT HIDES: in a ZONE and the FULL ARCADE (2D and 3D; `body.v-zone`) the front layer is hidden and the backdrop shows its own copies of those props (`.sl-fb`, hidden everywhere else), so they can never cover a cabinet or the QUICK JUMP strip; the lobby, ALL GAMES, PRESS START and Choose Your Instrument show the front layer. THE SPOOKY SCENE (Spooky Season): THE MOON is the hero and Mr. Graham's favorite: KEEP ITS DRAWING (the same SVG, craters, colors and glow; only its size and place may change): `--mw: clamp(128px,22vmin,280px)` (2× the old size), `right: 4%`, its top = `--sl-top` (season-look.js `place()`: the lowest of 12 % of the screen, the top bar's bottom + 10 px, and the bottom of any text WITHOUT its own solid background (the neon sign, its tagline, ALL GAMES' count, Choose Your Instrument's title…) in the moon's column in the top half; measured again on a view change, new content or a resize), so no bare text ever sits on the light moon (it moves, it's never dimmed); the sky's purple patch sits behind it and grows with it. THE BAT ACROSS THE MOON (`sl-mbat`, the `fx` bat's drawing, bigger): every 20–35 s (a timer adds `.fly`) one bat crosses the disc left to right on a gentle arc in 3 s, a slow flap (scaleY, 400 ms each way), in front of the moon and behind the hall; never with reduced motion or MOTION off (not drawn at all). THE HAUNTED BAND HALL (`SVG.bandhall`, `.sl-hall`): a cute silhouette (`--purple-ink` into `--deep`) on a hilltop in front of the moon's lower third: a crooked bell tower with an eighth-note weathervane, a bandshell arch over the front, arched windows glowing amber (breathing .55 ↔ .85 over 6 s, staggered), a treble-clef window, a crooked path down the hill; the hill's foot fades into the fog (`::after`); `--hw: min(clamp(150px,26vmin,330px), 25vw)`, its left edge always ≥ 70 % of the width (never in the middle 40 %: it shrinks on narrow phones). THE JACK-O'-LANTERNS (front layer): a soft dark ground shadow (`.pks`), the body, a warm candle glow behind the carved face (`.pkg`, `--amber`/`--yellow`, opacity .6 ↔ .9 over 4.6 s, each pumpkin its own phase `--ph`), the face (`.pf`) brightening with it; still = a steady glow (.75). Purple fog (back layer, behind the pumpkins) and the drifting `fx` bats as before; strips along the top (string lights, bunting) are fixed-shape pieces in a row, never stretched. 3D: `SeasonLook.palette()` → the haze and side lights' tokens, `Floor3D.look()` applies it (again on a change). MOTION: transforms/opacity only, slow (≥ 4 s cycles; lights breathe a strand at a time, never blink); still with reduced motion or MOTION off (`html.slook-still`). No `filter`, no pictures, no SVG ids (each scene is drawn three times). A new look = a `LOOKS` entry in season-look.js (props standing on the floor get `{front: true}`) + its colors in arcade.css + its id in `Seasons.LOOK_IDS`. `Arcade.SeasonLook.state()` (`hosts`, `fronts`), `.front(id)` (a look's front props), `.place()`, `.batPass()` for tests: tests/seasonal-looks.spec.js (the spooky scene at phone, iPad portrait/landscape, Chromebook, a big screen and phone landscape: the pumpkins on top of the floor and the fog via `elementsFromPoint`, the front layer hidden in a zone and the FULL ARCADE (2D and 3D), the moon's width 2× the old clamp and its top under the top bar, no bare text on the moon, the hall over the moon's lower part and out of the middle 40 %, the top bar's text and the zone signs ≥ 4.5:1 against what's really behind them (their own background, else the backdrop's brightest pixel), the bat's pass on `page.clock` and none with reduced motion (nothing in either layer animates), the slow candle and window breathing; `GALLERY=1` saves `docs/gallery/season-spooky-*.jpg`: the lobby and a zone at the four sizes, PRESS START and Choose Your Instrument, shown in docs/gallery.html's SEASONAL LOOKS).
- SEASONAL TOKENS (the `token` field of each event in `Arcade.SEASONS`; the pictures in `shared/tokens.css`; `Arcade.Tokens.skin()` / `iconHTML()` in shared/tokens.js, see THE TOKEN ICON in [prize-counter.md](prize-counter.md)): while an event runs, the arcade token's PICTURE changes everywhere it appears (the Prize Counter, its TURN IN tray, the lobby's sign and wish line, the badge menu, every results screen, Arcade Quest's pixel coin); the same tokens, balance and prices. `token`: spooky `candycorn` (white tip, orange middle, amber-yellow base), winter `snowflake` (a pale-blue coin, a white six-point snowflake), friendship `candyheart`, miosm `goldnote` (a gold coin with an eighth note), spring `blossom` (five pink petals), summer `seashell` (a peach scallop); the Concert template has `goldnote`. No `token`, an id without a picture, no event, or a BACKGROUND-ONLY season (school, harvest, frost; also `?season=frost`) = the normal `coin`. `?season=<event>` previews it, `?demo&today=` dates it. Every page that can show tokens loads seasons.js (tests/tokens.spec.js checks it), so every page shows the same picture on the same day; the picture is chosen when the HTML is written (no swap after load). A NEW EVENT GETS A TOKEN PICTURE: add an SVG to tokens.css (`.tk-<id>`, + `.tk-px.tk-<id>` for Arcade Quest) and its id in seasons.js (`token: '<id>'`). Tests: tests/tokens.spec.js (GALLERY=1 saves the sheet of every skin, docs/gallery/tokens.png; the UI gallery docs/gallery.html shows it live).
