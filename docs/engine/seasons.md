# Seasonal events and seasonal looks

- SEASONAL EVENTS (`shared/seasons.js`, loaded after skins.js on every page with skins; `season-lobby.js` on the floor; styles `.ev-*` in arcade.css): EVENT ITEMS ARE EARNED, SEASONAL SHOP ITEMS ARE BOUGHT: NEVER MIX THEM (an event's gift and ladder items, `unlock: {event}`, are never sold at either counter; the seasonal shop's own items, `unlock: {shop, season}`, are never a gift or a ladder step: see THE PRIZE COUNTER in [prize-counter.md](prize-counter.md)). Limited-time windows when special avatar items can be EARNED; earned items are owned forever (`store.ownItem`, like the Token Booth's) and wearable all year; each event returns every year. THE CALENDAR = `Arcade.SEASONS` at the top of seasons.js (Mat edits it): `{id (never rename), name, emoji, start, end ('MM-DD' every year, inclusive, device's local date, may cross New Year; 'YYYY-MM-DD' = a one-time event such as the commented Concert Week example, which wins over a yearly one on the same days), colors [main, accent] (tokens), deco, jingle, gift: '<field>:<id>', ladder: [{do: 'stars'|'levels'|'games'|'endless'|'days', n, item}] (3–5 steps), bonus (optional), bonusEmoji (optional)}`. THE BONUS LADDER (`bonus`, OPTIONAL, any event can have one; same step format as `ladder`, harder): it OPENS when every step of the main ladder is done, and its progress COUNTS THE WHOLE EVENT (the same activity log from the event's first day), so nobody starts again from zero; `Seasons.bonusOpen(o)`, `bonusSteps(o)` (with `bonus: true`, open or not), `allSteps(o)` (main + bonus once open: what `check()` pays out and the banner counts, "7 of 11"); a bonus item's requirement during the event is "Spooky Season bonus: Earn 50 ★" (the same "Returns next Spooky Season!" after). The EVENT PANEL shows "BONUS CHALLENGES" + `bonusEmoji` under the challenges: locked = one line ("Finish all 5 challenges to unlock 6 bonus challenges!") + the items as dark silhouettes (`.ev-sil`, the avatar wearing each one, no background), open = the same step rows. Spooky Season's bonus (👻): Play 8 different games → Glowing jack-o'-lantern (`hand:jacklantern`), Earn 30 ★ → Mummy wraps (`top:mummywraps`), Clear 15 levels → Vampire cape (`back:vampcape`), Play on 8 different days → Floating bats (`effect:floatbats`), Score 1,500 in any Endless mode → Candy Corn name plate (`plate:candycorn`), Earn 50 ★ → Lil' Reaper (`pet:reaper`). Step labels write big numbers with commas (1,500). Events: Spooky Season 10-01→11-01, Winter Fest 12-01→01-07, Friendship Week 02-07→02-14, Music In Our Schools Month 03-01→03-31, Spring Bloom 04-01→04-30, Summer Send-Off 05-01→05-22. THE ITEMS are parts in avatar-parts.js's SEASONAL ITEMS section with `unlock: {event: '<id>'}` (avatar.js: owned = unlocked; `requirement` = "Spooky Season: Play 3 different games" during it, "Returns next Spooky Season!" otherwise; never sold at the Token Booth; identity items are never event items) and in avatar-code.js TABLE. PROGRESS = THE DAILY ACTIVITY LOG in storage.js (`store.activity` `{'YYYY-MM-DD': {s stars, c levels cleared, g {game: 1}, e best Endless score, p plays, f {game: 1} rounds FINISHED (Today's Practice only, never counted by events: see [progress-and-saving.md](progress-and-saving.md))}}`, 400 days, in the Arcade Backup Code): `setLevel(game, inst, lvl, p, run)` (every game passes `run` = THIS play's stars: a level with ≥ 1 is cleared), `addEndless` (score), `noteActivity({game, play})` (LevelSelect.played, Dojo Duel / Neon Face-Off match start, an Arcade Quest battle). An event's progress = the log's days inside its dates, so only what happens during it counts and every year starts fresh. `Seasons.check()` earns finished steps (the running event + one that ended ≤ 14 days ago); skins.js calls it before every UNLOCKED! card (results screens, Select Player), so the card celebrates it (its line: "🎃 Spooky Season: yours forever"). The FREE GIFT is claimed in the lobby's EVENT PANEL (`gameData('seasons').claimed[occurrence key]`). THE LOBBY (season-lobby.js): the banner ("🎃 SPOOKY SEASON: 6 days left!", FREE GIFT! / "2 of 5"; a `countdown: true` event: "🎻 Concert in 5 days!" … "Concert tonight!", `Seasons.leftText`) above the lobby cards opens the EVENT PANEL (gift + CLAIM, the ladder with progress bars, every item previewed on the student's avatar, the SEASONAL LOOK switch); the event's jingle (sounds.js `event-<id>-jingle`, built-in fallback) plays on the banner tap and CLAIM only; a touch on each sign (`.lobby[data-deco]`, the event's `deco`) shows with the look. TESTING: `?season=<id>` previews an event today (banner, panel, look, items; progress = today's activity; claims/earned items/seen cards in sessionStorage only: nothing saved); `?demo&today=YYYY-MM-DD` pretends that date (saved normally); `?demo&unlockall` opens every item. `Arcade.Seasons` (`active/steps/progress/claim/check/requirement/owned/occurrences/look/backdrop/lookOn/setLookOn/leftText`) and `Arcade.SeasonLobby.state()` (+ `bonus`, `bonusOpen`, `count` = the banner's "10 of 11") for tests: tests/spooky-bonus.spec.js.
- SEASONAL LOOKS (`season-look.js`, the floor page only; styles "SEASONAL LOOKS" at the end of arcade.css; a few `--sl-*` tokens in theme.css): while a season runs, the arcade's MENUS get a full themed backdrop: the lobby, a zone, ALL GAMES, the FULL ARCADE (2D and 3D), Choose Your Instrument and PRESS START; NEVER a game page. WHICH look = `Seasons.look(date)`: the running EVENT's `look` (every event has one: spooky, winter, friendship, miosm, spring, summer; a concert: concert), else a BACKGROUND-ONLY SEASON's (`Arcade.SEASON_BACKDROPS` in seasons.js, Mat edits it: `{id, name, look, dates: [['MM-DD', 'MM-DD'], …]}`: Back to School 08-01→09-30 (school), Fall Harvest 11-02→11-30 (harvest), Frost 01-08→02-06 + 02-15→02-28 (frost)); an event always wins. CONCERT SEASON = the commented template at the end of `Arcade.SEASONS` (one-time 'YYYY-MM-DD' dates, `end` = the concert day, `look: 'concert'`, `countdown: true`, challenges without items). THE SWITCH "Seasonal look: On / Off" (`Seasons.lookOn()`/`setLookOn(on)`, `gameData('seasons').look`, ON by default, fires window `arcade:seasonlook`) is in the shared Settings panel (ui-kit.js, on any page with seasons.js) and the event panel; it REPLACED the DECORATIONS switch: an old `deco: false` reads as Off, and setting it deletes `deco`. Off = the normal arcade everywhere (the banner, panel and items still work). `?season=<id>` previews an event, a background-only season or a look by name (`?season=concert`), nothing saved, even with the switch off. THE DRAWING: one `.slook` (`sl-sky` gradients, `sl-p` props = small inline SVGs at the edges, `sl-fx` a few drifting bits, `sl-dim` darkening the middle so every text keeps its contrast) behind the floor (before `.room`; `html[data-slook]` makes the body transparent and drops the room's haze; the 3D canvas is see-through) and a copy inside `#pressStart` and `#selectView`; strips along the top (string lights, bunting) are fixed-shape pieces in a row, never stretched. 3D: `SeasonLook.palette()` → the haze and side lights' tokens, `Floor3D.look()` applies it (again on a change). MOTION: transforms/opacity only, slow (≥ 4 s cycles; lights breathe a strand at a time, never blink); still with reduced motion or MOTION off (`html.slook-still`). No `filter`, no pictures, no SVG ids (each scene is drawn three times). A new look = a `LOOKS` entry in season-look.js + its colors in arcade.css + its id in `Seasons.LOOK_IDS`. `Arcade.SeasonLook.state()` for tests: tests/seasonal-looks.spec.js.
