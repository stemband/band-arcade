# Pitch detection and the microphone

- `Arcade.Pitch.setInstrument(inst)`, `onFrame((reading, level, now) => …)` (~25×/s; `reading` is `null` or `{freq, midi, note, pc, cents}` with a concert `pc` and `note` = rounded SOUNDING midi), `onHeld((pc, now, note) => …)` (once per held note, `holdMs` = 280), `ignoreCurrent()`, `heldPc()`, `setSensitivity(0–100)`, `bars(level)`, `levelPct(level)`.
- ATTACKS (opt-in; only pages that subscribe run it): `Arcade.Pitch.onAttack(a => …)`, `a = {time, pc|null, midi|null, level, rise}` (`rise` = the envelope over the quietest point of the last `dipMs`, null for a jump or a ?demo key: information only) once per new articulation (tongued note, mallet strike, drum hit). A separate small analyser is read every 5–8 ms; its window follows the instrument (`ENV.periods` cycles of the lowest note, 256–2048 samples, resized by setInstrument/setRange). An attack = a rise of the loudness envelope after a dip (`ENV.rise` × the quietest point of the last `dipMs`), or a quick jump (`ENV.jump` × the level `jumpMs` ago) that also climbs `ENV.above` × the loudest point 40–120 ms ago (a bell restruck while ringing); gated by `gateK` × the sensitivity gate; `refractoryMs` 90; never during `suppress`. Its pitch = two agreeing detector readings 50–170 ms after (else the most common, else null). Slurs and vibrato must never fire: tune `Arcade.Pitch.ENV` only against the Note Checker's ARTICULATION test (and ask Mat). `?demo` when a page sets `Pitch.demoAttacks = true`: tap Space = an attack on `Pitch.demoTarget()` ({pc, midi} or null), tap W = a wrong one, hold S = a steady note with no attacks (`Pitch.demoHeld()`).
- `Arcade.Pitch.stop()`: the microphone off for good (tracks stopped, its AudioContext closed, `active` false): the pause menu's BACK TO ARCADE GAMES calls it before the page changes; `start()` again needs a tap.
- `Arcade.Pitch.pauseListening(on)`: a page that listens only at certain moments (Arcade Quest) pauses analysis; while paused `listening()` is false, no readings/holds/attacks, and resuming needs a fresh attack. STALE SOUND (pitch.js `H.stale`): a sound already ringing when the page resets the detector never counts as a new note: after `setRange(...)`, or `pauseListening(false)` with a sound really in the mic at that moment (one analysis on the spot), ANY sound continuing without a gap (110 ms of nothing heard) is ignored (a new range can misread the old sound a half step off), after `ignoreCurrent()` that same pitch (a slur to another pitch still counts). Before this, a reset forgot the ringing note and the next reading fired `onHeld` as brand new: Neon Face-Off showed "That's <the other player's note>" (spelled for the wrong player) whenever no hit sound muted the mic (tests/faceoff-notes.spec.js).
- `Arcade.Pitch.suppress(ms)`: hear nothing new for ms (sfx.js calls it for every sound while listening); what is still sounding afterwards stays counted, so only a new attack fires. `Pitch.softSuppress = true` (a page option, Note Storm only): nothing counts during the window, but a note started inside it counts right after it. `Sfx.muteMax` (ms, a page option, default null) caps how much of a sound mutes the mic (+ the 250 ms echo). `Pitch.suppressedUntil`, `Pitch.isSuppressed(t)` (games pause their clocks while true), `Pitch.listening()` (mic running, or `?demo` standing in). `Pitch.instrument()` / `Pitch.range()` report what the detector is listening for (tests).
- `Arcade.Pitch.a4` (default 440): the reference A every reading is measured against. Only Tune Up's Tuner changes it (Settings → Tuner → Reference A, 438–445, `gameData('tuneup').a4`) while its tab is open, and puts 440 back when it closes; no game ever sets it.
- `Arcade.Pitch.setRange(lowMidi, highMidi)` (Note Checker scale/chromatic modes, and games with a scale or Chromatic NOTES pool via `ModePicker.useRange`; First 5 never calls it): search a member's SOUNDING range, octave-exact holds (D4 then D5 fires twice), and a reading outside the range whose octave above/below is inside is reported in that octave (`reading.folded`), because built-in mics often hear low brass an octave off. For low ranges it averages samples first (÷2–4) so tuba E1 (41 Hz) costs less than a game's tuba range. `setRange(null)` restores the default. `?demo`: `Pitch.demoNote = <sounding midi>` "plays" a note (at loudness `Pitch.demoLevel`, default 0.1), wobbling by `Pitch.demoJitter` semitones (default 0.2 = ±10 cents; Sustain Speedway sets 0.01 for "perfectly in tune").

- `Arcade.requireMic(fn)` shows the "Turn on the microphone" reminder ONCE PER PLAY SESSION, then runs `fn`: in later games the microphone starts straight from the tap (the browser's permission request still happens when needed, and the screen always shows with its fix-it steps when the mic is blocked or missing); `requireMic.showing()` for tests. The reminder opens on top of whatever is showing (`UI.layer`: the Settings panel's ROOM CHECK asks for the mic too). Before it, THE ROOM CHECK's once-a-day offer may show (CLASSROOM MODE below); `fn` runs after the card either way.
- WHY THE BROWSER'S NOISE SUPPRESSION STAYS OFF: `getUserMedia` asks for `noiseSuppression: false`, `echoCancellation: false`, `autoGainControl: false`, also for Classroom mode. Those are voice-call filters: they treat a sustained instrument tone as background noise and fade it out within a second or two, and the gain control pumps the level the gates rely on. Classroom mode does the job for music instead.

## Detector tuning (shared/pitch.js)

YIN with threshold 0.15, clarity ≥ 0.8, loudness gate from the sensitivity slider, ±0.65-semitone hysteresis, 110 ms release gap. Per-instrument search range comes from `range` in `instruments.js` (min × 0.78, max × 2.3); the dip search starts past the edge of a dip whose bottom lies above the search range. Change these carefully and note why in the commit message.
- THE TWELFTH / OCTAVE CHECK (`lowerCandidate`, numbers in `Pitch.LOW`): YIN takes the FIRST low dip, so a strong overtone can win (a clarinet's chalumeau notes: strong 3rd harmonic, weak 2nd, so concert B♭3 read as F5 = written C read as G). After the dip at τ it tries 1.5τ, 2τ, 3τ (a fifth, an octave, a twelfth below; the nearest that passes wins) and moves down only if the lower pitch is inside the search range, YIN's difference there is low too, there is real energy AT the lower pitch (Goertzel: ≥ `LOW.ratio` × the pitch found and ≥ `LOW.floor` × the median of six points beside it) and at least `LOW.supportCount` more of its harmonics the higher note doesn't have (SUPPORT: a clarinet's 5th and 7th). Within `LOW.hum` Hz of 50/60 Hz mains hum the lower fundamental must be strong (`humRatio`). Tested with synthetic tones (odd-harmonic clarinet with an 8–20 % fundamental, flute, oboe, saxes incl. a loud 2nd harmonic, bassoon, trumpet, horn, trombone, tuba; noise and hum): see the PR table. Retune only against that kind of table and Mat's real instruments.
- STABILITY: while a note is held, a different pitch replaces it only after `Pitch.confirmFrames` (2) readings in a row; that frame reports null and the held note stays held (a one-frame jump to an overtone can't count, or restart a hold). From silence a note is taken at once.
- `?pitchdebug` on any page with pitch.js (also: the room floor, this device's room check and ratio, Classroom mode on/off and why, and the reason the last reading was rejected: "below room gate", "clarity …", "hold 300 < 340", "attack below room gate"): the PITCH DEBUG overlay (bottom left, ~6×/s): frequency, the note concert and written (the saved member's `sounds`), cents, clarity, level vs gate, the held note, and each candidate (f, f/1.5, f/2, f/3) with its YIN difference, magnitude, the level beside it, harmonics found and why it was or wasn't chosen. `Pitch._analyse(buf, sr)` (tests) runs one buffer through the same analysis (no hysteresis).

## CLASSROOM MODE + THE ROOM CHECK (a loud band room)

In Mr. Graham's band room up to 30 instruments play at once and every device's mic hears all of them: another student's note could count for this one (false credit), and the crowd can bury a quiet player. Classroom mode listens for the instrument CLOSEST to the device: this student is much louder at this mic than the rest of the room. Games don't need to know about it. Every number lives in ONE commented block, `Arcade.Pitch.ROOM` (shared/pitch.js); tune it only against the crowd test's table and Mat's real-room trial. With Classroom mode OFF every rule of the detector is exactly as before (tests/pitch.spec.js and the rest pass unchanged; the room floor is measured but changes nothing).
- THE ROOM FLOOR (always measured while the mic listens, cheap): the `floorPct` (20th) percentile of the frame RMS over the last `floorWindowS` (6 s), leaving out this student's own playing (a held note or an attack in the last 300 ms, when it also stands `floorMine` (1.5) × above the floor: otherwise a student tonguing for 10 s pulled the floor up to their own level and went deaf), followed slowly (`floorRise` 3 s up, `floorFall` 1 s down) so one loud chord doesn't spike it. The first floor is taken after ~1 s of frames (until then: the room check's room level). It FREEZES while listening is paused or a sound mutes the mic (`suppress`), so a game's own sounds never raise it. `Pitch.state()` = `{floor, classroom, why, setting, by, auto, loud, gate, own, ratio, checkAt, reject}`.
- THE RULES while Classroom mode is on: a reading counts only if its RMS ≥ the largest of the sensitivity gate, `overFloor` (2.5) × the floor and `ownShare` (0.35) × this device's ROOM CHECK level (when there is a check for this instrument); a held note needs `holdMs` 340 (280 otherwise) and a change of note `confirmFrames` 3 (2); an ATTACK (tonguing, mallets: `onAttack`) must reach `attackOverFloor` (3) × the floor and `ownShare` × the check, and a pitched instrument's attack counts only if its pitch settles from readings that passed the room rules (another student's attack across the room has none); the SNARE (no pitch to check) must reach `hitShare` (0.7) × the check's hits. shared/onsets.js (claps, the snare in Rhythm Dojo / Showtime / Blocktave) keeps its own floor of the emphasized signal and applies `attackOverFloor` and `hitShare` × the check's `ownOnset` the same way. `clarity` stays 0.8: the planned .86 lost about half the student's own notes in the crowd test (the crowd lowers the student's clarity as much as its own) while the loudness gate alone already rejected every crowd note.
- TURNING IT ON (`Pitch.classroomSetting()`, `Pitch.classroom()`): teacher-settings.js `CLASSROOM_MODE` `'on'` / `'off'` decides for every device; `'auto'` (the default) lets each device choose in Settings ("Classroom mode: Auto / On / Off", `gameData('mic').classroom`, `Pitch.setClassroom(mode)`). AUTO = on once the floor has stayed above `autoOn` (0.008 RMS) for `autoOnS` 3 s, off once it has stayed below `autoOff` (0.004) for `autoOffS` 10 s (the gap between them: no flipping at the edge). A small green "Classroom mode" chip shows in the Settings mic block and Tune Up's mic block while it's on.
- THE ROOM CHECK (`shared/room-check.js`, `Arcade.RoomCheck`; loaded after mic-gate.js by every page that listens): about 8 seconds, from the Settings mic block ("Room check"), Tune Up's mic block, or the offer below. A panel with a progress ring and a level meter: (1) "Stay quiet for 3 seconds" (the room can keep playing): the room's level (median); (2) "Play your FIRST note and hold it for 3 seconds" (the member's first-five tonic on a staff in their clef; the ring fills only while a note is heard) or, for the snare, "Hit your drum 4 times, normal loudness" (the median hit peak; `ownOnset` = the onset detector's level of the same hits); (3) "Great! Your instrument is 6× louder than the room. Classroom mode is ready." or, below `minRatio` 2, "The room is almost as loud as you. Move the device closer…" + TRY AGAIN. It measures with `Pitch.probe()` (the normal rules, nothing reaches the game: it works from a paused game's Settings); nothing is recorded, only `gameData('mic').room = {floor, own, ratio, at, member, ownOnset?}` is kept, per device + instrument (another instrument on a shared device asks again). Older than `staleDays` (7): stale (still used; the offer comes back). `gameData('mic')` is NEVER in the Arcade Backup Code (storage.js `exportAll` leaves it out, `importAll` keeps the device's own): it's about this room and this device.
- THE OFFER: once a day at most, when a listening game starts (`requireMic`) after the room was loud (Auto's rule, `loudMin` 10 minutes, remembered across pages as `gameData('mic').loudAt`) and this instrument has no check or a stale one: a small card "Loud room! A quick room check helps the game hear YOU" with CHECK NOW / Not now; the game starts after either. Never when Classroom mode is set Off (teacher or device), and never from `requireMic(fn, {offer: false})` (calls in the middle of play: Arcade Quest's battles, Blocktave's mode switch).
- `?teacher` (any game's Settings): this device's room check (ratio, date, instrument) and the current mode and why; the lobby's leaderboard `?teacher` panel lists the saved check and setting.
- TESTS: `tests/pitch-classroom.spec.js` with the synthetic room in `tests/crowd.js` (THE CROWD TEST; the numbers are in the PR "Classroom mode + room check"), Auto, the teacher setting, suppression, the room check and the offer in `?demo` (`Pitch.demoRoom` = the room's level; `demoNote`/`demoLevel` = the student). `Pitch._feed(buf, sr, now)` / `_envFeed(e, now)` / `_room({floor, auto, loudAt})` are for tests only.
- KNOWN LIMIT (from the crowd test): one LOUD stand partner (a single neighbor at ~0.4 of this student's level, nobody else) still gets through `ownShare` 0.35; 0.6 stopped it in the simulation, at the cost of soft playing (a student playing much softer than in the room check). Decide with Mat's trial.

The PR's crowd table says what to expect; then run the real-room trial in the PR and paste the results back to tune `ROOM`.
