TOAST `UI.toast(text, {near, kind, ms, top, icon, action})` (always above every panel; `top` = px from the top of the screen instead of the bottom; `icon` = a small picture before the text (trusted HTML, e.g. a portrait); `action: {label, onClick, aria}` = one small button after the text, the only part that takes taps, and tapping it removes the toast; with either the toast is `.rich`: the floor's PLAYING AS toast, [page-flow.md](page-flow.md)). PANELS ON TOP (`UI.layer`, ui-kit.js): a panel opened from another panel must never open behind it: `UI.layer.open(el, {min, trap, onEsc, focus})` gives `el` an inline z-index = max(its own, `min`, the highest visible fixed/absolute child of body + 1), remembers the focus, and with `trap` adds the kit's Tab trap + Esc (only the TOP layer reacts) + `body.ui-modal`; `UI.layer.close(el)` clears it and puts the focus back on the opener. Every kit panel (confirm, notice, intro, pause, settings) goes through it, so a confirmation always lands above what asked it; so do Backup / Restore (min 80: above Settings 70 and the leaderboard 70, below confirmations 90 and toasts 95), Create Your Player, the Locker, Sustain Speedway's garage, the lobby's event panel and the app's panels. Never change a panel's look for it; `UI.layer.state()` for tests (tests/backup-layer.spec.js). Every kit panel: role dialog + label, focus in and back out, Tab trapped, Esc, ↑/↓/←/→ between a menu's buttons, keys never reach the game underneath, `body.ui-modal` while one is open (Arcade Quest's keys stop). `UI.state()` for tests.

## Hold guard

- HOLD GUARD (`Arcade.holdGuard(el, {lock, touch})`, ui.js; `.hold-guard` / `.hold-lock` in theme.css): every on-screen control that is HELD or tapped fast (Arcade Quest's pad and game area, Button Masher's fingering diagram, Note Ninja's answer pad; Chime Heist's kit, Dojo Duel and AnswerPad already had the same CSS) never selects text, shows the iPad callout/magnifier, flashes a tap highlight, or (`lock`) scrolls/zooms: no selection/callout/highlight CSS, contextmenu/selectstart/dragstart blocked, `lock` = `touch-action: none`, `touch` = a non-passive touchstart preventDefault (only where every control uses pointer events). Put no selectable text in such buttons (SVG icons, CSS content, aria-labels). Use it for any new held control.

## Overlays

- EVERY WINDOW FITS A PHONE: every panel and window must fit a 360 px-wide phone: never give art, a row or a panel a fixed width that can't shrink. theme.css does the shared part: `.overlay{grid-template-columns:minmax(0,1fr)}` and `.overlay>*{min-width:0;max-width:100%}` (an auto grid column grows to its widest word or row and the panel's `width:min(…,100%)` follows it off the screen), `.ls-card{min-width:0}` (a level card's long word never widens its grid column). In a game's own CSS: `width:min(<size>px,100%)` for a panel; `minmax(0,1fr)` (not `1fr`) in grid columns and `min-width:0` on flex/grid children that hold text; `flex-wrap:wrap` on rows of buttons or chips; art sized from its row (`width:min(77px,calc((100% - gaps)/5)); aspect-ratio: …`, as Showtime's story band) instead of a fixed height; `overflow-wrap:anywhere` for long words; a table or strip that is wider on purpose goes in its own `overflow-x:auto` box, never the window. A dropdown hung from a button near the edge (the avatar badge's menu) spans the screen on phones (`position:fixed; left:16px; right:16px`). Buttons you resize stay ≥ 44 px tall. tests/phone-width.spec.js checks every page and window at 390, 360 and 375 px wide ([testing.md](testing.md) PHONE WIDTH).
- OVERLAYS SCROLL THEMSELVES: theme.css `.overlay` is the scroll container (`overflow-y: auto`, `overscroll-behavior: contain`, `touch-action: pan-y`, iOS momentum); its panel is centered by auto margins and starts at the top when taller than the screen. Never give an overlay's panel `max-height` + `overflow: hidden`, and never let it be cut off: let the overlay scroll. For a long overlay that must keep the page behind still, call `Arcade.lockScroll(true)` on open and `lockScroll(false)` on close (ui.js). Arcade Quest's `.q-panel` scrolls inside the game screen (`touch-action: pan-y` because `#stage` takes over touch).
