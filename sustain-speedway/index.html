Sustain Speedway

Your instrument is the engine. Your car only moves while you hold the lap's note, and it goes faster the more in tune and steady you are. Breathe whenever you need to (the car coasts), and rest in the pit stop between laps.

Difficulty

Demo: every track is unlocked. In a race, hold Space = the right note, perfectly in tune; hold D = the right note drifting sharp (+25 cents, drifting to +45), F = drifting flat; hold W = a wrong note; hold E = the right note, centered but wobbling (no nitro); let go = breathing (the car coasts).
