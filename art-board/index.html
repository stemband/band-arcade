Art Board

Every picture slot in the Band Arcade and what shows there today. A game's menu screens show its coded scene (animated, drawn in shared/bg-scenes.js) unless you upload a background picture (shared/backgrounds/, see its README), which then overrides it. The small box in each preview is where the menu sits, with the game's dark overlay on top of the background. Marquee pictures go in shared/marquees/, instrument portraits in shared/portraits/.
