Showtime Malfunction

Years ago, the Band Arcade had its own animatronic house band, THE SHOWTIME BAND, playing on a little stage between the cabinets. One day they were switched off and wheeled into the back room.

Tonight, after closing, they've powered back on. They're glitching, their voice boxes are sour and off-key, and they're lurching across the arcade floor toward you.

Only clean, in-tune notes can reboot them. Play each note as many times as the voice box shows, starting every note fresh, and they'll shuffle back to the stage where they belong.
