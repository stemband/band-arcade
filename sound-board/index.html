Sound Board

Every sound in the Band Arcade, from shared/sounds.js. Upload a recording into shared/sounds/ with the file name shown (.m4a or .mp3), then press RELOAD ALL SOUNDS. This page always asks the server for the live files (it skips the browser's saved copies), so a file you just uploaded, replaced or deleted shows up right away; GitHub Pages can take a minute to publish an upload. Game pages keep their fast saved copies: after replacing sounds, add 1 to SOUNDS_VERSION at the top of shared/sounds.js so students get the new files. YOUR FILE = your recording plays. FALLBACK = the arcade's own built-in sound for that moment. MISSING FILE, USING GENERATED = a short generic beep until you add one. This page isn't linked from the arcade. The PLAY buttons play even when SOUND is off; the effects volume still applies.

Music Highway's songs: the Song Board shows every song on a staff (concert pitch and any instrument) and plays it with its drums.

Rhythm Dojo's counting: the Counting Board shows every case of Mr. Graham's counting (quarters to 6/8, rests, ties, triplets) drawn on the one-line staff, each checked against the counting rule.

Music

Every game's menu music (games.js menuMusic) and the arcade's own loops. Record each as a seamless loop, 30–90 s, exported as .m4a, named exactly as shown. Until a game's file is uploaded, its menus play the arcade's select music.

Arcade Quest — Episode 1 Rooms

Each explorable room of Ghost Notes Manor has its own music slot (which room plays which: arcade-quest/data/music.js; several rooms may share one). Until a room's file is uploaded, it plays what it always has (the fallback shown). Seamless loops, 30–90 s, .m4a.
